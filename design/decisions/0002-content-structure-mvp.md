# 0002 Content structure and first release

Status: Accepted (2026-10-03)

## Context
Existing resources are organised by administrative logic; nobody offers neutral, plain-German content by life event with all 26 cantons comparable. Life-event pages depend on the line-by-line filing content.

## Decision
Site areas: Lebenslagen · Steuererklärung Schritt für Schritt · So funktioniert das System · Kantone (generated from data) · Reformen · Nachschlagen (Glossar, Beträge & Tarife, Fristen, Quellen) · Werkzeuge (later).

**First release:** System basics, filing step by step for tax year 2025, 26 canton pages, glossary, reforms (Eigenmietwert 2029, Individualbesteuerung 2032). Target: live before the 2027 filing season (January). Life events follow.

Every page shows «Stand: Steuerjahr … · geprüft am …», its sources, and the notice that it is general information, not tax advice.

## Consequences
- Canton data quality gates the release: open points from the research report («Offene Punkte vor der Publikation») must be resolved or shown as unverified.
- No paid individual advice and no use of «Treuhand»/«fiduciario» wording (Ticino licensing).
