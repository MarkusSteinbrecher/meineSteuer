## 1. Constitutional basis and the three levels (Bund, Kantone, Gemeinden)

### Takeaway
Switzerland has three taxing levels. The cantons hold general taxing power (Art. 3 BV); the Confederation may only levy taxes the Constitution expressly grants it, and its income tax and VAT are time-limited (currently until end of 2035). Municipalities tax only as far as cantonal law allows, usually as a multiplier (Steuerfuss) on the cantonal "simple tax".

### Cited Findings
- **Art. 3 BV**: cantons are sovereign insofar as the Constitution does not limit them; they exercise all rights not transferred to the Confederation. In tax terms: cantons have *originäre Steuerhoheit*; the Confederation only has the taxes listed in the BV. — [Statute] [BV](https://www.fedlex.admin.ch/eli/cc/1999/404/de)
- **Art. 127 BV (Grundsätze der Besteuerung)**: tax subjects, object and assessment must be set by law (Legalitätsprinzip); principles of *Allgemeinheit*, *Gleichmässigkeit* and *Besteuerung nach der wirtschaftlichen Leistungsfähigkeit*; Abs. 3 forbids intercantonal double taxation (interkantonales Doppelbesteuerungsverbot). — [Statute] [BV](https://www.fedlex.admin.ch/eli/cc/1999/404/de)
- **Art. 128 BV (direkte Steuern)**: the federal income tax on natural persons is capped at **11.5 %** of income (8.5 % profit tax for legal persons); at least 17 % of the revenue goes to the cantons (DBG Art. 196 currently fixes the cantonal share at **21.2 %** since 2020, STAF reform). Cantons assess and collect the direct federal tax. — [Statute] [BV](https://www.fedlex.admin.ch/eli/cc/1999/404/de), [DBG](https://www.fedlex.admin.ch/eli/cc/1991/1184_1184_1184/de); the 21.2 % share is also mentioned in the ESTV page on Individualbesteuerung (loss split 78.8 % Bund / 21.2 % cantons) — [ESTV](https://www.estv.admin.ch/de/individualbesteuerung)
- **Art. 129 BV (Steuerharmonisierung)**: the Confederation sets principles for harmonising cantonal/municipal direct taxes: *Steuerpflicht, Gegenstand und zeitliche Bemessung, Verfahrensrecht, Steuerstrafrecht*. **Tariffs, tax rates and tax-free amounts (Steuertarife, Steuersätze, Steuerfreibeträge) are expressly excluded** and remain cantonal. — [Statute] [BV](https://www.fedlex.admin.ch/eli/cc/1999/404/de)
- **Art. 130 BV** VAT (Mehrwertsteuer); **Art. 131** special consumption taxes (tobacco, spirits, beer, cars, mineral oil); **Art. 132** stamp duties and **Verrechnungssteuer** (withholding tax); **Art. 133** customs; **Art. 134** what the Confederation subjects to VAT/special consumption/stamp/withholding tax may not be taxed by cantons/municipalities in the same way; **Art. 135** Finanz- und Lastenausgleich (fiscal equalisation). — [Statute] [BV](https://www.fedlex.admin.ch/eli/cc/1999/404/de)
- **Art. 196 Ziff. 13/14 BV (Übergangsbestimmungen)**: the right to levy the direct federal tax and VAT is time-limited; after the "Neue Finanzordnung 2021" (accepted by People and cantons on 4 June 2018) it runs **until end of 2035**. — [Statute] [BV](https://www.fedlex.admin.ch/eli/cc/1999/404/de) (date of the 2018 vote: from researcher knowledge, verify)
- ESTV/SSK publish the brochure **"Das schweizerische Steuersystem"** (21st edition, 2025), the standard plain-language overview of federal, cantonal and municipal taxes and Steuerhoheit; good primary reference for the website. — [law.ch news on the 2025 edition](https://law.ch/lawnews/2025/07/neue-broschuere-das-schweizerische-steuersystem/); [ESTV Steuersystem Schweiz](https://www.estv.admin.ch/estv/de/home/die-estv/steuersystem-schweiz.html); PDF: [kmu.admin.ch](https://www.kmu.admin.ch/dam/kmu/de/dokumente/Publications/CH-Steuersystem.pdf.download.pdf/CH-Steuersystem.pdf) (returned HTTP 502 in this session)
- ESTV also publishes "Steuermäppchen" dossiers on individual taxes, e.g. Personalsteuer and Erbschafts-/Schenkungssteuer. — [Personalsteuer PDF](https://www.estv.admin.ch/dam/estv/de/dokumente/estv/steuersystem/steuermaeppchen/personalsteuer-de-fr.pdf.download.pdf/personalsteuer-de-fr.pdf), [Erbschafts-/Schenkungssteuer PDF](https://www.estv.admin.ch/dam/estv/de/dokumente/estv/steuersystem/steuermaeppchen/erbschafts-schenkungssteuer-de-fr.pdf.download.pdf/erbschafts-schenkungssteuer-de-fr.pdf) (not opened in this session)

**Overview table (who levies what for private persons)** — [Statute] BV Arts. 128–134, StHG Art. 2; inheritance tax being cantonal is per the ESTV dossier above:

| Level | Taxes relevant to private persons |
|---|---|
| **Bund** | Direkte Bundessteuer (income only, **no federal wealth tax**); Verrechnungssteuer (35 % on interest/dividends, lottery wins; refundable to honest residents); MWST/VAT; Stempelabgaben; special consumption taxes; customs; Wehrpflichtersatzabgabe |
| **Kantone** | Einkommens- und Vermögenssteuer (mandatory per StHG Art. 2); Quellensteuer; Grundstückgewinnsteuer (mandatory); Erbschafts-/Schenkungssteuer (almost all cantons; not SZ, OW, partly LU – verify); Personal-/Kopfsteuer (only some cantons); Motorfahrzeugsteuer; Handänderungssteuer (some cantons); Kirchensteuer (cantonal law, levied by recognised churches) |
| **Gemeinden** | Mostly surcharges (Steuerfuss/multiplier) on cantonal income/wealth tax; sometimes Personalsteuer, Liegenschaftssteuer, Hundesteuer, Grundstückgewinnsteuer (in some cantons communal, e.g. ZH) |

### Inferences
- For a lay audience: "There is no single Swiss income tax: you usually pay federal, cantonal and municipal income tax (and often church tax) on one return, but at three different rates. Only the cantons and municipalities tax wealth."
- Tax burden varies widely by canton and municipality because Art. 129 BV leaves rates to the cantons.

## 2. Key federal laws and how cantonal law relates to the StHG

### Takeaway
The DBG governs the federal income tax directly and in full (including tariffs). The StHG is a framework law addressed to the cantons: it harmonises *formal* matters (who is taxed, what is taxed, time periods, procedure, criminal law) but **not** rates, tariffs or most deduction amounts ("vertikale und horizontale formelle Harmonisierung", no "materielle/tarifliche" harmonisation). Each canton has its own tax law (Steuergesetz) that must comply with the StHG.

### Cited Findings
- **DBG (SR 642.11)**: federal income tax on natural persons (and profit tax on legal persons), including tariffs (Art. 36), deductions (Arts. 26–35), procedure (Arts. 102 ff.), and criminal provisions (Arts. 174 ff.). Assessed and collected by the cantonal tax administrations under ESTV supervision (Art. 2, 102–104 DBG). — [Statute] [DBG](https://www.fedlex.admin.ch/eli/cc/1991/1184_1184_1184/de)
- **StHG (SR 642.14)**: Art. 1 Abs. 3: "Die Bestimmung der Steuertarife, Steuersätze und Steuerfreibeträge bleibt Sache der Kantone" (cantons keep tariffs, rates and tax-free amounts). Art. 2: cantons must levy income and wealth tax on natural persons, profit and capital tax on legal persons, source tax on certain persons, and real-estate gains tax. Art. 72 ff.: adaptation deadlines; after a deadline expires, the federal law applies directly if cantonal law contradicts it (Art. 72 Abs. 2 type provisions). — [Statute] [StHG](https://www.fedlex.admin.ch/eli/cc/1991/1256_1256_1256/de)
- The StHG practice of giving cantons time to adapt: "Das Steuerharmonisierungsgesetz verlangt, dass der Bund dabei Rücksicht auf die Kantone nimmt und ihnen in der Regel eine Frist von mindestens zwei Jahren für die Anpassung ihrer Gesetzgebung lässt" (cited as reason for Eigenmietwert reform timing). — [Search summary of admin.ch press release 1 Apr 2026](https://www.admin.ch/de/newnsb/yGTqBPowRqyVh0zPokW-q)
- **VStG (SR 642.21)**: 35 % Verrechnungssteuer on income from movable capital (interest on bank deposits above threshold, bond interest, dividends), Swiss lottery/gambling wins above the exemption amount, and certain insurance benefits (lower rates). For residents it is a **security tax (Sicherungssteuer)**: refunded/credited if the income and underlying asset are declared (Art. 21–23 VStG). Art. 23 Abs. 2 (in force since 2019): no forfeiture of the refund if the income was omitted by negligence and is declared before the assessment becomes final. — [Statute] [VStG](https://www.fedlex.admin.ch/eli/cc/1966/371_385_384/de)
- **Quellensteuer**: DBG Arts. 83–101 and StHG Arts. 32–38a, detailed in the **Quellensteuerverordnung (QStV, SR 642.118.2)**; foreign employees without C permit are taxed at source; since the reform in force 1 Jan 2021, residents taxed at source with gross earnings ≥ CHF 120,000 must file a regular return (nachträgliche ordentliche Veranlagung, obligatory), others can request one by **31 March** of the following year; non-residents can request it if "quasi-resident" (≥ 90 % of worldwide income taxed in CH). — [Statute] [DBG](https://www.fedlex.admin.ch/eli/cc/1991/1184_1184_1184/de) (amounts/dates from researcher knowledge; verify in QStV/ESTV Kreisschreiben 45)
- The EFD's annual cold-progression ordinance also amends the source-tax regulations (Quellensteuerverordnung) each year. — [admin.ch, 22 Sep 2026](https://www.admin.ch/de/newnsb/rq9rumbCaofX)

### Inferences
- For the website: "Federal law decides *how* and *what*; your canton decides *how much*."
- Because the StHG harmonises procedure, the procedural rules below (Einsprache 30 days, Nachsteuer 10 years, Selbstanzeige) are essentially identical federally and in all cantons; the article numbers differ (DBG vs. StHG vs. cantonal law).

## 3. Unlimited vs. limited tax liability, start/end, moves between cantons and abroad

### Takeaway
Persons with tax domicile (Wohnsitz) or qualified stay (Aufenthalt) in Switzerland are taxed on worldwide income and wealth (except foreign real estate and foreign permanent establishments, which are exempt but count for the rate, "Progressionsvorbehalt"). Others are taxed only on Swiss-source items (economic ties). Moving between cantons: the canton of residence on **31 December** (end of the tax period) taxes the whole year. Moving to/from abroad: liability starts/ends on the day of arrival/departure and the income is converted to an annual rate.

### Cited Findings
- **Art. 3 DBG / Art. 3 StHG (persönliche Zugehörigkeit, unbeschränkte Steuerpflicht)**: tax domicile = staying with the intention to remain permanently (*Absicht dauernden Verbleibens*) or a special legal domicile; tax residence (Aufenthalt) = stay of **≥ 30 days with gainful activity** or **≥ 90 days without** (short interruptions ignored). Married couples: income/wealth of both spouses added regardless of property regime. — [Statute] [DBG](https://www.fedlex.admin.ch/eli/cc/1991/1184_1184_1184/de), [StHG](https://www.fedlex.admin.ch/eli/cc/1991/1256_1256_1256/de)
- **Art. 4 DBG (wirtschaftliche Zugehörigkeit)**: non-residents liable if they own Swiss real estate, a business or a permanent establishment in CH. **Art. 5 DBG**: other Swiss-source items, e.g. gainful activity in CH, board fees, mortgage-secured interest, pensions from Swiss public employers or Swiss pension funds, real-estate brokerage. These are typically collected as Quellensteuer. — [Statute] [DBG](https://www.fedlex.admin.ch/eli/cc/1991/1184_1184_1184/de)
- **Art. 6 DBG (Umfang)**: unlimited liability covers worldwide income except foreign businesses, permanent establishments and real estate (exemption with progression). Limited liability covers only Swiss-source items, but the rate is that of total income (Art. 7). — [Statute] [DBG](https://www.fedlex.admin.ch/eli/cc/1991/1184_1184_1184/de)
- **Art. 8 DBG (Beginn und Ende)**: liability starts on the day domicile/residence is taken up or the Swiss asset acquired; ends on death, departure or loss of the Swiss asset. **Art. 40 Abs. 3 / Art. 209 (old) → today Arts. 40–41 DBG**: if liability lasts less than a year, income is taxed for that period, but periodic income is annualised to determine the rate (*Satzbestimmung*). — [Statute] [DBG](https://www.fedlex.admin.ch/eli/cc/1991/1184_1184_1184/de)
- **Art. 105 DBG / Art. 68 StHG (Wechsel des Wohnsitzes innerhalb der Schweiz)**: the canton where the person resides **at the end of the tax period (31 Dec)** taxes the full year (also for the direct federal tax; the cantonal shares are not split). Exceptions: real estate and permanent establishments in another canton remain taxed there (economic ties, with intercantonal allocation, "Steuerausscheidung"). Capital benefits (Kapitalleistungen aus Vorsorge) are taxed in the canton of residence at the time of payout. — [Statute] [DBG](https://www.fedlex.admin.ch/eli/cc/1991/1184_1184_1184/de), [StHG](https://www.fedlex.admin.ch/eli/cc/1991/1256_1256_1256/de)
- **Death**: heirs step into the deceased's tax obligations (Steuernachfolge, Art. 12 DBG) up to their inheritance share. — [Statute] [DBG](https://www.fedlex.admin.ch/eli/cc/1991/1184_1184_1184/de)
- **Aufwandbesteuerung (lump-sum/expenditure tax, Art. 14 DBG)**: available only to foreign nationals taking up residence for the first time (or after 10 years abroad) who do not work in CH; federal minimum basis CHF 400,000 (index-adjusted); abolished in several cantons (e.g. ZH, SH, AR, BL, BS). — [Statute] [DBG](https://www.fedlex.admin.ch/eli/cc/1991/1184_1184_1184/de) (amount and canton list from researcher knowledge; verify)

### Inferences
- Plain-language rule for the website: "Moved within Switzerland? Your new canton on 31 December taxes you for the whole year. Moved abroad or arrived from abroad? Switzerland taxes you only for the days you lived here."
- International cases are further shaped by double-taxation agreements (DBA); out of scope here except to mention them.

## 4. Tax period and assessment; family taxation

### Takeaway
Since 2003 all cantons and the Confederation use the **postnumerando annual assessment with current-year measurement (einjährige Gegenwartsbemessung)**: the tax period is the calendar year, income of that year is taxed, the return is filed in the following year. Wealth is measured at the **end of the tax period (Stichtag 31 Dec)** or end of liability. Married couples and registered partners are currently taxed jointly (**Faktorenaddition**) with relief via a married tariff/splitting; this will change to individual taxation on **1 January 2032**.

### Cited Findings
- **Art. 40 DBG**: tax period = calendar year. **Art. 41 DBG**: income is measured by the income earned in the tax period. **Art. 17 / 66 StHG**: wealth assessed at end of tax period or end of liability. — [Statute] [DBG](https://www.fedlex.admin.ch/eli/cc/1991/1184_1184_1184/de), [StHG](https://www.fedlex.admin.ch/eli/cc/1991/1256_1256_1256/de)
- **Art. 9 DBG / Art. 3 Abs. 3 StHG (Faktorenaddition)**: income of spouses living in a legally and factually undivided marriage is added together regardless of matrimonial property regime; same for registered partners (Art. 9 Abs. 1bis DBG). Children's income is added to the parent with parental authority, **except the child's earned income (Erwerbseinkommen)**, for which the child is taxed separately. Separation/divorce: separate taxation for the whole tax period in which it occurs; marriage: joint taxation for the whole year in which it occurs. — [Statute] [DBG](https://www.fedlex.admin.ch/eli/cc/1991/1184_1184_1184/de)
- **Federal tariffs (Art. 36 DBG)**: Grundtarif (single persons, Abs. 1), **Verheiratetentarif** (married/registered partners and single parents living with children, Abs. 2) = a "Doppeltarif" approach; **Elterntarif** (Abs. 2bis): tax reduced by a fixed amount per child (CHF 263 for 2025 per researcher knowledge; verify). Additional relief: **Zweiverdienerabzug** (Art. 33 Abs. 2) and **Verheiratetenabzug** (Art. 35 Abs. 1 lit. c). — [Statute] [DBG](https://www.fedlex.admin.ch/eli/cc/1991/1184_1184_1184/de)
- **Federal child deduction**: CHF 6,800 per child (current), to rise to **CHF 12,000** under the Individualbesteuerung. — [ESTV](https://www.estv.admin.ch/de/individualbesteuerung)
- **Cantonal systems (StHG Art. 11 Abs. 1)** require cantons to reduce the tax for married couples compared with single persons; cantons choose the method: **Doppeltarif** (separate tariff for married couples), **Vollsplitting** (combined income divided by 2 for the rate) or **Teilsplitting** (divisor e.g. 1.6–1.9). — [Statute] [StHG](https://www.fedlex.admin.ch/eli/cc/1991/1256_1256_1256/de) (assignment of cantons to methods not verified)
- **Individualbesteuerung**: accepted in the referendum of **8 March 2026** with **54.23 % yes** (turnout 55.6 %). Married couples will be taxed like unmarried couples (each spouse files separately; income/wealth attributed by civil-law ownership); federal child deduction rises to CHF 12,000; tariff lowered for low/middle incomes, slightly raised for high incomes; applies at all three levels; cantons keep tariff autonomy. Estimated revenue loss ~CHF 630 million (federal tax), 78.8 % borne by the Bund, 21.2 % by cantons. Parliament adopted the law in June 2025 (ESTV says 23 June 2025; other sources say final vote 20 June 2025 — verify on parlament.ch, Geschäft 24.026). — [ESTV](https://www.estv.admin.ch/de/individualbesteuerung); [SRF](https://www.srf.ch/news/schweiz/abstimmungen-8-3-2026/abstimmungen-vom-8-maerz-kuenftig-soll-jede-und-jeder-eine-eigene-steuererklaerung-ausfuellen); [swissinfo](https://www.swissinfo.ch/ger/schweizer-politik/eidgen%C3%B6ssische-abstimmung-vom-8-3-2026-individualbesteuerung/91004072)
- **Entry into force of Individualbesteuerung: 1 January 2032**, the latest date permitted by law, decided by the Federal Council on 19 August 2026 so cantons can adapt laws, tariffs, social deductions and IT. — [20 Minuten](https://www.20min.ch/story/heiratsstrafe-bundesrat-individualbesteuerung-tritt-erst-im-jahr-2032-in-kraft-103619469); [Tages-Anzeiger](https://www.tagesanzeiger.ch/individualbesteuerung-bundesrat-setzt-einfuehrung-auf-2032-610699923317); [watson](https://www.watson.ch/schweiz/bundesrat/167096762-bundesrat-setzt-individualbesteuerung-erst-2032-um) (date of BR decision from search summary; confirm on admin.ch)

### Inferences
- Until tax year 2031 inclusive, joint taxation of married couples/registered partners stays in force; the site should present Faktorenaddition as current law and individual taxation as "beschlossen, ab 2032".
- The "Heiratsstrafe" (marriage penalty) explanation is the natural lead-in for the reform.

## 5. Procedure: from tax return to court, corrections, limitation, evasion and penalties

### Takeaway
Switzerland uses a **mixed assessment procedure (gemischtes Veranlagungsverfahren)**: the taxpayer must file and cooperate; the authority checks and issues a formal decision (Veranlagungsverfügung). Appeals: Einsprache (30 days) to the assessing authority, then cantonal court(s), then the Federal Supreme Court. Final decisions can be reopened in favour of the taxpayer (Revision) or against them (Nachsteuer, up to 10 years). Tax evasion is an administrative offence with fines; tax fraud (forged documents) is a criminal offence. A first-time voluntary disclosure (straflose Selbstanzeige) avoids the fine.

### Cited Findings

**Filing and cooperation**
- **Art. 124 DBG**: the authority invites taxpayers by public notice or personal letter to file a return; it must be filled in truthfully and completely, signed and returned with attachments by the deadline; a taxpayer who receives no form must still request one. Extension of the deadline on request. Deadlines are set by the cantons (commonly 31 March of the following year, with extensions available, often online and partly fee-based; varies by canton). — [Statute] [DBG](https://www.fedlex.admin.ch/eli/cc/1991/1184_1184_1184/de) (typical deadline from researcher knowledge; verify per canton)
- **Art. 125 DBG** attachments (Lohnausweis, Wertschriftenverzeichnis, bookkeeping for self-employed); **Art. 126 DBG Mitwirkungspflichten** (must do everything to enable correct assessment, provide information, documents); **Arts. 127–129 DBG** third-party certificates (employers, pension funds, insurers etc.). Banks are not obliged to report to the tax authority; the taxpayer obtains bank statements. — [Statute] [DBG](https://www.fedlex.admin.ch/eli/cc/1991/1184_1184_1184/de)
- **Art. 130 DBG**: the authority checks the return; if the taxpayer fails to file or the facts cannot be established reliably, it assesses at its discretion (**Ermessensveranlagung**, Abs. 2); this can only be challenged for being "offensichtlich unrichtig" (Art. 132 Abs. 3). **Art. 131**: assessment notified in writing with appeal instructions; deviations from the return must be indicated. — [Statute] [DBG](https://www.fedlex.admin.ch/eli/cc/1991/1184_1184_1184/de)
- **Art. 174 DBG Verletzung von Verfahrenspflichten**: fine up to CHF 1,000, in serious cases or repeat up to CHF 10,000 (e.g. not filing after reminder). — [Statute] [DBG](https://www.fedlex.admin.ch/eli/cc/1991/1184_1184_1184/de)

**Appeals**
- **Art. 132–135 DBG Einsprache**: written objection within **30 days** of notification to the assessing authority; free of charge as a rule; the authority can also change the assessment to the taxpayer's detriment (reformatio in peius) after hearing. — [Statute] [DBG](https://www.fedlex.admin.ch/eli/cc/1991/1184_1184_1184/de)
- **Art. 140–145 DBG**: Beschwerde (also called Rekurs in some cantons) to an independent cantonal appeals body (Steuerrekursgericht/Steuerrekurskommission) within **30 days**; cantons may provide a further cantonal instance (Art. 145, e.g. Verwaltungsgericht). Costs may be charged. — [Statute] [DBG](https://www.fedlex.admin.ch/eli/cc/1991/1184_1184_1184/de)
- **Art. 146 DBG + Arts. 82 ff. BGG**: Beschwerde in öffentlich-rechtlichen Angelegenheiten to the **Bundesgericht** (Federal Supreme Court, Lausanne), 30 days (Art. 100 BGG). For cantonal taxes, StHG Art. 73 opens the same route for harmonised matters. — [Statute] [DBG](https://www.fedlex.admin.ch/eli/cc/1991/1184_1184_1184/de), [StHG](https://www.fedlex.admin.ch/eli/cc/1991/1256_1256_1256/de)

**Corrections of final decisions**
- **Revision (Arts. 147–149 DBG)**, in favour of the taxpayer: on discovery of significant new facts/evidence, if the authority overlooked significant facts, or a crime influenced the decision. Excluded if the taxpayer could have raised it in the ordinary procedure with due care. Request within **90 days** of discovery, at most **10 years** after notification. — [Statute] [DBG](https://www.fedlex.admin.ch/eli/cc/1991/1184_1184_1184/de)
- **Berichtigung (Art. 150 DBG)**: correction of calculation errors and clerical mistakes, within **5 years** after notification. — [Statute] [DBG](https://www.fedlex.admin.ch/eli/cc/1991/1184_1184_1184/de)
- **Nachsteuer (Arts. 151–153 DBG / Art. 53 StHG)**, against the taxpayer: if facts unknown to the authority show that an assessment was wrongly omitted or is incomplete. Not possible if the taxpayer declared fully and correctly and the authority merely valued it wrongly. Right to initiate expires **10 years** after the end of the tax period; right to assess expires **15 years** after the end of the period. Interest is added. — [Statute] [DBG](https://www.fedlex.admin.ch/eli/cc/1991/1184_1184_1184/de)

**Limitation (Verjährung)**
- **Veranlagungsverjährung (Art. 120 DBG)**: right to assess expires **5 years** after end of tax period; interrupted/restarted by official acts; absolute limit **15 years**. — [Statute] [DBG](https://www.fedlex.admin.ch/eli/cc/1991/1184_1184_1184/de)
- **Bezugsverjährung (Art. 121 DBG)**: tax claims expire **5 years** after the assessment became final; absolute limit **10 years** after the end of the year in which it became final. — [Statute] [DBG](https://www.fedlex.admin.ch/eli/cc/1991/1184_1184_1184/de)

**Evasion, fraud, voluntary disclosure**
- **Steuerhinterziehung (Art. 175 DBG / Art. 56 StHG)**: intentionally or negligently causing an under-assessment. Fine usually **1× the evaded tax**, reducible to **1/3** for minor fault, up to **3×** for serious fault. Attempt (Art. 176): 2/3 of the fine for completed evasion. Prosecuted by the tax administration (administrative criminal law). Limitation of prosecution: completed evasion 10 years, attempt 6 years after end of period (Art. 184). — [Statute] [DBG](https://www.fedlex.admin.ch/eli/cc/1991/1184_1184_1184/de)
- **Straflose Selbstanzeige (Art. 175 Abs. 3 DBG / Art. 56 Abs. 1bis StHG, since 1 Jan 2010)**: on the **first** voluntary disclosure, no fine if (a) the evasion was not yet known to any tax authority, (b) the taxpayer cooperates unconditionally in determining the back tax, and (c) seriously tries to pay it. Only Nachsteuer + Verzugszins for up to the last 10 years are due. Usable **once in a lifetime**; each further disclosure is fined at **1/5** of the evaded tax. — [Kanton Aargau Merkblatt](https://ag.ch/media/kanton-aargau/dfr/dokumente/steuern/natuerliche-personen/merkblaetter-zu-steuern-natuerliche-personen/straflose-selbstanzeige.pdf); [Kanton AI Merkblatt](https://www.ai.ch/themen/steuern/publikationen/merkblaetter-gueltig-ab-steuerperiode-2020/12-merkblatt-zur-straflosen-selbstanzeige-und-zur.pdf); [Statute] [DBG](https://www.fedlex.admin.ch/eli/cc/1991/1184_1184_1184/de)
- **Vereinfachte Nachbesteuerung von Erben (Art. 153a DBG / Art. 53a StHG)**: heirs who disclose the deceased's undeclared assets pay back tax plus interest only for the **last 3 completed tax periods** before the year of death, no fine (conditions: not known to authorities, full cooperation). — [Kanton AI Merkblatt](https://www.ai.ch/themen/steuern/publikationen/merkblaetter-gueltig-ab-steuerperiode-2020/12-merkblatt-zur-straflosen-selbstanzeige-und-zur.pdf)
- **Steuerbetrug (Art. 186 DBG / Art. 59 StHG)**: use of false, falsified or untrue documents (e.g. balance sheets, salary certificates) to deceive the tax authority; penalty **custodial sentence up to 3 years or monetary penalty**; prosecuted by the cantonal criminal justice authorities, in addition to the evasion fine. Limitation 15 years (Art. 189). — [Statute] [DBG](https://www.fedlex.admin.ch/eli/cc/1991/1184_1184_1184/de)
- **Besondere Untersuchungsmassnahmen der ESTV (Arts. 190–195 DBG)**: for serious tax offences (schwere Steuerwiderhandlungen) the ESTV can investigate with coercive measures (incl. bank information) under administrative criminal law. — [Statute] [DBG](https://www.fedlex.admin.ch/eli/cc/1991/1184_1184_1184/de)

**Payment, interest, remission**
- **Fälligkeit and Bezug (Arts. 161–166 DBG)**: the direct federal tax is generally due on **31 March** of the year following the tax period (Art. 161, unless the canton sets otherwise), payable within 30 days; the cantons issue **provisional invoices** (provisorischer Bezug, Art. 162) based on the last return or the probable amount, then a final settlement (Schlussrechnung) with refunds or additional amounts. Cantonal/municipal taxes follow cantonal rules (often instalments). — [Statute] [DBG](https://www.fedlex.admin.ch/eli/cc/1991/1184_1184_1184/de)
- **Interest rates for federal taxes 2026**: **Verzugszins (late-payment interest) and Rückerstattungszins 4.00 %** (2025: 4.50 %); **Vergütungszins on voluntary advance payments 0.00 %** (2025: 0.75 %). Rates are reviewed annually under the federal Zinssatzverordnung. Cantonal rates differ (see e.g. ZG, SO). — [ESTV news](https://www.estv.admin.ch/de/newnsb/bCxrY5LSQCuF); [law.ch](https://law.ch/lawnews/2025/10/neue-verguetungs-und-verzugszinssaetze-fuer-bundessteuern-abgaben-sanktionen-ab-2026/); [Kanton ZG](https://zg.ch/en/steuern-finanzen/steuern/inkasso/zinsenskonto); [Kanton SO](https://so.ch/verwaltung/finanzdepartement/steueramt/zahlungen/zinssaetze/)
- **Steuererlass (Art. 167–167g DBG, revised by the Federal Act on the new remission rules, in force since 1 Jan 2016, plus the Steuererlassverordnung SR 642.121)**: taxes, interest and fines can be fully or partly remitted where payment would mean **severe hardship (grosse Härte)** due to an emergency situation; request to the cantonal authority; for the federal tax decided by the canton (since 2016, no longer by a federal commission). Remission does not cover Nachsteuer cases where the person caused the emergency. — [Statute] [DBG](https://www.fedlex.admin.ch/eli/cc/1991/1184_1184_1184/de) (2016 date from researcher knowledge; verify)

### Inferences

## 6. Bank secrecy and tax: domestic vs. international (AEOI)

### Takeaway
Swiss bank-client secrecy (Art. 47 BankG) still applies **domestically**: Swiss banks do not report residents' accounts to Swiss tax authorities; compliance relies on self-declaration, the 35 % Verrechnungssteuer as an incentive to declare, and investigative powers only for serious offences. Internationally, Switzerland has exchanged account data automatically (AEOI/AIA, OECD Common Reporting Standard) since 2018 (data from 2017), and receives data on Swiss residents' **foreign** accounts, which is forwarded to the cantonal tax authorities.

### Cited Findings
- AEOI is the OECD standard (model agreement, CRS, commentary, technical guidance); data are exchanged once a year; banks report name, address, tax residence, date of birth, TIN, account number, balance, gross interest/dividends and gross sale proceeds. Domestic bank secrecy is not affected. — [SHKB fact sheet](https://image.shkb.ch/dynamic/noop/a83594e34f0f3de8be1712726d916331e9b6a1f9/automatischer-informationsaustausch.pdf); [economiesuisse](https://www.economiesuisse.ch/de/api/pdf/artikel/austausch-von-finanzdaten-zu-steuerzwecken-bankgeheimnis-im-internationalen-verhaeltnis)
- In 2024 the ESTV sent information on about **3.7 million financial accounts** to partner states (over 100 states). — [finews.ch](https://www.finews.ch/news/finanzplatz/64723-aia-informationsaustausch-estv-2024)
- Legal basis: AIAG (SR 653.1) in force 1 Jan 2017, first exchange 2018. — [law.ch 2017](https://law.ch/lawnews/2017/01/finanzkonten-automatischer-informationsaustausch-aia/); AIAG reference from researcher knowledge
- **Art. 47 BankG** criminalises breaches of bank-client secrecy; in domestic tax matters banks have no duty to provide information in ordinary assessment and evasion proceedings; disclosure only in criminal proceedings for tax fraud/serious offences (Art. 190 ff. DBG). — [Statute] [BankG](https://www.fedlex.admin.ch/eli/cc/51/117_121_129/de), [DBG](https://www.fedlex.admin.ch/eli/cc/1991/1184_1184_1184/de)

### Inferences
- Practical consequence: foreign accounts of Swiss residents are visible to the cantonal tax office via AEOI, so voluntary disclosure (Selbstanzeige) before AEOI data arrive is the only penalty-free route; once the authority "knows" through AEOI data, the Selbstanzeige is no longer straflos.

### Gaps
- No official source fetched on how/when ESTV forwards incoming AEOI data to cantons, or on the cantonal practice treating AEOI data as "Kenntnis" for Selbstanzeige purposes. Needs ESTV AIA page.

## 7. Recent and pending reforms (state as of 3 October 2026)

### Takeaway
Two big reforms affecting private persons are decided but not yet in force: **abolition of the Eigenmietwert from 1 Jan 2029** and **individual taxation from 1 Jan 2032**. The cold-progression adjustment continues annually (latest: for tax year 2027). The proposed higher tax on capital withdrawals from pillar 2/3a (Entlastungspaket 27) was dropped by Parliament in spring 2026. OECD minimum tax is irrelevant to private persons.

### Cited Findings

**Eigenmietwert (imputed rental value) abolition**
- On **28 September 2025** People and cantons accepted the Bundesbeschluss on cantonal property taxes on second homes, thereby triggering the abolition of imputed rent taxation; yes share **57.7 %**. — [SRF](https://www.srf.ch/news/schweiz/abstimmung-28-september-57-7-prozent-sagen-ja-zur-abschaffung-des-eigenmietwerts); [admin.ch](https://www.admin.ch/de/newnsb/yGTqBPowRqyVh0zPokW-q)
- On **1 April 2026** the Federal Council set entry into force for **1 January 2029**. Reason: StHG custom of ≥ 2 years for cantonal adaptation; majority of cantonal finance directors (FDK) wanted 2029 at the earliest. — [admin.ch press release](https://www.admin.ch/de/newnsb/yGTqBPowRqyVh0zPokW-q); [SRF](https://www.srf.ch/news/schweiz/bundesratsentscheid-bundesrat-setzt-abschaffung-des-eigenmietwerts-ab-2029-in-kraft)
- Content from 1 Jan 2029 (all levels): no Eigenmietwert on owner-occupied **primary and secondary** homes; **no deduction for maintenance costs** of owner-occupied property (still allowed for rented property); **mortgage interest deductible only in proportion to rented/leased property** to total assets; time- and amount-limited **Ersterwerberabzug** for first-time buyers; federal deductions for energy-saving/environmental investments end (cantons may keep them for a limited time); cantons may introduce a **property tax on mainly owner-occupied second homes**. — [admin.ch press release](https://www.admin.ch/de/newnsb/yGTqBPowRqyVh0zPokW-q)
- Until end of 2028 the current system (Eigenmietwert taxed; maintenance and interest deductible) remains in force. Kanton Zürich, for example, decided in Nov 2025 not to raise its Eigenmietwerte. — [admin.ch](https://www.admin.ch/de/newnsb/yGTqBPowRqyVh0zPokW-q); [Kanton ZH](https://www.zh.ch/de/news-uebersicht/medienmitteilungen/2025/11/kanton-zuerich-verzichtet-auf-erhoehung-der-eigenmietwerte.html)

**Individualbesteuerung** — see section 4: accepted 8 March 2026 (54.23 %), in force **1 Jan 2032** (BR decision Aug 2026). — [ESTV](https://www.estv.admin.ch/de/individualbesteuerung); [20 Minuten](https://www.20min.ch/story/heiratsstrafe-bundesrat-individualbesteuerung-tritt-erst-im-jahr-2032-in-kraft-103619469)

**Kalte Progression (Art. 39 DBG; annual adjustment)**
- Art. 39 DBG requires the EFD to adjust federal tariffs and deductions annually for inflation (LIK, reference date 30 June). — [Statute] [DBG](https://www.fedlex.admin.ch/eli/cc/1991/1184_1184_1184/de); described in [admin.ch 22 Sep 2026](https://www.admin.ch/de/newnsb/rq9rumbCaofX)
- For **tax year 2026**: EFD ordinance of 10 Sep 2025, inflation since last adjustment 0.1 %; in force 1 Jan 2026. Separately, the commuting deduction for private vehicles was raised to **CHF 0.75/km** (from 0.70). — [ESTV news](https://www.estv.admin.ch/de/newnsb/VzaAUrhkPx2EPde4a6e3O); ESTV circular 2-215-D-2025 [PDF](https://www.estv.admin.ch/dam/de/sd-web/vFK3ntWLQ4s4/2-215-D-2025-d.pdf)
- For **tax year 2027** (press release **22 Sep 2026**): inflation **0.47 %** (LIK, 30 June 2026). Adjusted: childcare deduction (Drittbetreuung) CHF **25,900** (was 25,800); vocational training costs CHF **13,100** (was 13,000); married-couple amount CHF **29,900** (was 29,700; the press release's "Verheiratete" threshold — exact nature to verify); top-rate threshold CHF **946,000** (was 941,400); gambling-winnings exemption CHF **1,076,100** (was 1,071,000). Berufskostenverordnung amounts unchanged. Source-tax rules adjusted accordingly. — [admin.ch](https://www.admin.ch/de/newnsb/rq9rumbCaofX)

**Entlastungspaket 27 / capital withdrawals**
- The Federal Council proposed (consultation 29 Jan–5 May 2025) higher taxation of lump-sum withdrawals from pillar 2 and 3a (~CHF 160 m/yr). Parliament struck this measure in early March 2026 (National Council as second chamber rejected it); current privileged separate taxation of capital benefits (Art. 38 DBG, 1/5 of the tariff) stays. — [BDO](https://www.bdo.ch/de-ch/publikationen/kapitalbezug-vorsorge-steuererhoehung-abgelehnt); [VermögensZentrum](https://www.vermoegenszentrum.ch/wissen/kapitalbezug-parlament-will-steuern-nicht-erhoehen); [OBT](https://www.obt.ch/de/infoboard/planungssicherheit-bei-pensionierung-keine-steuererhoehung-auf-kapitalbezuegen)

- Constitutional basis accepted 18 June 2023; 15 % minimum for groups with turnover ≥ EUR 750 m; Swiss QDMTT since 1 Jan 2024. Not relevant to private persons. — [Statute] [BV Art. 129a / transitional provision](https://www.fedlex.admin.ch/eli/cc/1999/404/de) (dates from researcher knowledge; verify)
