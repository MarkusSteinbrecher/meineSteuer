# Filing a Swiss personal tax return (Steuererklärung): process, forms, deadlines, software, aftermath

State: researched 3 October 2026 (tax year 2025, filed in 2026). Scope: natural persons taxed in the ordinary way (not Quellensteuer-only). Research note: several SEO aggregator sites (accountex.ch, checkeverything.ch, clarotax.ch, mein-steuerrechner.ch) publish per-canton deadline tables that **contradict each other and the official sites** (e.g. Bern given as 15 March, 31 May or 31 July; Jura as 28 Feb vs 31 March). They are NOT used as sources below except where flagged; per-canton facts come from official cantonal pages.

## 1. Standard forms and their purpose; how federal and cantonal tax are filed together

### Takeaway
One single return per household (married couples/registered partners file jointly) covers the cantonal + communal tax (Staats- und Gemeindesteuer, incl. church tax) **and** the direct federal tax (direkte Bundessteuer); the canton assesses and collects both. The return = a main form (Hauptformular / déclaration / dichiarazione) plus a set of standard schedules (Verzeichnisse/Hilfsblätter), whose names and letters differ by canton but whose content is harmonised (StHG).

### Cited Findings
- The Zurich form is titled "Steuererklärung 2025 für natürliche Personen — Staats-, Gemeinde- und direkte Bundessteuer"; the deduction columns on the main form are split into "Staatssteuer" and "Bundessteuer" (because some deduction caps differ, e.g. commuting costs capped at CHF 3,300 for the federal tax, child deduction amounts differ) — [ZH Wegleitung 2025 (unterjährig edition), pp. 9–19, 31](https://www.zh.ch/content/dam/zhweb/bilder-dokumente/themen/steuern-finanzen/steuern/natuerlichepersonen/2025/est-wegleitungen-uj/305%20Wegleitung%20ZH%202025%20UJ%20bf%20DEF.pdf)
- Zurich's set of forms referenced in the 2025 guide: Steuererklärung (main form, 4 pages); Wertschriften- und Guthabenverzeichnis mit Verrechnungsantrag (securities & bank balances + claim for refund of 35% withholding tax); Liegenschaftenverzeichnis (real estate: rental value/Eigenmietwert, rents, maintenance costs, tax value); Schuldenverzeichnis (debts and debt interest); Formular Berufsauslagen / Versicherungsprämien (employment expenses + insurance premiums/savings interest deduction); Berufsorientierte Aus- und Weiterbildungskosten; Aufstellung über Krankheits- und Unfallkosten; Aufstellung über behinderungsbedingte Kosten; Qualifizierte Beteiligungen im Privat-/Geschäftsvermögen; Hilfsblatt A (self-employed), Hilfsblatt B/G (agriculture), Hilfsblatt STAF — [ZH Wegleitung 2025, pp. 2, 31](https://www.zh.ch/content/dam/zhweb/bilder-dokumente/themen/steuern-finanzen/steuern/natuerlichepersonen/2025/est-wegleitungen-uj/305%20Wegleitung%20ZH%202025%20UJ%20bf%20DEF.pdf); [ZH Steuererklärung Privatpersonen](https://www.zh.ch/de/steuern-finanzen/steuern/steuern-natuerliche-personen/steuererklaerung-natuerliche-personen.html)
- Basel-Stadt uses lettered forms for 2025: Hauptformular; Formular W (Wertschriftenverzeichnis); L (Liegenschaftenverzeichnis); S (Schuldenverzeichnis); B (Berufskosten); A (Alimente/Unterstützungen); K/Z/F (Krankheitskosten / Zuwendungen = donations / Kinderbetreuung = childcare); E (Erbengemeinschaft); D (DA‑1/R‑US 164: foreign withholding-tax credit / additional US withholding); plus forms for qualified participations — [BS: Wegleitungen und Formulare für natürliche Personen](https://www.bs.ch/en/node/30345); [BS Hauptformular 2025 (Ansichtsexemplar)](https://media.bs.ch/original_file/65fcfdaa82df1b23cb365a951416678aef461436/15000-fw-np-ste-25-02-hauptformular-ansichtsexemplar-0.pdf)
- Zurich: "Belege zu den Aufstellungen und Formularen sind nur dann zusammen mit der Steuererklärung einzureichen, wenn dies ausdrücklich verlangt ist. Insbesondere sind … die Belege zu den Liegenschaftskosten und gemeinnützigen Zuwendungen erst im Einschätzungsverfahren auf Verlangen nachzureichen." Paper copies must be A4; documents are not returned; CDs/DVDs not accepted — [ZH Wegleitung 2025, p. 31](https://www.zh.ch/content/dam/zhweb/bilder-dokumente/themen/steuern-finanzen/steuern/natuerlichepersonen/2025/est-wegleitungen-uj/305%20Wegleitung%20ZH%202025%20UJ%20bf%20DEF.pdf)
- Zurich scans all paper submissions and destroys the paper after scanning; all files are kept electronically — [ZH Wegleitung 2025, p. 6](https://www.zh.ch/content/dam/zhweb/bilder-dokumente/themen/steuern-finanzen/steuern/natuerlichepersonen/2025/est-wegleitungen-uj/305%20Wegleitung%20ZH%202025%20UJ%20bf%20DEF.pdf)
- Handwritten paper forms: block letters, blue/black pen, no pencil/typewriter; data written outside the fields is ignored by the scanner — [ZH Wegleitung 2025, p. 32](https://www.zh.ch/content/dam/zhweb/bilder-dokumente/themen/steuern-finanzen/steuern/natuerlichepersonen/2025/est-wegleitungen-uj/305%20Wegleitung%20ZH%202025%20UJ%20bf%20DEF.pdf)
- The Wertschriftenverzeichnis doubles as the application for refund of the 35% Verrechnungssteuer (withholding tax) on Swiss interest/dividends ("mit Verrechnungsantrag"); the canton credits/refunds it via the tax bill — [ZH Wegleitung 2025, pp. 24–27](https://www.zh.ch/content/dam/zhweb/bilder-dokumente/themen/steuern-finanzen/steuern/natuerlichepersonen/2025/est-wegleitungen-uj/305%20Wegleitung%20ZH%202025%20UJ%20bf%20DEF.pdf)

- For the guide: describe the forms generically by purpose and show 2–3 cantonal naming examples (ZH descriptive names; BS letter codes), because each canton brands them differently.
- Online software generates the right schedules automatically; the "form set" matters mostly for paper filers.

### Gaps

## 2. Documents checklist

### Takeaway
Core documents: Lohnausweis (salary certificate, Form 11), pension/AHV certificates, bank/depot year-end statements (ideally as eSteuerauszug), 3a and Pensionskasse buy-in certificates, health insurer premium statement, mortgage/debt interest statements, receipts for deductions (childcare, training, medical, donations, maintenance). Many receipts are only shown on request.

### Cited Findings
- Zurich's "So gehen Sie am besten vor" checklist before filling in: Lohnausweis of the employer(s); Bescheinigung der Arbeitslosenkasse (unemployment benefits); Rentenbescheinigungen; credit notes for interest and dividends; purchase/sale notes for bonds/shares; depot bank statements (Wertschriftenverzeichnisse der Depotbanken); certificate of pension-fund contributions if not on the Lohnausweis; certificate of the insurer/bank foundation for 3a contributions — [ZH Wegleitung 2025, p. 31](https://www.zh.ch/content/dam/zhweb/bilder-dokumente/themen/steuern-finanzen/steuern/natuerlichepersonen/2025/est-wegleitungen-uj/305%20Wegleitung%20ZH%202025%20UJ%20bf%20DEF.pdf)
- Zurich required attachments (Beilagen): employees — Lohnausweis(e) incl. attachments, certificates on employee share plans, form Berufsauslagen/Versicherungsprämien; self-employed — signed annual accounts (or simplified records), Hilfsblatt A; board members — certificate of fees; unemployed — Arbeitslosenkasse certificate; property owners — Liegenschaftenverzeichnis; heirs — statement on the Erbengemeinschaft. When claiming deductions: statement of actual property maintenance (if not flat rate), actual employment expenses, charitable donations (gemeinnützige Zuwendungen), support payments, Fremdbetreuungskosten für Kinder (childcare), 3a certificates, AHV/pension contribution certificates, Schuldenverzeichnis, training costs, medical costs, disability costs — [ZH Wegleitung 2025, p. 31](https://www.zh.ch/content/dam/zhweb/bilder-dokumente/themen/steuern-finanzen/steuern/natuerlichepersonen/2025/est-wegleitungen-uj/305%20Wegleitung%20ZH%202025%20UJ%20bf%20DEF.pdf)
- Medical costs (ZH): attach the health insurer's annual cost & benefit statement (Kosten- und Leistungsabrechnung der Krankenkasse) together with the form "Aufstellung über Krankheits- und Unfallkosten" — [ZH Wegleitung 2025, p. ~24 (Krankheitskosten section)](https://www.zh.ch/content/dam/zhweb/bilder-dokumente/themen/steuern-finanzen/steuern/natuerlichepersonen/2025/est-wegleitungen-uj/305%20Wegleitung%20ZH%202025%20UJ%20bf%20DEF.pdf)
- eSteuerauszug (electronic tax statement): PDF with embedded barcode under standard eCH-0196, containing year-end balances, depot values, interest/dividends, mortgages/loans, transactions, deductible fees; can be imported into the cantonal software so values populate automatically. Bank fees vary widely: free at many cantonal banks (e.g. AKB, BEKB, LUKB, SZKB, TKB, UKB), Valiant, Neon and most robo-advisors; otherwise roughly CHF 21.65–540.50, UBS CHF 100–250 depending on holdings — [moneyland.ch: E-Steuerauszug](https://www.moneyland.ch/de/esteuerauszug-schweiz)
- All cantons have implemented the eSteuerauszug standard and can process it electronically (since about January 2024) — [search-result summary of finanzdepot.ch / bank documentation](https://finanzdepot.ch/gratis-steuerauszug-schweiz-das-musst-du-wissen/) (secondary; not verified against an official cantonal source)
- Bank checklists for the return exist (e.g. TKB, LUKB) — [TKB Checkliste Steuererklärung](https://www.tkb.ch/.dam/05f8dee5-da55-4c57-8638-46a1e3fe2246/Checkliste-Steuererklaerung_interaktiv.pdf); [LUKB Checkliste](https://www.lukb.ch/.dam/0e19f651-2328-43d4-b5b3-4b3f05e1baaa/LUKB-Steuererklaerung-Checkliste.pdf)

### Consolidated checklist (for the guide; derived from the sources above)
- Personal: last year's return and assessment (for comparison), tax letter with access code/ID.
- Income: Lohnausweis(e); AHV/IV/pension certificates; unemployment/sickness/accident daily-allowance certificates; board fees; alimony received; side income records; self-employed accounts.
- Assets: bank and depot statements at 31 Dec (eSteuerauszug), crypto holdings, life insurance surrender values (Rückkaufswert), vehicles, foreign assets/real estate.
- Debts: mortgage and loan statements with balance at 31 Dec and interest paid.
- Deductions: 3a certificate (Bescheinigung Säule 3a); Pensionskasse buy-in certificate (Einkauf 2. Säule); health insurance premium statement (Prämienbescheinigung) and premium-reduction notices; Kosten- und Leistungsabrechnung for medical costs; childcare invoices (Kita, Tagesmutter); training/further-education invoices; donation receipts; alimony paid; property maintenance invoices; commuting details (GA/ÖV-Abo, km).

### Inferences
- Reconciling the Lohnausweis (gross salary, pension contributions in boxes 9/10, Spesen) with the return is the single highest-value check for most employees.

### Gaps
- Which cantons require the Lohnausweis as an upload vs. accept it on request only was not compiled for all 26.

## 3. Per-canton facts: deadlines (TY 2025), extensions, software / e-filing, login, payment

### Takeaway
Most cantons set 31 March 2026, but early (NE 20 Feb, JU end Feb, BE/VD 15 March) and late (ZG, OW, AI, TI 30 April) deadlines exist; LU, GL, AR, TG and BL print the date on the individual form. First extensions are nearly everywhere free online (exceptions: FR and VS charge CHF 20 from the first extension; GE CHF 20–60). The 2025 tax period marks a broad shift to browser-only software with signature-free submission (ZH, AG, SG, ZG, BS, AI dropped or replaced download software); AGOV is the emerging standard login (ZH, BE, AG, SG, BS, AR), while others use access codes from the tax letter (SO, BL, VD, UR) or own logins (ZG ZugLogin, JU SwissID).

### Sourcing note
Per-canton facts were gathered by three parallel research passes restricted to official cantonal sites (October 2026). Cells marked **(S)** come from a search-engine summary of an official page rather than a page read in full; **?** = not verified / not found. Do not fill gaps from the aggregator sites mentioned at the top.

## 4. After filing: invoices, payment, interest, assessment, objection (federal + cantonal)

### Takeaway
Pay during the tax year on the basis of provisional invoices (instalments differ by canton); the canton later issues the assessment (Veranlagungsverfügung / Einschätzungsentscheid) and a final invoice with interest settlement. The direct federal tax for year N falls due on 1 March N+1 and must be paid by 31 March N+1. Objections must be lodged within 30 days.

### Cited Findings
- Direct federal tax: general due date 1 March of the following year with a 30-day payment period (i.e. by 31 March); the canton sends a provisional federal invoice around 1 March; provisional invoices generally only from CHF 300 of tax (Schwyz practice) — [SZ Steuerverwaltung: Direkte Bundessteuer](https://www.sz.ch/verwaltung/finanzdepartement/steuerverwaltung/steuerbezug-steuerrechnung/direkte-bundessteuer.html/8756-8758-8802-10332-10354-10494-10643); legal basis Art. 161 and 163 DBG — [law.ch: Steuerfälligkeit/Steuerbezug](https://law.ch/lawinfo/steuerverfahrensrecht/bezug-steuersicherung/steuerfaelligkeit-steuerbezug/)
- Due date is not postponed by an objection or appeal (Art. 161 para. 5 DBG); payment within 30 days of due date (Art. 163 para. 1 DBG) — [SZ: Direkte Bundessteuer](https://www.sz.ch/verwaltung/finanzdepartement/steuerverwaltung/steuerbezug-steuerrechnung/direkte-bundessteuer.html/8756-8758-8802-10332-10354-10494-10643)
- Federal interest rates (EFD ordinance, all federal taxes incl. DBG): from 1 Jan 2026 Verzugszins (late-payment interest) 4.0% (2025: 4.5%); Vergütungszins on refunds 4.0%; Vergütungszins on voluntary advance payments of direct federal tax 0.0% (2025: 0.75%) — [law.ch news Oct 2025](https://law.ch/lawnews/2025/10/neue-verguetungs-und-verzugszinssaetze-fuer-bundessteuern-abgaben-sanktionen-ab-2026/); [RSM Switzerland: Parameter per 1.1.2026](https://www.rsm.global/switzerland/de/node/703); [ESTV circular 2-215-D-2025](https://www.estv.admin.ch/dam/de/sd-web/vFK3ntWLQ4s4/2-215-D-2025-d.pdf)
- For 2027 the federal rates remain unchanged (4.00% Verzugszins, 4.00% refund interest, 0.00% on voluntary DBG advance payments); announced 22 Sept 2026 — [admin.ch press release](https://www.admin.ch/de/newnsb/bCxrY5LSQCuF)
- Zurich (Staats- und Gemeindesteuern) uses a symmetric "Ausgleichszins" system: payments made in the tax year before 1 October earn interest; from 1 October the definitive tax amount bears interest charged to the taxpayer; rate in 2025: 1% both ways; interest also charged on deferrals/instalment plans; after the final invoice (Schlussrechnung) and its 30-day payment period, Verzugszins of 4.5% applies (rate in force since 2008, temporarily 0.25% May–Dec 2020) — [ZH Wegleitung 2025, p. 7](https://www.zh.ch/content/dam/zhweb/bilder-dokumente/themen/steuern-finanzen/steuern/natuerlichepersonen/2025/est-wegleitungen-uj/305%20Wegleitung%20ZH%202025%20UJ%20bf%20DEF.pdf)
- Objection (Einsprache / réclamation / reclamo): ZH: written objection within 30 days of service of the assessment (§ 140 StG ZH; Art. 132 DBG federal equivalent); the period starts the day after service and is a forfeiture deadline — late objections are not considered unless restitution is granted — [ZH Steuerrekursgericht decision (entscheidsuche)](https://entscheidsuche.ch/docs/ZH_Steuerrekurs/ZH_SRK_001_DB-2025-148_2026-01-23.pdf)
- An assessment by discretion (Ermessenseinschätzung, after failure to file despite reminder) can only be challenged for "offensichtliche Unrichtigkeit"; the objection must be reasoned, name evidence, and usually succeeds only if the missing return/documents are supplied — [ZH Wegleitung 2025, p. 6](https://www.zh.ch/content/dam/zhweb/bilder-dokumente/themen/steuern-finanzen/steuern/natuerlichepersonen/2025/est-wegleitungen-uj/305%20Wegleitung%20ZH%202025%20UJ%20bf%20DEF.pdf)
- Limitation: the right to assess expires 5 years after the end of the tax period (Art. 120 DBG), absolutely after 15 years; Nachsteuer (back tax) proceedings may be opened up to 10 years after the end of the tax period, and back tax determined up to 15 years — [SO Steuerbuch 138-01 Veranlagungsverjährung](https://steuerbuch.so.ch/fileadmin/steuerbuch/230220/138-01_Veranlagungsverjaehrung_V01_2020-12-10.pdf); [BL Steuerbuch Band 3, 166-01](https://kanton.baselland.ch/finanz-und-kirchendirektion/steuerverwaltung-steuerbuch/band-3/nachsteuern-und-steuerstrafrecht/downloads-1/band3_166_01.pdf)
- Late filing sanctions: Mahnung (reminder, "Mahnfristen sind nicht erstreckbar"), then Ermessenseinschätzung; procedural fine for violation of procedural duties up to CHF 1,000, in serious/repeat cases up to CHF 10,000 (Art. 174 DBG; § 234 StG ZH) — [ZH Wegleitung 2025, p. 6](https://www.zh.ch/content/dam/zhweb/bilder-dokumente/themen/steuern-finanzen/steuern/natuerlichepersonen/2025/est-wegleitungen-uj/305%20Wegleitung%20ZH%202025%20UJ%20bf%20DEF.pdf)
- Tax evasion: attempted evasion fine = 2/3 of the fine for completed evasion; completed evasion: back tax plus fine from 1/3 to 3x the back tax; first voluntary disclosure (straflose Selbstanzeige) is penalty-free under conditions and requires an explicit statement that undeclared income/assets are being disclosed — [ZH Wegleitung 2025, p. 7](https://www.zh.ch/content/dam/zhweb/bilder-dokumente/themen/steuern-finanzen/steuern/natuerlichepersonen/2025/est-wegleitungen-uj/305%20Wegleitung%20ZH%202025%20UJ%20bf%20DEF.pdf)

### Inferences
- With 0% interest on voluntary federal advance payments in 2026/2027, prepaying the direct federal tax no longer earns anything; cantonal Skonto/Vergütungszins may still make prepaying cantonal tax worthwhile (canton-specific, see table).

## 5. Practical tips: checking the assessment, record keeping, Treuhänder vs DIY, pitfalls

### Takeaway
Compare the assessment with your return line by line (and with last year), object within 30 days if needed; keep records at least until the assessment is final and practically ~10 years (Nachsteuer window); a Treuhänder costs a few hundred francs for a simple return but quality varies.

### Cited Findings
- Zurich advises checking every document received from the tax office immediately (correspondence, Einschätzungsvorschläge, decisions, invoices) because missed deadlines carry legal consequences; complete and precise returns reduce queries and back-tax claims — [ZH Wegleitung 2025, p. 6](https://www.zh.ch/content/dam/zhweb/bilder-dokumente/themen/steuern-finanzen/steuern/natuerlichepersonen/2025/est-wegleitungen-uj/305%20Wegleitung%20ZH%202025%20UJ%20bf%20DEF.pdf)
- Common errors (BDO, Feb 2025): timing of property maintenance deductions; missed partial taxation of dividends from qualified holdings; unclaimed foreign withholding-tax refunds (3-year window); training costs; reduced Eigenmietwert during vacancy; Pillar 2/3a payments missed cannot be claimed after the fact; unreimbursed medical/dental costs; failure to declare foreign real estate; mistaken belief that side income under CHF 2,500 (AHV threshold) is tax-free — [moneycab: BDO 10 gängige Steuerfehler](https://www.moneycab.com/schweiz/bdo-10-gaengige-steuerfehler-die-nerven-und-geld-kosten/)
- Typos (e.g. 10,000 vs 100,000 salary) and scanner misreads of handwriting are frequent; comparing with the previous year's return/assessment is a good underused check — [cash.ch interview with a Steuerkommissar](https://www.cash.ch/news/top-news/steuerkommissar-gerade-im-liegenschaftsbereich-fallen-die-abzuge-manchmal-sehr-kreativ-aus-794994) (via search snippet)
- Treuhänder fees: association recommendations CHF 80–420/hour depending on difficulty and who does the work; reputable firms state fees up front — [Beobachter: Treuhänder- und Steuerberater-Honorar](https://www.beobachter.ch/beratung/rechtsratgeber/konsum/dienstleistungen/treuhand-steuerberatung/treuhander-und-steuerberater-honorar)
- Kassensturz test (SRF, 21 March 2017, older): 12 tax advisers in BS and AG; 9 of 12 failed to find most planted errors; charges CHF 75–300; advice: check diplomas/association membership, expect CHF 200–500 for a simple return, get a quote, review the return yourself because you remain liable — [SRF Kassensturz](https://www.srf.ch/sendungen/kassensturz-espresso/steuerberater-im-test-fast-alle-fallen-durch)
- Simple return without property/securities about CHF 200 at a Treuhänder (search snippet of commercial blog; low reliability) — [pfeffersack.ch](https://pfeffersack.ch/blog/treuhaender-kosten-schweiz-2026)
- Retention: no explicit statutory retention duty for private persons' receipts was found; the practical horizon is the limitation periods (assessment 5 years, Nachsteuer 10 years, absolute 15 years — see section 4). Zurich only demands many receipts "auf Verlangen" during the assessment, so they must be kept at least until the assessment is final — [ZH Wegleitung 2025, p. 31](https://www.zh.ch/content/dam/zhweb/bilder-dokumente/themen/steuern-finanzen/steuern/natuerlichepersonen/2025/est-wegleitungen-uj/305%20Wegleitung%20ZH%202025%20UJ%20bf%20DEF.pdf); self-employed: 10-year retention under the Code of Obligations (Art. 958f OR) — general knowledge, not fetched in this session.

### Inferences
- Recommend in the guide: keep all tax-relevant documents ≥10 years; keep property investment records (purchase, value-adding investments) until the property is sold + 10 years, since they matter for Grundstückgewinnsteuer (inference; not sourced).

- No K-Tipp/Beobachter article with current (2025/2026) Treuhänder price surveys was retrieved.
- Deductibility of tax-preparation costs not verified.
