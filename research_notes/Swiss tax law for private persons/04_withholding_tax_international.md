# Swiss Withholding Tax on Earned Income (Quellensteuer / impôt à la source) and International Aspects of Personal Taxation

State researched: 3 October 2026. Legal basis: DBG Art. 83–101 (federal), StHG Art. 32–38a (cantons), Quellensteuerverordnung (QStV, SR 642.118.2), ESTV Kreisschreiben Nr. 45 (KS 45, "Quellenbesteuerung des Erwerbseinkommens von Arbeitnehmern"). Note: the ESTV Quellensteuer landing page URL tried (estv.admin.ch/estv/de/home/direkte-bundessteuer/quellensteuer.html) returned 404 during research (ESTV site was restructured); facts below rely on cantonal authority documents and law-firm summaries.

## 1. Who is subject to Quellensteuer; tariff codes; monthly vs annual model

### Takeaway
Foreign employees resident in Switzerland without a settlement permit C (i.e., permits B, L, F, N, plus short-term workers) and all non-resident employees (cross-border commuters / permit G, international transport staff, board members, pension recipients abroad) are taxed at source; C-permit holders and people married to a Swiss citizen or C-permit holder are taxed ordinarily. The employer applies a tariff code of the form [letter][number of children][church Y/N], e.g. "C2N"; 5 French/Italian-speaking cantons (FR, GE, TI, VS, VD) use the annual model, all others the monthly model.

### Cited Findings
- Canton Zurich: Quellensteuer applies to foreign employees without settlement permit C who are not married to a person with a C permit or Swiss nationality; also cross-border commuters, weekly residents, international transport workers, and persons abroad receiving employee participation benefits from Zurich entities. Responsible: Kantonales Steueramt (employees, insurance recipients); Gemeindesteueramt (artists, athletes, board members, pension recipients). — [Kanton Zürich, Quellensteuer](https://www.zh.ch/de/steuern-finanzen/steuern/quellensteuer.html)
- Code structure: [letter][digit][Y/N] — letter = personal/family situation, digit = number of children entitling to a child deduction, Y/N = church tax included or not. Example "C2N" = dual-earner married couple, 2 children, no church tax. — [ibani guide](https://www.ibani.com/de/leitfaden/quellensteuer-tarifcodes-schweiz)
- Standard codes (ibani, secondary source): A = single (unmarried, separated, divorced, widowed) without children in household; B = married/registered partnership, single earner; C = married, both spouses earning (Doppelverdiener); H = single person living with children/dependants they mainly support; D = cantonal residual/secondary-income cases (described by ibani as "Restfälle"); E = simplified settlement procedure (vereinfachtes Abrechnungsverfahren) for small wages; F = Ticino-specific tariff for Italian cross-border workers whose spouse works outside Switzerland; G = replacement income (Ersatzeinkünfte: IV, accident, unemployment, maternity) paid not via employer; HE = board fees for non-residents (Verwaltungsratshonorare); ME = employee participations for non-residents. — [ibani guide](https://www.ibani.com/de/leitfaden/quellensteuer-tarifcodes-schweiz)
- German cross-border commuters (DBA CH-DE Art. 15a): L (≈A), M (≈B), N (≈C), P (≈H), Q (≈G), capped at 4.5% of gross. — [ibani](https://www.ibani.com/de/leitfaden/quellensteuer-tarifcodes-schweiz); [Kanton Thurgau StP 112 Nr. 1](https://steuerpraxis.tg.ch/steuerpraxis/2026-09/stp-112-nr-1-steuertarife-fur-quellenbesteuerung)
- "New" Italian cross-border commuters (2020 agreement, from 1 Jan 2024): R (≈A), S (≈B), T (≈C), U (≈H); 80% of the normal tariff ("V" for replacement income per ibani). "Old" Italian cross-border workers under the transitional regime keep A, B, C, H. Legal basis: QStV Art. 1 para. 1 (AS 2023 398). — [GR Steuerverwaltung FAQ CH-IT, 31.10.2023](https://www.gr.ch:443/DE/institutionen/verwaltung/dfg/stv/steuererklaerung/quellensteuer/Documents/GG%20Abkommen%20CH-IT%20FAQ%20DE.pdf); [ibani](https://www.ibani.com/de/leitfaden/quellensteuer-tarifcodes-schweiz)
- Non-resident spouse rule (GR practice for KS 45): if the spouse lives abroad, tariff B / M1 / S2 (single-earner) only on written application with proof that the foreign spouse has no income, within 3 months of starting the job and renewed annually; same for non-resident single parents (H / P / U). Until approved, A0 / L0 / R0 applies. If the spouse lives in Switzerland, no application needed. — [GR Ergänzungen zum KS 45 (2026)](https://www.gr.ch/DE/institutionen/verwaltung/dfg/stv/steuererklaerung/quellensteuer/WegleitungenMerkbltterTarife/KS%2045%20Erg%c3%a4nzungen-2026.pdf)
- Without the German residence certificate (Gre-1/Gre-2), the ordinary codes (A/B/C/E/H) apply instead of L–Q. — [Kanton Thurgau StP 112 Nr. 1](https://steuerpraxis.tg.ch/steuerpraxis/2026-09/stp-112-nr-1-steuertarife-fur-quellenbesteuerung)
- Monats- vs Jahresmodell: annual model (Jahresmodell) in Fribourg, Geneva, Ticino, Valais, Vaud; monthly model (Monatsmodell) in all other cantons. The model affects treatment of irregular payments (13th salary, bonuses) and mid-year changes. — [ibani](https://www.ibani.com/de/leitfaden/quellensteuer-tarifcodes-schweiz)
- KS 45 was published by ESTV on 12 June 2019 and sets binding, uniform rules for all cantons from 1 Jan 2021. — [law.ch](https://law.ch/lawnews/2019/06/quellenbesteuerung-von-arbeitnehmererwerbseinkommen-neues-kreisschreiben/)
- Employer procedure (GR): monthly settlement (half-yearly if ≤5 source-taxed staff); employer commission (Bezugsprovision) 1%, 2% with electronic ELM-QST / SofTax; reporting of new cross-border workers within 8 days; fines up to CHF 1,000 (serious cases CHF 10,000). — [GR Ergänzungen KS 45 (2026)](https://www.gr.ch/DE/institutionen/verwaltung/dfg/stv/steuererklaerung/quellensteuer/WegleitungenMerkbltterTarife/KS%2045%20Erg%c3%a4nzungen-2026.pdf)
- Corrections of the tariff code or source-taxable wage can only be requested until 31 March of the year following the payment (Art. 137 DBG Verfügung). — [forms-legal summary](https://forms-legal.com/de/switzerland/government/tax-forms/quellensteuer-tarifkorrektur-schweiz) (secondary source)

### Inferences
- Permits B, L, F (provisionally admitted), N (asylum seekers) and G all fall under "foreign without C permit" and are thus source-taxed; Swiss citizens and C holders resident in Switzerland are not (except when non-resident, e.g. Swiss living abroad and commuting in, who are source-taxed as non-residents).
- Tariff codes are federally harmonised via QStV/KS 45, but rates differ by canton (cantonal and municipal tax built in).

### Gaps
- Exact official definition of code D (ibani says "cantonal residual cases"; in KS 45 D is commonly described as the tariff for secondary employment / Nebenerwerb). Primary KS 45 text not retrieved; verify on ESTV.
- Primary SEM source for permit types not fetched in this session.

## 2. 2021 reform: NOV, Antrag, irreversibility, quasi-residency, Tarifkorrektur

### Takeaway
Since 1 Jan 2021 (law of 16 Dec 2016), residents earning ≥ CHF 120,000 gross are mandatorily assessed ordinarily after the fact (NOV); other residents can request a NOV by 31 March of the following year, and that choice is irrevocable until source-tax liability ends. Non-residents can request a NOV only if they are "quasi-resident" (≥90% of worldwide household income taxable in Switzerland), and must re-apply each year. The old "Tarifkorrektur" on request for extra deductions has been replaced by the NOV on request.

### Cited Findings
- Revised provisions in force 1 Jan 2021; the revision act dated 16 Dec 2016; DBG Art. 89, 89a, 99a. — [BE "Info NOV 2022"](https://www.sv.fin.be.ch/content/dam/sv_fin/dokumente/de/info_10minuten/info-nov_2022_de.pdf); [law.ch](https://law.ch/lawnews/2019/06/quellenbesteuerung-von-arbeitnehmererwerbseinkommen-neues-kreisschreiben/)
- Mandatory NOV (residents), Bern: gross employment income ≥ CHF 120,000; or other income not subject to source tax ≥ CHF 3,000; or Swiss-taxable assets ≥ CHF 150,000; or foreign real estate with official value ≥ CHF 300,000; or income from foreign real estate ≥ CHF 6,000. Once triggered, the person must request a tax return by 31 March of the following year (otherwise risk of tax evasion fine) and remains in NOV in subsequent years. Married couples living together are assessed jointly if one spouse qualifies. — [BE Info NOV 2022](https://www.sv.fin.be.ch/content/dam/sv_fin/dokumente/de/info_10minuten/info-nov_2022_de.pdf)
- NOV on request (residents): written application to the cantonal tax administration by 31 March of the following year, typically to claim deductions not (fully) covered by tariffs: pension fund buy-ins (2nd pillar), pillar 3a, higher professional expenses, debt interest, childcare costs, training costs, support of dependants, child deduction where no child allowances paid, alimony. If leaving Switzerland, the deadline ends at deregistration (Abmeldung). "Ein gestellter Antrag kann nicht zurückgezogen werden" — after applying, a tax return is issued every year until the end of source-tax liability; on separation/divorce both spouses remain in NOV. — [BE Info NOV 2022](https://www.sv.fin.be.ch/content/dam/sv_fin/dokumente/de/info_10minuten/info-nov_2022_de.pdf)
- Quasi-residency (non-residents): NOV on request by 31 March of the following year if (a) ≥90% of worldwide gross income (incl. spouse's) is taxable in Switzerland, or (b) income in the residence state too low to take personal/family deductions into account, or (c) a DBA requires Switzerland to grant certain deductions. Must be re-applied for each tax period; a Swiss representative or Swiss delivery address must be named, otherwise the application is dismissed and the withholding becomes final. — [BE Info NOV 2022](https://www.sv.fin.be.ch/content/dam/sv_fin/dokumente/de/info_10minuten/info-nov_2022_de.pdf); [BDO](https://www.bdo.ch/de-ch/publikationen/fachartikel/nl/neuerungen-bei-der-quellensteuer-ab-1-januar-2021-internationales)
- NOV ex officio (von Amtes wegen) for non-residents with mixed source-taxed and ordinarily taxed Swiss income/assets. — [BE Info NOV 2022](https://www.sv.fin.be.ch/content/dam/sv_fin/dokumente/de/info_10minuten/info-nov_2022_de.pdf)
- Procedure: reference-date principle (Stichtagsprinzip) — assessed for the whole year by the canton of residence/weekly stay/work at year-end or end of liability; source tax paid is credited without interest; only amounts actually remitted by the employer are credited (discrepancies are a civil claim against the employer). — [BE Info NOV 2022](https://www.sv.fin.be.ch/content/dam/sv_fin/dokumente/de/info_10minuten/info-nov_2022_de.pdf)
- The 90% quasi-residency test and 31 March deadline confirmed by BDO. — [BDO, Neuerungen bei der Quellensteuer ab 1.1.2021](https://www.bdo.ch/de-ch/publikationen/fachartikel/nl/neuerungen-bei-der-quellensteuer-ab-1-januar-2021-internationales)
- Zurich's page still uses the term "Tarifkorrektur" for administrative corrections. — [Kanton Zürich](https://www.zh.ch/de/steuern-finanzen/steuern/quellensteuer.html)

### Inferences
- The pre-2021 "Tarifkorrektur" on request (to claim 3a, PK buy-ins etc. without a full return) no longer exists; such deductions now require a NOV on request (Bern lists exactly these deductions as reasons). The remaining "correction" is a dispute of the tariff/wage applied (Verfügung under Art. 137 DBG, deadline 31 March). Treat this as consistent with the sources but verify wording on ESTV KS 45.
- Practical tip for the website: requesting NOV is attractive when large deductions exist (PK buy-ins, 3a), but it's a one-way door and brings worldwide income/assets (wealth tax) into the declaration.
- The CHF 3,000 / 150,000 / 300,000 / 6,000 thresholds are Bern's figures; other cantons may use different thresholds for "other income/assets".

### Gaps
- Uniform cantonal thresholds for assets/other income could not be verified; only Bern's figures sourced.
- Whether income falling below CHF 120,000 later returns one to pure source taxation: Bern says "In den Folgejahren erhalten Sie in jedem Fall wieder eine Steuererklärung" (i.e., stays in NOV), but federal text not checked.

## 3. Cross-border commuters (Grenzgänger / frontaliers)

### Takeaway
Each neighbour has its own regime: Germany — Switzerland withholds max 4.5% and Germany taxes with credit (lose status above 60 work-related non-return days); France — Geneva taxes at source, eight other cantons leave taxation to France (1983 accord), with a new 40% telework rule since 2026; Italy — new agreement since 2024 (new commuters: Switzerland 80% of tariff + Italian tax with credit; old commuters: Swiss-only taxation), 25% home-office allowed; Liechtenstein — private-sector commuters not taxed at source in Switzerland.

### Cited Findings
**Germany (DBA CH-DE Art. 15a)**
- Grenzgänger = resident in Germany, working in Switzerland, regularly returning home; certified by residence certificate Gre-1 (renewal Gre-2) from the German tax office; Swiss withholding capped at 4.5% of gross (tariffs L–Q). — [Kanton Thurgau StP 112 Nr. 1](https://steuerpraxis.tg.ch/steuerpraxis/2026-09/stp-112-nr-1-steuertarife-fur-quellenbesteuerung)
- 60-day rule: status lost if > 60 working days per calendar year of work-related non-return. Home-office days at the German residence do not count as non-return days. COVID consultation agreement terminated per 30 June 2022. — [ibani, Grenzgänger-Homeoffice](https://www.ibani.com/de/leitfaden/grenzgaenger-homeoffice-regeln); [EY, Keine Nichtrückkehrtage bei Homeoffice](https://www.ey.com/de_de/technical/steuernachrichten/grenzpendler-schweiz-keine-nichtrueckkehrtage-bei-homeoffice); [PwC, Kündigung Konsultationsvereinbarung](https://www.pwc.ch/de/insights/mybusiness/kuendigung-der-konsultationsvereinbarung-mit-deutschland.html)

**France**
- Geneva: Switzerland keeps full taxation right (source tax). Cantons BE, SO, BS, BL, VD, VS, NE, JU (1983 accord): France taxes, no Swiss withholding. — [EY tax alert](https://www.ey.com/en_ch/technical/tax-alerts/update-on-switzerland-france-cross-border-taxation-new-telework-and-reporting-obligations)
- Amendment to the CH-FR DBA entered into force 24 July 2025; telework rules apply from 1 Jan 2026: no change to cross-border status if telework in France ≤ 40% of working time (including up to 10 days of business travel abroad); Switzerland pays financial compensation to France for teleworking up to the threshold; automatic exchange of salary data between tax authorities; new reporting obligations from 1 Jan 2026. — [EY tax alert](https://www.ey.com/en_ch/technical/tax-alerts/update-on-switzerland-france-cross-border-taxation-new-telework-and-reporting-obligations); [PwC](https://www.pwc.ch/en/insights/mybusiness/telework-ch-fr.html)

**Italy (agreement of 23 Dec 2020)**
- In force 17 July 2023, applicable from 1 Jan 2024; 1974 agreement valid until 31 Dec 2023. — [GR FAQ CH-IT](https://www.gr.ch:443/DE/institutionen/verwaltung/dfg/stv/steuererklaerung/quellensteuer/Documents/GG%20Abkommen%20CH-IT%20FAQ%20DE.pdf)
- Definition (Art. 2 lit. b): resident in a municipality within the 20 km border zone (official joint list), working in the border area (CH: Graubünden, Ticino, Valais; IT: Lombardy, Piedmont, Aosta Valley, Bolzano), returning home in principle daily; up to 45 non-return days per year for work reasons allowed, otherwise status lost for the whole year. Nationality irrelevant; permit G not required/not decisive. — [GR FAQ CH-IT](https://www.gr.ch:443/DE/institutionen/verwaltung/dfg/stv/steuererklaerung/quellensteuer/Documents/GG%20Abkommen%20CH-IT%20FAQ%20DE.pdf)
- "New" Grenzgänger (not working as such between 31 Dec 2018 and 17 July 2023): Switzerland withholds 80% of the normal source tax; Italy taxes with credit. "Old" Grenzgänger (transitional regime, Art. 9): taxed exclusively in Switzerland at source; Italy does not tax the Swiss salary. No minimum employment duration; employer must have remitted source taxes. — [GR FAQ CH-IT](https://www.gr.ch:443/DE/institutionen/verwaltung/dfg/stv/steuererklaerung/quellensteuer/Documents/GG%20Abkommen%20CH-IT%20FAQ%20DE.pdf)
- Swiss residents working in Italy: from 2024 Italy withholds 80% of its rate; 1/5 of salary taxed in Switzerland, 4/5 exempt with progression. — [GR FAQ CH-IT](https://www.gr.ch:443/DE/institutionen/verwaltung/dfg/stv/steuererklaerung/quellensteuer/Documents/GG%20Abkommen%20CH-IT%20FAQ%20DE.pdf)
- Home office up to 25% of working time without effect on taxing right or status. Total burden for new commuters can reach 25–30% of net wages (press). — [PwC HR-Update 2023/2024](https://www.pwc.ch/de/insights/mybusiness/hr-update-neuerungen-2023-2024.html); [blue News](https://www.bluewin.ch/de/news/schweiz-und-italien-unterzeichnen-steuerregeln-fuer-grenzgaenger-li.2229197)

**Austria and Liechtenstein**
- GR table: Austria residents (private or public employment) — taxed "nach Tarif" (normal source tax). Liechtenstein residents in private employment — not subject to Swiss source tax (taxed in Liechtenstein); in public-law employment — taxed by tariff in Switzerland. — [GR Ergänzungen KS 45 (2026)](https://www.gr.ch/DE/institutionen/verwaltung/dfg/stv/steuererklaerung/quellensteuer/WegleitungenMerkbltterTarife/KS%2045%20Erg%c3%a4nzungen-2026.pdf)
- Austria taxes cross-border workers' Swiss income under its own law and credits the Swiss source tax (secondary summary). — [Comparis](https://www.comparis.ch/neu-in-der-schweiz/finanzen/quellensteuer/graubuenden)

### Inferences
- Website should stress: tax "Grenzgänger" status ≠ permit G; it is defined per treaty.
- Weekly returners (Wochenaufenthalter) are taxed at normal tariffs regardless of country (GR table).

### Gaps
- Exact financial-compensation figures (commonly cited: Geneva retrocedes 3.5% of gross wages of French frontaliers; France pays 4.5% to the eight cantons under the 1983 accord; Italy 2024 regime: compensation to Italian border municipalities for old commuters) — not verified in this session.
- My training memory suggests the CH-AT DBA assigns commuters to the residence state with a Swiss compensation share; the GR table instead shows normal Swiss source tax. Conflict not resolved — verify against DBA CH-AT Art. 15 on fedlex.
- Social-security home-office thresholds (EU framework agreement, 25–<50%) are a separate topic not covered here.

## 4. Double taxation agreements, progression reservation, foreign income/assets, pensions, AEOI

### Takeaway
Switzerland generally avoids double taxation as residence state by exempting foreign business/real-estate/permanent-establishment income and assets but applying the rate for total worldwide income (exemption with progression, Progressionsvorbehalt). Foreign pensions and investment income are generally taxable in Switzerland. Source tax on capital pension benefits paid to persons abroad can be refunded if a DBA allocates taxation to the residence state.

### Cited Findings
- Italy example illustrates the mechanism: 4/5 of salary exempt but included for the rate ("Steuerbefreiung mit Progression"). — [GR FAQ CH-IT](https://www.gr.ch:443/DE/institutionen/verwaltung/dfg/stv/steuererklaerung/quellensteuer/Documents/GG%20Abkommen%20CH-IT%20FAQ%20DE.pdf)
- In a NOV, worldwide income and assets must be declared. — [BE Info NOV 2022](https://www.sv.fin.be.ch/content/dam/sv_fin/dokumente/de/info_10minuten/info-nov_2022_de.pdf)
- Capital benefits from 2nd pillar and pillar 3a paid to non-residents are always subject to source tax at a special rate; if no DBA, the withholding is final; if a DBA assigns taxation to the residence state, the tax can be reclaimed (e.g., USA, most European states). Some DBAs (examples cited: Denmark, UK, Iceland, Canada, Sweden, South Africa) do not allow reclaiming for public-law/pension-fund benefits in certain cases. — [swissinfo on Pensionskasse when emigrating](https://www.swissinfo.ch/ger/wirtschaft/auslandschweizer_auswandern-und-pensionskasse-so-spart-man-dabei-steuern/43670970); [AG Merkblatt privatrechtliche Vorsorgeleistungen 2026](https://www.ag.ch/media/kanton-aargau/dfr/dokumente/steuern/quellensteuer/quellensteuer-arbeitnehmende/qust-merkblaetter/qust-ffrecht-vorsorgeleistungen-2026.pdf)
- Withdrawal should be requested only after moving abroad so the benefit falls under source taxation, not ordinary taxation. — [swissinfo](https://www.swissinfo.ch/ger/wirtschaft/auslandschweizer_auswandern-und-pensionskasse-so-spart-man-dabei-steuern/43670970)

### Inferences
- The list of countries where refund is excluded should be checked per DBA and per type of benefit (public-law vs private pension), since the swissinfo article is older (2018).

### Gaps
- ESTV DBA list, Switzerland's AEOI start dates (data collection 2017, first exchange 2018) and partner list — not fetched; standard knowledge but unsourced here.
- Taxation of foreign pensions (AHV equivalents, foreign occupational pensions) — no primary source retrieved.

## 5. Moving to/from Switzerland mid-year

### Takeaway
Arrival or departure during the year creates partial-year liability (unterjährige Steuerpflicht): only income earned while resident is taxed, but it is annualised to determine the rate; assets are valued at the end of liability. Leaving requires deregistration (Abmeldung), a final return, often a Swiss tax representative, and source tax on pension capital paid after departure.

### Cited Findings
- Taxable only income from 1 Jan until departure; annualised for the rate (Umrechnung auf Jahreseinkommen); wealth valued at departure date. — [swissinfo, Steuern beim Auswandern (27 May 2026)](https://www.swissinfo.ch/ger/swiss-abroad/steuern-beim-auswandern-aus-der-schweiz-das-sollten-sie-wissen/88054351); [BE Merkblatt Wohnsitzwechsel](https://www.sv.fin.be.ch/content/dam/sv_fin/dokumente/de/merkblaetter/einkommens_vermoegenssteuer/2021/mb01_ev_wohnsitzwechsel_2021_de.pdf); [GR Merkblatt unterjährige Steuerpflicht 2023](https://www.gr.ch/DE/institutionen/verwaltung/dfg/stv/steuererklaerung/einkommens_und_vermoegenssteuer/WegleitungenMerkbltterTarife/Merkblatt%202023%20unterj%c3%a4hrige%20Steuerpflicht.pdf)
- A Swiss-resident authorised tax representative is mandatory if the assessment cannot be completed before departure or limited tax liability continues (e.g., Swiss property). — [BE Merkblatt Wohnsitzwechsel](https://www.sv.fin.be.ch/content/dam/sv_fin/dokumente/de/merkblaetter/einkommens_vermoegenssteuer/2021/mb01_ev_wohnsitzwechsel_2021_de.pdf)
- Contact the cantonal tax office ≥ 30 days before departure; some cantons demand payment before leaving, others (e.g., Bern) accept payment from abroad. Limited liability continues for Swiss real estate, annuities, self-employment in CH. — [swissinfo (27 May 2026)](https://www.swissinfo.ch/ger/swiss-abroad/steuern-beim-auswandern-aus-der-schweiz-das-sollten-sie-wissen/88054351)
- For source-taxed persons leaving: the deadline for a NOV application ends at deregistration. — [BE Info NOV 2022](https://www.sv.fin.be.ch/content/dam/sv_fin/dokumente/de/info_10minuten/info-nov_2022_de.pdf)

### Inferences
- Source-taxed persons without NOV generally have no final return; the withholding is final, but they lose the chance to claim deductions if they don't apply before Abmeldung.

### Gaps
- Restriction on cash withdrawal of the mandatory BVG portion when moving to EU/EFTA states (since 2007) — not sourced here.

## 6. Expatriate deductions (ExpaV) and lump-sum taxation (Pauschalbesteuerung / Aufwandbesteuerung / imposition d'après la dépense)

### Takeaway
Revised ExpaV (in force 1 Jan 2016) allows qualifying expatriates (temporary assignment ≤ 5 years) to deduct moving costs, housing costs (only if a foreign home is kept), and foreign-language private school fees. Lump-sum taxation exists for foreigners without Swiss gainful activity; federal minimum CHF 434,700 since 2025 (≥ 7× rent); abolished in Zurich, Schaffhausen, Appenzell Ausserrhoden, Basel-Landschaft and Basel-Stadt.

### Cited Findings
- ExpaV revised by EFD, in force 1 Jan 2016; narrower definition of expatriates; temporary assignment max. 5 years; housing deductible only if residence abroad permanently kept for own use; moving costs only if directly related; school fees for minor foreign-language children at foreign-language private school only if public schools don't teach in their language (no meals, transport, care). — [iww](https://www.iww.de/pistb/steuerrecht-aktuell/schweiz-revidierte-bestimmungen-ueber-steuerabzuege-fuer-expatriates-gelten-ab-anfang-2016-n82534); [LU SteuerPraxis 2015/12](https://steuern.lu.ch/-/media/Steuern/Dokumente/SteuerBulletin/SteuerPraxis201512SteuerabzuegefuerExpatriates.pdf)
- Aufwandbesteuerung DBG minimum: CHF 434,700 from 1 Jan 2025 (inflation adjustment). — [ESTV Wegleitung DBST 2025](https://www.estv.admin.ch/dam/de/sd-web/vNMjUsQXp1ZL/dbst-wgl-03a-2025-de.pdf); [SO newsletter 01/2025](https://so.ch/verwaltung/finanzdepartement/steueramt/informationen/newsletter/newsletter-01-2025/info-natuerliche-personen/)
- Conditions: no Swiss citizenship, unlimited liability for the first time or after ≥10 years' absence, no gainful activity in Switzerland; cantons set their own minima and wealth-tax component. Since 2016 the minimum expense basis is 7× rent / imputed rent (previously 5×); the original federal minimum was CHF 400,000. — [SO newsletter 01/2025](https://so.ch/verwaltung/finanzdepartement/steueramt/informationen/newsletter/newsletter-01-2025/info-natuerliche-personen/); [Grant Thornton](https://www.grantthornton.ch/globalassets/1.-member-firms/switzerland/insights/pdf/2019/201901/pauschalbesteuerung_in_der_schweiz_de.pdf)
- Abolished in: Zurich (first, 2009 ballot), Schaffhausen, Appenzell Ausserrhoden, Basel-Landschaft, Basel-Stadt. Federal initiative to abolish rejected on 30 Nov 2014. — [SRF](https://www.srf.ch/news/schweiz/pauschalbesteuerung-steuerprivileg-fuer-reiche-auslaender-bleibt); [swissinfo](https://www.swissinfo.ch/ger/politik/abstimmung-30-november-pauschalbesteuerung/41136868)

### Inferences
- Lump-sum taxation is not open to anyone employed in Switzerland, so it is irrelevant for typical expats on permit B.
