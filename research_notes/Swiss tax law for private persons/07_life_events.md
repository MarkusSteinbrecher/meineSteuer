# Life events ("Lebenslagen") and their tax consequences for private persons in Switzerland (as of October 2026)

Scope: natural persons, Swiss residents. DBG = Bundesgesetz über die direkte Bundessteuer (Federal Direct Tax Act, SR 642.11); StHG = Steuerharmonisierungsgesetz (Tax Harmonisation Act); StG = cantonal tax law. "DBSt" = direkte Bundessteuer (direct federal tax). Cantonal amounts differ a lot; federal figures are given where available.

Research method note: ~25 tool calls. Primary sources reached: ESTV (federal tax administration) source-tax deduction table 2026, ESTV/EFD press release on cold progression (Sept 2025), cantonal Merkblätter (fact sheets) from Aargau, Bern, Schwyz, a Zurich-canton municipality (Bassersdorf), Thurgau Steuerpraxis. Several cantonal sites (baselland.ch, winterthur.ch PDF) blocked automated fetching (HTTP 403 / HTML instead of PDF). fedlex.admin.ch was not fetched (JS-rendered); DBG articles are cited via the cantonal/ESTV documents that quote them.

## 0. Cross-cutting: pending reforms and 2026 federal reference figures

### Takeaway
Two big reforms are decided but not yet in force: Eigenmietwert (imputed rental value) abolition from 1 Jan 2029, and Individualbesteuerung (individual taxation) from 1 Jan 2032 at the latest. For tax year 2026 the old system applies in full. Federal deduction amounts for 2026 are essentially unchanged from 2025 (inflation 0.1%), except the car commuting rate.

### Cited Findings
- **Eigenmietwert abolition**: accepted by popular vote on 28 Sept 2025; the Federal Council decided on 1 April 2026 to put it into force on **1 January 2029**. Until end of 2028 the imputed rental value stays taxable and mortgage-interest and maintenance deductions remain allowed. The Federal Council said 2028 would have been theoretically possible, but the link to the new cantonal property tax on second homes required a later date — [Bilanz](https://www.bilanz.ch/invest/immobilien/bundesrat-eigenmietwert-faellt-ab-1-januar-2029-weg/zdkh62c); [Blick](https://www.blick.ch/politik/kein-bundesrat-wehrte-sich-darum-kommt-eigenmietwert-aus-erst-2029-id21950121.html)
- **Content of the system change (Systemwechsel Wohneigentumsbesteuerung)**: Eigenmietwert dropped for *all* owner-occupied property (primary and secondary homes); maintenance costs (Unterhaltskosten) for owner-occupied property no longer deductible (exception: heritage-protection work, "denkmalpflegerische Arbeiten"); cantons may allow time-limited deductions for energy-saving and environmental measures until 2050; no more debt-interest deduction for owner-occupied property, except **Ersterwerberabzug** (first-time buyer deduction): up to CHF 10,000 (married) / CHF 5,000 (single) interest in the first year after acquisition, reducing linearly by 10% per year over 10 years; not for second homes. Cantons may levy a special property tax on second homes (Liegenschaftssteuer auf Zweitwohnungen). Wealth tax on property unchanged — [ZKB fact sheet, Oct 2025](https://www.zkb.ch/media/zkb/dokumente/merkblaetter/FS_Systemwechsel_Wohneigentumsbesteuerung_09-25%20(1).pdf)
- After the change, private debt interest (mortgage, Lombard, private loans) is deductible only in proportion of rented real estate to total assets ("quotal restriktive Methode"). ZKB example: total assets CHF 5m, rented property CHF 1.5m (30%) → only 30% of CHF 20,000 interest = CHF 6,000 deductible. Maintenance on rented property stays deductible — [ZKB fact sheet](https://www.zkb.ch/media/zkb/dokumente/merkblaetter/FS_Systemwechsel_Wohneigentumsbesteuerung_09-25%20(1).pdf)
- ZKB practical tip: bring forward planned renovations before the change; for condominium owners (Stockwerkeigentum) consider higher contributions to the renewal fund (Erneuerungsfonds); rethink mortgage level — [ZKB fact sheet](https://www.zkb.ch/media/zkb/dokumente/merkblaetter/FS_Systemwechsel_Wohneigentumsbesteuerung_09-25%20(1).pdf)
- **Individualbesteuerung** (individual taxation): accepted on **8 March 2026** with 54.3% yes, after a referendum (65,672 signatures; opponents incl. Mitte, SVP, EVP, EDU, Bauernverband) — [summary via search of bs.ch / gfs.bern VOX](https://vox.gfsbern.ch/wp-content/uploads/2026/04/de_zusammenfassung_vox_maerz_2026_def.pdf); [Blick](https://www.blick.ch/politik/abstimmung-wohl-im-maerz-2026-allianz-reicht-referendum-gegen-individualbesteuerung-ein-id21308116.html)
- The Federal Council decided on **19 August 2026** to put individual taxation into force on **1 January 2032**, the latest date permitted by law, to give cantons time (cantons must adapt tariffs and social deductions; cantonal referendums possible) — [admin.ch press release](https://www.admin.ch/de/newnsb/khPH1Sn08Zr6iGZYe4tsB); [Südostschweiz](https://www.suedostschweiz.ch/schweiz-und-welt/individualbesteuerung-kommt-zum-letztmoeglichen-termin-2127205)
- Under individual taxation: joint taxation of spouses abolished, one federal tariff only, child-related deductions generally split equally between parents with joint custody; federal child deduction to rise from CHF 6,800 to CHF 12,000 per child (in the new law, not yet in force) — [Bär & Karrer briefing, 8 March 2026](https://www.baerkarrer.ch/userdata/files/publications/briefing-individualbesteuerung-variante-annahme-final-20260308.pdf)
- **ESTV 2026 federal deduction parameters** (from the source-tax calculation basis, which reproduces DBG deductions): Berufskosten flat rate 3% of net salary, min CHF 2,000 / max CHF 4,000 (Art. 26 Abs. 1 lit. c DBG); meals away from home CHF 3,200; insurance premiums/savings interest single max CHF 1,800, married CHF 3,700, per child CHF 700 (Art. 33 Abs. 1 lit. g / 1bis lit. b DBG); **Zweiverdienerabzug** (dual-earner deduction) 50% of the lower income, min CHF 8,600 / max CHF 14,100 (Art. 33 Abs. 2 DBG); **Kinderabzug** CHF 6,800 per child (Art. 35 Abs. 1 lit. a DBG); Ehepaarabzug (married couple deduction) CHF 2,800 (Art. 35 Abs. 1 lit. c DBG); **Elterntarif** reduction of CHF 263 per child from the tax amount (Art. 36 Abs. 2bis DBG) — [ESTV, QSt-Berechnungsgrundlagen 2026](https://www.estv.admin.ch/dam/de/sd-web/nuQbhdzXDPm0/qst-berechnungsgrundlagen-2026-de.pdf)
- EFD press release (11 Sept 2025): for tax year 2026, inflation since last adjustment was 0.1%; "Bei den Abzügen ändert sich aufgrund der geringen Teuerung nichts", except car commuting rate raised to CHF 0.75/km (was 0.70); fire-brigade pay exemption CHF 5,400 — [ESTV/EFD](https://www.estv.admin.ch/de/newnsb/VzaAUrhkPx2EPde4a6e3O)

### Inferences
- For an educational site in late 2026, every Lebenslage page should have a "what changes in 2029 (Eigenmietwert) / 2032 (Individualbesteuerung)" box; tax years 2026–2028 still use the old housing regime, 2026–2031 still use joint taxation of spouses.

### Gaps
- Exact 2026 federal ceilings for Kinderdrittbetreuungskosten, Weiterbildungskosten and Unterstützungsabzug were not confirmed from a primary 2026 table (statutory base is CHF 25,000 for childcare, see section 3; indexed amount for 2026 not verified). Use ESTV's "Steuerbelastung/Abzüge" table or DBG Art. 33 directly before publishing.

## 1. First job, students, apprentices (Erste Steuererklärung, Lernende, Studierende)

### Takeaway
Own tax liability starts with the tax year in which a person turns 18; a return must be filed even if income is below the tax-free threshold. Until then, a minor's work income is taxed separately, other income is added to the parents'. Public scholarships are tax-free.

### Cited Findings
- Full own tax liability starts from the beginning of the year in which you turn 18; a tax return must be filed in every case, even if income is below the tax-free minimum (on average around CHF 12,000/year, varies by canton) — [Beobachter](https://www.beobachter.ch/geld/steuern/die-erste-steuererklarung-so-klappt-es-ganz-einfach-362876); [Blick](https://www.blick.ch/wirtschaft/endlich-volljaehrig-die-erste-steuererklaerung-so-klappt-es-ganz-einfach-id21765280.html)
- Scholarships (Stipendien) from public sources are treated as public support payments and are tax-free — [Beobachter](https://www.beobachter.ch/geld/steuern/die-erste-steuererklarung-so-klappt-es-ganz-einfach-362876)
- Parents keep the child deduction for adult children in training: federal CHF 6,800 per adult child in education whose maintenance the taxpayer provides (Art. 35 Abs. 1 lit. a DBG); Schwyz e.g. CHF 11,000 per adult child in training up to age 28 if parents bear the main cost of maintenance — [Schwyz Merkblatt Unterhaltsbeiträge und Kinderabzüge](https://www.sz.ch/public/upload/assets/17833/Merkblatt_Steuerliche_Behandlung_von_Unterhaltsbeitraegen_Alimentenzahlungen_und_damit_zusammenhaengende_Sozialabzuege.pdf?fp=4)
- Payments by parents to adult children for livelihood and training are tax-free for the child (not income) — [Basel-Landschaft Steuerbuch, Band 1, 29/12 (search summary)](https://kanton.baselland.ch/finanz-und-kirchendirektion/steuerverwaltung-steuerbuch/band-1/einkommen/downloads-1/band1_029_12.pdf)
- Some cantons apply a practical age limit around 25 for the child deduction for adult children in training — [Zug Steuerbuch, Kinderabzug (search summary)](https://zgchtest.webcloud7.ch/izug/platform/behoerden/finanzdirektion/steuerverwaltung/steuerbuch-zug/erlaeuterung-zu-a733-sozialabzuege/kinderabzug/export_pdf); Schwyz uses 28 — [SZ Merkblatt](https://www.sz.ch/public/upload/assets/17833/Merkblatt_Steuerliche_Behandlung_von_Unterhaltsbeitraegen_Alimentenzahlungen_und_damit_zusammenhaengende_Sozialabzuege.pdf?fp=4)
- Self-employed side-income up to CHF 2,300/year: AHV contributions only on request (relevant for students with small freelance gigs) — [Solothurn press release, Sept 2024](https://so.ch/verwaltung/staatskanzlei/medien/medienarchiv-2024/september/september-2024/news/ahv-beitraege-ja-zur-erhebung-bei-geringfuegigen-einkommen/)

### Inferences
- First return is filed in the spring after the 18th birthday year. Practical tips for the site: keep Lohnausweis (salary certificate) of every part-time job, claim Berufskosten (commuting, meals) and 3a only if there is earned income; even with zero tax, filing allows refund of Verrechnungssteuer (35% withholding tax) on bank interest.
- Since 2016 the federal law distinguishes deductible Aus- und Weiterbildungskosten (professional training costs after a first secondary-level qualification, Art. 33 Abs. 1 lit. j DBG) from non-deductible first education; first apprenticeship / first degree costs are generally not deductible.

### Gaps
- Federal maximum for Aus-/Weiterbildungskosten (statutory CHF 12,000, indexed; often quoted ~CHF 13,000) not confirmed from a 2026 primary source.
- Taxation of minors' income (own Erwerbseinkommen separately taxed under 18; other income added to parent) not re-verified in this session; rule is in Art. 9 Abs. 2 DBG.

## 2. Marriage, registered partnership, Heiratsstrafe, concubinage

### Takeaway
Spouses (and registered partners) are taxed jointly for the *whole* year of marriage, regardless of wedding date. At federal level, high-income dual earners and pensioner couples can pay more than unmarried couples ("Heiratsstrafe"); partial remedies are the married tariff, Zweiverdienerabzug and Ehepaarabzug. Individual taxation (from 2032) will remove it. Unmarried couples file separately; child deductions/Elterntarif are allocated by a set of rules from ESTV Kreisschreiben 30.

### Cited Findings
- On marriage, both partners become jointly taxable retroactively from the beginning of the year of marriage; separate provisional tax bills already issued are cancelled and replaced by a joint provisional bill, payments are transferred — [Gemeinde Bassersdorf (ZH), Zivilstandsänderung](https://www.bassersdorf.ch/politik-verwaltung/verwaltung/abteilungen/finanzen-liegenschaften/steuern/zivilstandsaenderung.html/278/print/pdf)
- Statutory rule (cantonal, mirroring Art. 18 StHG / federal practice): "Bei Heirat werden beide Eheteile für die ganze Steuerperiode gemeinsam besteuert" — [Aargau Merkblatt Kapitalleistungen, Stand 1 Aug 2026, § 61 StG](https://www.ag.ch/media/kanton-aargau/dfr/dokumente/steuern/natuerliche-personen/merkblaetter-zu-steuern-natuerliche-personen/kapitalleistungen-zeitliche-bemessung-8-2026.pdf)
- Registered partners are treated like spouses (§ 9a StG SZ / Art. 9 Abs. 1bis DBG) — [SZ Merkblatt](https://www.sz.ch/public/upload/assets/17833/Merkblatt_Steuerliche_Behandlung_von_Unterhaltsbeitraegen_Alimentenzahlungen_und_damit_zusammenhaengende_Sozialabzuege.pdf?fp=4)
- Federal Supreme Court ruling of 13 April 1984: married couples may not be taxed more heavily than unmarried couples in the same economic situation; a marriage penalty exists when married couples pay ≥10% more federal tax than comparable unmarried couples. Federal tariff system has three tariffs: Grundtarif, Verheiratetentarif, Elterntarif (married tariff + reduction per child) — [Federal Council message, newsd.admin.ch](https://www.newsd.admin.ch/newsd/message/attachments/27803.pdf)
- Around 80,000 dual-income married couples with higher incomes and numerous pensioner couples with medium/higher incomes are still more heavily burdened than unmarried couples for the direct federal tax — [Federal Council message](https://www.newsd.admin.ch/newsd/message/attachments/27803.pdf)
- Partial remedies 2026: Zweiverdienerabzug 50% of lower income, min 8,600, max 14,100; Ehepaarabzug CHF 2,800; married insurance deduction CHF 3,700 — [ESTV 2026](https://www.estv.admin.ch/dam/de/sd-web/nuQbhdzXDPm0/qst-berechnungsgrundlagen-2026-de.pdf)
- Same-sex marriage (since 2022) has the same tax effects as heterosexual marriage — [VZ VermögensZentrum](https://www.vermoegenszentrum.ch/wissen/gleichgeschlechtliche-ehe-der-schweiz-das-aendert-sich-mit-der-heirat)
- **Concubinage with children (KS 30, ESTV)**: unmarried parents living together with a joint minor child and joint custody, *without* maintenance payments → the parent with the higher income generally gets the Elterntarif; the child deduction is split half-half. If maintenance payments are made → payer deducts them, recipient gets child deduction and Elterntarif. Unmarried parents in a common household with joint custody generally cannot deduct maintenance payments for federal tax — [search summary of ESTV Kreisschreiben Nr. 30 Ziff. 14.8.2 and cantonal practice, e.g. GR Praxis 039-03-01](https://www.gr.ch/DE/institutionen/verwaltung/dfg/stv/dokumentation/praxis/PraxisEinkommenVermgen/039-03-01.pdf); [SG Steuerbuch 048_2](https://www.sg.ch/content/dam/sgch/steuern-finanzen/steuern/steuerbuch/art-29-52-stg/048_2.pdf.ocFile/048_2.pdf)
- Schwyz accepts a KESB (child protection authority) decision as evidence when unmarried parents living together with joint children claim deductions for child alimony — [SZ Merkblatt Rz. 7](https://www.sz.ch/public/upload/assets/17833/Merkblatt_Steuerliche_Behandlung_von_Unterhaltsbeitraegen_Alimentenzahlungen_und_damit_zusammenhaengende_Sozialabzuege.pdf?fp=4)

### Inferences
- Note the conflict: the search summary says cohabiting parents with joint custody cannot deduct alimony federally, while Schwyz (cantonal) admits KESB-backed alimony between cohabiting parents. Practice differs by canton; verify against KS 30 text before publishing.
- Practical tip: marriage late in the year still triggers joint taxation for the whole year; couples often ask whether marrying in January vs December matters → it does not change the rule, only which tax year is first joint.

### Gaps
- Direct text of ESTV Kreisschreiben Nr. 30 (Ehepaar- und Familienbesteuerung) not fetched; the concubinage rules come from search summaries of cantonal documents quoting it.

## 3. Children: birth, Kinderabzug, childcare, adult children, child alimony

### Takeaway
A child born at any point in the year counts for the full-year child deduction (situation at end of tax period). Federal: CHF 6,800 per child plus CHF 263 off the tax via Elterntarif, plus CHF 700 extra insurance deduction; third-party childcare costs deductible up to CHF 25,000 (statute) for children under 14. Child alimony is deductible by the payer and taxable for the recipient only while the child is a minor.

### Cited Findings
- Federal child deduction CHF 6,800 per minor child, and per adult child in training, if the taxpayer provides for its maintenance; Elterntarif reduction CHF 263 per child; insurance deduction +CHF 700 per child — [ESTV 2026](https://www.estv.admin.ch/dam/de/sd-web/nuQbhdzXDPm0/qst-berechnungsgrundlagen-2026-de.pdf); [SZ Merkblatt](https://www.sz.ch/public/upload/assets/17833/Merkblatt_Steuerliche_Behandlung_von_Unterhaltsbeitraegen_Alimentenzahlungen_und_damit_zusammenhaengende_Sozialabzuege.pdf?fp=4)
- Cantonal example (Schwyz): CHF 9,000 per minor child, CHF 11,000 per adult child in training up to 28 — [SZ Merkblatt](https://www.sz.ch/public/upload/assets/17833/Merkblatt_Steuerliche_Behandlung_von_Unterhaltsbeitraegen_Alimentenzahlungen_und_damit_zusammenhaengende_Sozialabzuege.pdf?fp=4)
- Third-party childcare (Kinderdrittbetreuungskosten): DBG allows documented costs up to CHF 25,000 per child under 14 living in the same household, if in direct causal connection with the taxpayer's employment, training or inability to work — [search summary of SP parliamentary document 17.344 / cantonal practice](https://staging2.sp-ps.ch/wp-content/uploads/2022/06/17-344_steuerliche_berucksichtigung_der_kinderdrittbetreuungskosten.pdf); [GR Praxis 036-l-01](https://www.gr.ch/DE/institutionen/verwaltung/dfg/stv/dokumentation/praxis/PraxisEinkommenVermgen/036-l-01.pdf)
- Child alimony is taxable for the receiving parent and deductible for the paying parent (Art. 23 lit. f and Art. 33 Abs. 1 lit. c DBG) only for minor children; payments to adult children (after 18) fall under other family support duties and are **neither taxable nor deductible**. Only payments to the other parent count — not pocket money or direct payment of school fees, holidays, etc. Payments must be legally owed (agreement or court ruling) and actually paid; correspondence principle (Korrespondenzprinzip): what one deducts, the other taxes — [SZ Merkblatt Rz. 2–11](https://www.sz.ch/public/upload/assets/17833/Merkblatt_Steuerliche_Behandlung_von_Unterhaltsbeitraegen_Alimentenzahlungen_und_damit_zusammenhaengende_Sozialabzuege.pdf?fp=4)
- For adult children either a child deduction (if in training) or a support deduction (Unterstützungsabzug) may apply — [SG VWEK decision via entscheidsuche](https://entscheidsuche.ch/docs/SG_Gerichte/SG_VWEK_001_I-1-2009-101_2010-06-29.pdf)
- Maternity allowance (EO Mutterschaftsentschädigung) = 80% of average gross income before birth, max CHF 220/day — [BSV FAQ](https://faq.bsv.admin.ch/de/erwerbsersatz-eo/wie-hoch-ist-die-mutterschaftsentschaedigung); replacement income is fully taxable (see section 7).

### Inferences
- Tip: the payer of alimony for a child who turns 18 mid-year loses the deduction from that point (per Rz. 9 logic); the recipient no longer taxes it. Site should show a timeline "child turns 18".

### Gaps
- Indexed 2026 federal childcare cap (statute 25,000; I believe indexed to 25,500 since 2023 but could not confirm) — verify.
- Proration of the child deduction in the birth year: not verified from a primary source (general rule: social deductions by situation at end of period, Art. 35 Abs. 2 DBG).

## 4. Separation and divorce (Trennung, Scheidung)

### Takeaway
Separation or divorce in any month leads to separate taxation for the *whole* year. Ongoing alimony (Renten) is deductible/taxable; lump-sum capital settlements (Kapitalabfindung) are neither.

### Cited Findings
- "Bei Scheidung und bei rechtlicher oder tatsächlicher Trennung werden beide Eheteile für die ganze Steuerperiode getrennt besteuert" (cantonal § 61 Abs. 2 StG AG; same at federal level) — [AG Merkblatt](https://www.ag.ch/media/kanton-aargau/dfr/dokumente/steuern/natuerliche-personen/merkblaetter-zu-steuern-natuerliche-personen/kapitalleistungen-zeitliche-bemessung-8-2026.pdf)
- Practice: both become taxable as individuals retroactively from 1 January; provisional joint payments are split as both spouses instruct within 10 days, otherwise half-half up to the separation date — [Bassersdorf](https://www.bassersdorf.ch/politik-verwaltung/verwaltung/abteilungen/finanzen-liegenschaften/steuern/zivilstandsaenderung.html/278/print/pdf)
- Spousal and child alimony paid as periodic payments: taxable for recipient, deductible for payer; payments before separation/divorce are irrelevant — [SZ Merkblatt](https://www.sz.ch/public/upload/assets/17833/Merkblatt_Steuerliche_Behandlung_von_Unterhaltsbeitraegen_Alimentenzahlungen_und_damit_zusammenhaengende_Sozialabzuege.pdf?fp=4)
- Capital settlement instead of an annuity (Art. 126 ZGB): since 2001 neither taxable for the recipient nor deductible for the payer — [search summary of GR Praxis 036-l-01 / BL Steuerbuch](https://kanton.baselland.ch/finanz-und-kirchendirektion/steuerverwaltung-steuerbuch/band-1/einkommen/downloads-1/band1_024_14.pdf)
- Capital benefits from pension schemes in the year of separation: if paid before and after a civil-status change in the same year, separate annual taxes may be assessed — [AG Merkblatt sec. 3.5/4.5](https://www.ag.ch/media/kanton-aargau/dfr/dokumente/steuern/natuerliche-personen/merkblaetter-zu-steuern-natuerliche-personen/kapitalleistungen-zeitliche-bemessung-8-2026.pdf)

### Inferences
- Tip: the choice between periodic alimony and lump sum has big tax effects (progression); periodic payments shift income to the lower-income ex-spouse.

### Gaps
- Tax treatment of the **Vorsorgeausgleich** (pension splitting under Art. 122 ff. ZGB): commonly tax-neutral transfer between pension funds; not verified from a primary source in this session. Also the allocation of child deduction / Elterntarif after divorce with shared custody (KS 30) only from summaries.

## 5. Buying and selling a home

### Takeaway
Until end of 2028: Eigenmietwert taxed as income, mortgage interest and maintenance deductible. From 2029: no Eigenmietwert, no maintenance deduction, limited first-buyer interest deduction. Pension money (WEF) can finance the purchase; withdrawal is taxed separately at reduced rate. Sale profits are subject to cantonal Grundstückgewinnsteuer, falling with holding period; reinvestment in a new main residence defers it.

### Cited Findings
- Current system: owner-occupied property → Eigenmietwert taxed as income (based on achievable rent, in practice somewhat lower); in return debt interest and value-preserving maintenance (incl. energy-saving and environmental measures) deductible — [ZKB](https://www.zkb.ch/media/zkb/dokumente/merkblaetter/FS_Systemwechsel_Wohneigentumsbesteuerung_09-25%20(1).pdf)
- From 1 Jan 2029: see section 0 (Ersterwerberabzug CHF 10,000/5,000 declining over 10 years, no maintenance deduction, cantonal energy deductions possible until 2050) — [ZKB](https://www.zkb.ch/media/zkb/dokumente/merkblaetter/FS_Systemwechsel_Wohneigentumsbesteuerung_09-25%20(1).pdf); [Bilanz](https://www.bilanz.ch/invest/immobilien/bundesrat-eigenmietwert-faellt-ab-1-januar-2029-weg/zdkh62c)
- WEF (Wohneigentumsförderung) withdrawals from 2nd pillar and 3a are capital benefits taxed separately; at federal level capital benefits are taxed at one-fifth of the ordinary tariff, social deductions not granted (Art. 38 DBG); taxable in the canton of residence at the time of payment — [AG Merkblatt](https://www.ag.ch/media/kanton-aargau/dfr/dokumente/steuern/natuerliche-personen/merkblaetter-zu-steuern-natuerliche-personen/kapitalleistungen-zeitliche-bemessung-8-2026.pdf)
- Grundstückgewinnsteuer: rate depends on holding period from last taxable transfer; nearly all cantons reduce the tax for long holding periods, maximum reduction typically after 20–35 years, 50–70% and sometimes up to 90% — [VZ VermögensZentrum](https://www.vermoegenszentrum.ch/wissen/grundstuckgewinnsteuern)
- Ersatzbeschaffung (replacement purchase): if proceeds are reinvested in a replacement owner-occupied main residence within a set period (usually 2 years), the tax is deferred (Art. 12 Abs. 3 StHG) — [neho.ch](https://neho.ch/de/blog/grundstuckgewinnsteuer-ersatzbeschaffung); WEF advance withdrawal / pledging are also deferral grounds — [forms-legal summary](https://forms-legal.com/de/switzerland/real-estate/purchase-sale/grundstueckgewinnsteuer-aufschub-schweiz)
- Aargau form 112.01 "Grundstückgewinne 2026" exists as the declaration form for property gains — [AG form](https://www.ag.ch/media/kanton-aargau/dfr/dokumente/steuern/natuerliche-personen/steuerformulare-natuerliche-personen/112-01-grundst-ckgewinne-2026.pdf)

### Inferences
- Renovation planning window: maintenance costs incurred in 2026–2028 are still deductible; after 2029 only rented property and (cantonally) energy measures. Big renovations could be scheduled before 2029 (ZKB tip).
- On sale of a home financed with WEF money, the WEF withdrawal must normally be repaid to the pension fund and the tax paid at withdrawal can be reclaimed (interest-free) — standard rule, not verified this session.

### Gaps
- Pauschalabzug (flat maintenance deduction) rates (federal 10%/20% of Eigenmietwert depending on building age) not verified.
- Repayment of WEF and refund of tax (Art. 83a BVG) not verified from primary source.

## 6. Self-employment and side jobs (Selbständigkeit, Nebenerwerb)

### Takeaway
Self-employed income is declared via a profit-and-loss statement (Erfolgsrechnung) attached to the return; AHV/IV/EO contributions are paid directly to the compensation office and are deductible. Side income up to CHF 2,300 per year is AHV-exempt unless requested. Self-employed persons without a pension fund can pay 20% of net income into Säule 3a, max CHF 36,288 (2026).

### Cited Findings
- AHV is mandatory for the self-employed (incl. IV and EO); a self-employed side activity must be registered even if main employment is already AHV-insured — [Swiss Life blog](https://www.swisslife.ch/de/private/blog/nebenberuflich-selbstaendig.html)
- Self-employed side income ≤ CHF 2,300 per calendar year: AHV contributions only on request of the insured person (Geringfügigkeitsgrenze) — [Kanton Solothurn, Sept 2024](https://so.ch/verwaltung/staatskanzlei/medien/medienarchiv-2024/september/september-2024/news/ahv-beitraege-ja-zur-erhebung-bei-geringfuegigen-einkommen/)
- Säule 3a 2026: CHF 7,258 with pension fund; without pension fund ("grosse 3a") 20% of net earned income, max CHF 36,288. For self-employed, net income = balance of the P&L after deducting AHV/IV/EO contributions — [VZ VermögensZentrum](https://www.vermoegenszentrum.ch/wissen/saeule-3a-maximalbetrag); [UBS 2026](https://www.ubs.com/ch/en/help/pension/maximal-contribution-2026.html)
- 3a catch-up (Einkauf / Nachzahlung): possible from 2026 for gaps arising from 2025 onwards, up to 10 years back; earlier gaps cannot be filled; payment in the same year as the application — [search summary incl. UBS / finpension](https://www.ubs.com/ch/en/services/pension/pillar-3/maximal-contribution.html)
- On final cessation of self-employment after age 55 or due to disability, liquidation gains (stille Reserven of the last two years) are taxed separately at reduced rate (Aargau: notional pension buy-in portion at capital-benefit rate, remainder at 40% of tariff; heirs who don't continue the business get the same relief) — [AG Merkblatt § 45 StG](https://www.ag.ch/media/kanton-aargau/dfr/dokumente/steuern/natuerliche-personen/merkblaetter-zu-steuern-natuerliche-personen/kapitalleistungen-zeitliche-bemessung-8-2026.pdf)
- Home office for self-employed (Einzelfirma): deductible as business expense if a dedicated room is used — [pfeffersack.ch](https://pfeffersack.ch/blog/homeoffice-abzug-einzelfirma-schweiz) (secondary source)

### Inferences
- The decision whether someone counts as self-employed is made by the AHV compensation office (criteria like own risk, several clients, own investment, no subordination); tax authorities usually follow it.

### Gaps
- Exact Swiss Life figures for reduced contributions (CHF 10,100 / 5,371 thresholds from search summary) not verified and possibly outdated (2023/2024 values); do not publish without checking the current AHV Merkblatt 2.02 (ahv-iv.ch).
- Bookkeeping requirements (simple accounts of income/expenses and assets under Art. 957 OR for revenue < CHF 500,000) not verified this session.

## 7. Unemployment, parental leave (EO), sickness/accident daily allowances

### Takeaway
All replacement income — unemployment benefits, EO maternity/paternity allowance, IV/sickness/accident daily allowances — is 100% taxable like salary. The paying body issues a certificate for the tax return.

### Cited Findings
- Replacement income such as unemployment benefits and daily allowances from disability, sickness, accident and military insurance are 100% taxable with other income — [Basel-Landschaft, Arbeitslosigkeit/Ersatzeinkünfte](https://www.baselland.ch/politik-und-behorden/direktionen/finanz-und-kirchendirektion/steuerverwaltung/steuererklaerung-natuerliche-personen-und-e-tax-bl/einkuenfte/arbeitslosigkeit-ersatzeinkuenfte) (page blocked to fetch; content from search snippet)
- The unemployment fund (Arbeitslosenkasse) issues an annual certificate of paid daily allowances in Jan/Feb of the following year, used as voucher for the tax return — [iQtax help](https://intercom.help/iqtax/de/articles/11932123-versicherungseinkommen) (secondary)
- EO maternity allowance: 80% of average gross income, max CHF 220/day — [BSV FAQ](https://faq.bsv.admin.ch/de/erwerbsersatz-eo/wie-hoch-ist-die-mutterschaftsentschaedigung)

### Inferences
- Daily allowances paid through the employer appear on the Lohnausweis (salary certificate), those paid directly by the insurer need a separate certificate — avoid double counting. During unemployment, commuting/meal deductions don't apply but job-search costs may.
- Unemployed persons usually can't pay into 3a unless they have AHV-liable income (ALV daily allowances are AHV-liable, so 3a is generally possible).

### Gaps
- Source-tax (Quellensteuer) withholding on ALV benefits for foreign workers not covered.

## 8. Retirement (Pensionierung)

### Takeaway
Annuities (AHV, BVG) are fully taxable income each year; capital withdrawals (BVG, 3a) are taxed once, separately, at a reduced rate (federal: one-fifth of the tariff), all capital benefits of the same year (and of spouses) being added together. Staggering withdrawals over several years and between spouses breaks progression. Afterwards the capital is subject to wealth tax and its returns to income tax.

### Cited Findings
- Art. 38 DBG: capital benefits from pension schemes and payments on death or for permanent disability are taxed separately with a full annual tax, at one-fifth of the ordinary tariff; no social deductions; taxed in the year of payment — [AG Merkblatt quoting Art. 38 DBG](https://www.ag.ch/media/kanton-aargau/dfr/dokumente/steuern/natuerliche-personen/merkblaetter-zu-steuern-natuerliche-personen/kapitalleistungen-zeitliche-bemessung-8-2026.pdf)
- All capital benefits paid in the same year to single or jointly taxed persons are added together for the rate — [AG Merkblatt](https://www.ag.ch/media/kanton-aargau/dfr/dokumente/steuern/natuerliche-personen/merkblaetter-zu-steuern-natuerliche-personen/kapitalleistungen-zeitliche-bemessung-8-2026.pdf)
- Cantonal example Aargau: capital benefits taxed at 30% of tariff, minimum rate 1%; special topic: capital withdrawals within 3 years after a pension-fund buy-in (Art. 79b Abs. 3 BVG) — [AG Merkblatt](https://www.ag.ch/media/kanton-aargau/dfr/dokumente/steuern/natuerliche-personen/merkblaetter-zu-steuern-natuerliche-personen/kapitalleistungen-zeitliche-bemessung-8-2026.pdf)
- Annuity is 100% taxable as income; capital is taxed once at a reduced rate, then counts as wealth and its returns as income; withdrawal possible typically up to five years before ordinary AHV age; mixed solutions are common — [comparis](https://www.comparis.ch/altersvorsorge/Pensionskassenrente-Kapitalbezug); [TKB Merkblatt Rente oder Kapital](https://www.tkb.ch/.dam/c1383576-556f-4e69-a686-25063e8eb3cd/ARME0033_B_Merkblatt-Rente-oder-Kapital.pdf)
- Staggering 3a withdrawals: VZ example in Basel — CHF 150,000 in one year costs CHF 9,833 tax vs. about CHF 6,000 when split over three years; 3a contributions possible until 70 if still working — [VZ VermögensZentrum](https://www.vermoegenszentrum.ch/wissen/saeule-3a-maximalbetrag)
- Capital benefits are taxable in the canton of residence at the time of payment (exception to the 31 Dec rule) — [Bern Merkblatt 1 Wohnsitzwechsel](https://www.sv.fin.be.ch/content/dam/sv_fin/dokumente/de/merkblaetter/einkommens_vermoegenssteuer/2021/mb01_ev_wohnsitzwechsel_2021_de.pdf)
- Severance payments with pension character on early retirement are also taxed at the reduced capital-benefit rate (AG § 45 lit. e) — [AG Merkblatt](https://www.ag.ch/media/kanton-aargau/dfr/dokumente/steuern/natuerliche-personen/merkblaetter-zu-steuern-natuerliche-personen/kapitalleistungen-zeitliche-bemessung-8-2026.pdf)

### Inferences
- Vermögensverzehr (spending down capital) is tax-neutral: drawing on wealth is not income, unlike annuity payments — strong argument for capital in low-return scenarios; but wealth tax applies.
- Moving to a low-tax canton *before* the capital payment date changes where the withdrawal is taxed.
- Pensioner couples are one of the groups hit by the federal Heiratsstrafe (two AHV annuities capped at 150% for married couples plus joint progression).

### Gaps
- AHV married couple cap (150%) and the 2026 13th AHV pension (first paid Dec 2026) and its taxability not verified this session.

## 9. Death of a spouse, inheritance

### Takeaway
Death ends joint taxation: a joint return covers 1 Jan to date of death; the survivor is taxed separately from the following day. Heirs succeed into the deceased's tax obligations and are jointly and severally liable up to their share of the estate. Inheritance itself is subject to cantonal inheritance tax (spouses are exempt in all cantons — not verified here), not income tax.

### Cited Findings
- "Bei Tod eines Eheteils werden beide bis zum Todestag gemeinsam besteuert. Der Tod gilt als Beendigung der gemeinsamen Steuerpflicht und als Beginn der Steuerpflicht des überlebenden Eheteils" (§ 61 Abs. 3 StG AG; federal: survivor assessed separately for the rest of the period at the tariff applicable to them, Art. 18 StHG) — [AG Merkblatt](https://www.ag.ch/media/kanton-aargau/dfr/dokumente/steuern/natuerliche-personen/merkblaetter-zu-steuern-natuerliche-personen/kapitalleistungen-zeitliche-bemessung-8-2026.pdf)
- Practice: a joint return for 1 Jan to day of death; for the rest of the year the survivor receives a questionnaire for provisional billing — [Bassersdorf](https://www.bassersdorf.ch/politik-verwaltung/verwaltung/abteilungen/finanzen-liegenschaften/steuern/zivilstandsaenderung.html/278/print/pdf)
- Capital benefits partly before and partly after the death of a spouse in the same year → two separate annual taxes — [AG Merkblatt § 31 StGV](https://www.ag.ch/media/kanton-aargau/dfr/dokumente/steuern/natuerliche-personen/merkblaetter-zu-steuern-natuerliche-personen/kapitalleistungen-zeitliche-bemessung-8-2026.pdf)
- Heirs are jointly and severally liable for the deceased's taxes up to the amount of their share of the estate, including advance gifts (Vorempfänge), values at date of death (Art. 12 DBG); this limit also applies when the estate is accepted under public inventory (öffentliches Inventar) — [Thurgau Steuerpraxis StP 15 Nr. 1 Steuernachfolge, Sept 2026](https://steuerpraxis.tg.ch/steuerpraxis/2026-09/stp-15-nr-1-steuernachfolge-steuersukzession)
- Capital payments on death/disability (not 2nd pillar / 3a), where premiums were paid by the taxpayer or relatives: Aargau grants a CHF 200,000 exemption per event — [AG Merkblatt § 45 Abs. 4](https://www.ag.ch/media/kanton-aargau/dfr/dokumente/steuern/natuerliche-personen/merkblaetter-zu-steuern-natuerliche-personen/kapitalleistungen-zeitliche-bemessung-8-2026.pdf)

### Inferences
- What to do: heirs file the deceased's final return (period up to death), cooperate with the tax inventory (Steuerinventar / Nachlassinventar, mandatory in most cantons after a death; used to detect undeclared assets), and the survivor files his/her own return for the rest of the year with single (or Elterntarif) tariff.

### Gaps
- Mandatory tax inventory (Art. 154 ff. DBG, cantonal Inventarisation) and simplified "straflose Nachbesteuerung für Erben" (Art. 153a DBG) not verified this session.
- Cantonal inheritance tax rates/exemptions (spouses, descendants) not covered — likely in another research file.

## 10. Moving within Switzerland (Wohnsitzwechsel)

### Takeaway
Whoever moves between communes or cantons within Switzerland is taxed for the whole year where they live on 31 December; no pro-rata split. The former canton refunds instalments. Exceptions: pension capital benefits are taxed where you live on the payment date; real estate is always taxed where it is located.

### Cited Findings
- Moving within Bern or within Switzerland: taxable for the whole year at the place of residence on 31 December; declare income of the whole year and wealth on 31 Dec. If municipal rates differ, instalments are corrected with the final bill. Leaving canton Bern: already-paid instalments are refunded — [Bern Merkblatt 1 Wohnsitzwechsel (ab 2021)](https://www.sv.fin.be.ch/content/dam/sv_fin/dokumente/de/merkblaetter/einkommens_vermoegenssteuer/2021/mb01_ev_wohnsitzwechsel_2021_de.pdf)
- Aargau: on moving to another canton, the end of tax liability is backdated to the beginning of the current period; capital benefits (2nd pillar, 3a, death) remain taxable in AG if residence was there at the due date; same rule applies for moves between AG communes (§ 156 StG) — [AG Merkblatt](https://www.ag.ch/media/kanton-aargau/dfr/dokumente/steuern/natuerliche-personen/merkblaetter-zu-steuern-natuerliche-personen/kapitalleistungen-zeitliche-bemessung-8-2026.pdf)
- Direct federal tax is levied by the canton of residence at the end of the tax period (Art. 105 DBG) — [AG Merkblatt](https://www.ag.ch/media/kanton-aargau/dfr/dokumente/steuern/natuerliche-personen/merkblaetter-zu-steuern-natuerliche-personen/kapitalleistungen-zeitliche-bemessung-8-2026.pdf)
- Contrast — moving to/from abroad: only part-year liability; income annualised for the rate; wealth tax pro rata; flat deductions and most social deductions reduced pro rata (not childcare or external-education deductions); Zuzüger fill in a short declaration (Kurzdeklaration) — [Bern Merkblatt 1](https://www.sv.fin.be.ch/content/dam/sv_fin/dokumente/de/merkblaetter/einkommens_vermoegenssteuer/2021/mb01_ev_wohnsitzwechsel_2021_de.pdf)

### Inferences
- Planning tip: moving from a high-tax to a low-tax canton in December gives the whole year at the low rate (and vice versa — move from low-tax canton in January). The site should warn that "Wohnsitz" means the real centre of life, not just registration.

### Gaps
- Municipal split within some cantons (e.g. Zurich: some cantons split the Gemeindesteuer pro rata between communes for intra-cantonal moves) — not verified; Bern applies the 31 Dec rule also intra-cantonally.

## 11. Home office, remote work, multiple employers, Lohnausweis

### Takeaway
Employees can deduct home office costs only with a dedicated room used mainly for work because the employer provides no workplace; this replaces the Berufskosten flat rate and is rarely worth it. Zurich doesn't allow home office deductions for employees in general. Multiple employers mean multiple Lohnausweise; the flat-rate professional deduction is based on total salary.

### Cited Findings
- Home office deductible only if employer-mandated or with a separate dedicated room, costs borne by the taxpayer, documented; claiming it requires giving up the Berufskostenpauschale (CHF 2,000–4,000), which often makes it unattractive. Cantonal differences: Lucerne and Bern allowed commuting costs during home office (Covid period), St. Gallen and Thurgau reduced commuting/meal deductions proportionally, Zurich allowed no home office deduction (article from Feb 2021, Covid context) — [Beobachter](https://www.beobachter.ch/geld/steuern/wie-geht-homeoffice-bei-den-steuern-334003)
- Zurich: no deduction for the use of a work room; travel/meal/flat-rate professional costs unchanged — [law.ch, Feb 2021](https://law.ch/lawnews/2021/02/coronavirus-covid-19-steuererklaerung-2020-home-office-und-berufskosten/)
- 2026 federal car commuting rate CHF 0.75/km — [ESTV](https://www.estv.admin.ch/de/newnsb/VzaAUrhkPx2EPde4a6e3O)
- Federal Berufskosten flat 3%, min 2,000 / max 4,000; meals CHF 3,200 — [ESTV 2026](https://www.estv.admin.ch/dam/de/sd-web/nuQbhdzXDPm0/qst-berechnungsgrundlagen-2026-de.pdf)

### Inferences
- With regular home office days, cantons tend to reduce commuting and meal deductions; users should record office days.
- Lohnausweis errors (wrong salary, missing fringe benefits) must be corrected by the employer (new certificate); the taxpayer should not simply declare different figures without explanation.

### Gaps
- Federal commuting cap (Fahrkostenabzug max CHF 3,200 since 2016, indexed ~3,300) not verified for 2026.
- Current (post-Covid) official positions on home office for 2026 not found; Beobachter info is from 2021. Multiple-employer and Lohnausweis correction rules (Wegleitung zum Lohnausweis) not fetched.

## 12. Low income, Steuererlass, debts

### Takeaway
Tax remission (Steuererlass) is possible when paying would cause great hardship because of an emergency situation (Notlage); applications must be documented. Payment by instalments (Ratenzahlung) is the usual first step.

### Cited Findings
- Art. 167 DBG: remission requires an emergency (Notlage) and that payment would be a great hardship. Notlage exists when (a) the person's means are insufficient to cover the subsistence minimum under debt-enforcement law (betreibungsrechtliches Existenzminimum), or (b) the total debt is disproportionate to financial capacity — [search summary citing Art. 167 DBG and BL Steuerbuch Band 3, 139/04](https://kanton.baselland.ch/finanz-und-kirchendirektion/steuerverwaltung-steuerbuch/band-3/bezug-abschreibung-und-erlass-von-steuern/downloads-1/band3-139-04.pdf)
- Remission must durably improve the taxpayer's economic situation and benefit the taxpayer, not other creditors; application must contain the necessary evidence — [same, plus AR Merkblatt Steuererlass](https://sozialhilfe.ar.ch/fileadmin/user_upload/sozialhilfe.ar.ch/Mustervorlagen/Steuererlass_Merkblatt_2016.pdf)
- Filing is required even for very low incomes (e.g. students) — [Beobachter](https://www.beobachter.ch/geld/steuern/die-erste-steuererklarung-so-klappt-es-ganz-einfach-362876)

### Inferences
- Persons on social assistance often get remission; an assessment by estimate (Ermessensveranlagung) after not filing can produce too-high taxes — filing is always the first step.

### Gaps
- Deadlines for remission applications, effect of pending debt enforcement, and cantonal practice for Zurich/Bern/Vaud not verified. Debts are deductible from wealth and debt interest from income (Art. 33 Abs. 1 lit. a DBG, cap = investment income + 50,000) — not verified this session.
