# Swiss tax education landscape, reusable open data, and legal constraints for publishing (state: October 2026)

Research date: 2026-10-03. About 30 search/fetch calls. Every claim carries a source. Things that could not be verified are listed under Gaps.

## 1. Official resources (ESTV, SSK, ch.ch, cantons)

### Takeaway
The public sector already offers a lot of good, neutral material: the SSK/ESTV "Dossier Steuerinformationen" (about 40 articles), two civics brochures, the interactive youth site steuern-easy.ch, a talk service for schools, the ESTV tax calculator and cantonal Wegleitungen. All of it is organised by tax type or by administrative logic, not by life events. English coverage is thin, and nothing joins the 26 cantonal guides into one plain-language, comparable view.

### Cited Findings
- The ESTV page "Steuersystem Schweiz" lists the following for the public and for schools: **"Das schweizerische Steuersystem"** (a brochure for civics teaching), **"Leitfaden für zukünftige Steuerpflichtige"** (how income and wealth tax assessment works), **Steuern-easy** (an interactive site that helps young people with the tax return) and a **"Vortragsservice für Schulklassen"** (two lessons of theory and practice for Sekundarstufe II). — [ESTV Steuersystem Schweiz](https://www.estv.admin.ch/de/steuersystem-schweiz)
- The SSK published both brochures for tax education in upper-secondary and vocational schools. They run to about 70 and 90 pages, have been updated over time, and can be downloaded from the ESTV. Completing a tax return is part of federally prescribed curricula, and cantonal tax offices give out forms free of charge for teaching. — search-result summary of a cantonal/SSK document (primary not fetched); see [ESTV Steuersystem Schweiz](https://www.estv.admin.ch/de/steuersystem-schweiz) for the brochures themselves
- **steuern-easy.ch** is made "im Auftrag der Schweizerische Steuerkonferenz SSK" and run by the Kantonales Steueramt Zürich. It offers "Ausfüllen.easy" (guided practice on a tax return), modules on the tax system, quizzes, budgeting tips, a glossary and videos (tax return, 3-pillar system). It uses personas in different life situations (Lea, Alex, Pia, Nico, Laura). Languages: DE/FR/IT. No English version was seen. — [steuern-easy.ch](https://www.steuern-easy.ch/de)
- The **Dossier Steuerinformationen** has about 40 articles in categories A–F (Steuersystem, einzelne Steuern, Steuerbegriffe, steuerliche Herausforderungen, allgemeine Angaben, Aktualitäten). It is aimed at "Steuerfachleuten, öffentlichen Verwaltungen, Schulen, Medien, aber auch Einzelpersonen". DE and FR are confirmed; the site's language switcher also offers IT and EN. Its copyright line reads: "Die Verwendung von Auszügen oder der gesamten Texte ist nur unter Angabe der Quelle gestattet." — [ESTV Steuerinformationen](https://www.estv.admin.ch/de/steuerinformationen)
- The ESTV keeps the dossier up to date topic by topic (for example, Liegenschaftensteuer in 2024, Rechtsmittel in 2023, Erbschafts-/Schenkungssteuer in 2020). — [law.ch news 2024](https://law.ch/lawnews/2024/08/liegenschaftensteuer-dossier-steuerinformationen-wurde-von-estv-aktualisiert/); [law.ch news 2023](https://law.ch/lawnews/2023/08/estv-hat-steuerinformationen-zu-den-rechtsmitteln-gegen-einkommens-und-vermoegenssteuerveranlagungen-aktualisiert/)
- The ESTV also publishes a plain PDF on filling in the tax return ("a-ausfuellen-steuererklaerung-de.pdf"). — [ESTV PDF](https://www.estv2.admin.ch/stp/ds/a-ausfuellen-steuererklaerung-de.pdf)
- The **ESTV Steuerrechner** went live on 12 May 2020. It calculates income/wealth tax, inheritance tax and lump-sum pension payouts for every commune, and it publishes tax-burden statistics and cantonal tax data. Its interface runs in DE/FR/IT/EN/RM. — [admin.ch media release](https://www.admin.ch/gov/en/start/documentation/media-releases.msg-id-79034.html); [ESTV Steuerbelastung](https://www.estv.admin.ch/de/steuerbelastung-in-der-schweiz)
- **ch.ch**: the tax-return page at https://www.ch.ch/de/steuern-und-finanzen/steuererklarung/ appeared in search results, but on 2026-10-03 the fetch returned a 404 page. The portal itself runs in DE/FR/IT/RM/EN and now has an experimental AI search that carries the disclaimer "Ergebnisse können unvollständig oder ungenau sein". — [ch.ch (fetched page)](https://www.ch.ch/de/steuern-und-finanzen/steuererklarung/)
- **Cantonal guidance and e-filing tools**: each canton publishes its own Wegleitung and software. Examples: Graubünden's 2025 Wegleitung ([GR PDF](https://intwww.gr.ch/DE/institutionen/verwaltung/dfg/stv/steuererklaerung/einkommens_und_vermoegenssteuer/WegleitungenMerkbltter/Wegleitung%202025%20Prim%c3%a4rsteuerpflichtige.pdf)); Schwyz's eTax.SZ online solution ([SZ](https://www.sz.ch/finanzdepartement/steuerverwaltung/natuerliche-personen/online-schalter/steuererklaerungs-software.html/8756-8758-8802-10332-10354-10376-10750-10395)); Zurich's new **ZHprivateTax**, which replaces Private Tax from tax period 2025 (reported in search results, [ZH Steuererklärung Privatpersonen](https://www.zh.ch/de/steuern-finanzen/steuern/steuern-natuerliche-personen/steuererklaerung-natuerliche-personen.html)).

### Inferences
- The official material is accurate and neutral, but it is fragmented. Federal pages explain the system, cantonal pages explain the forms, and steuern-easy covers beginners. Nobody links "my situation" (moving canton, marriage, child, first job, Säule 3a, property, retirement payout) to the rules across all cantons.
- steuern-easy.ch is the closest public competitor to meineSteuer: interactive, persona-based and backed by the SSK. Its gaps are no English, a beginner/youth focus, and no cantonal comparison.
- meineSteuer could add most value with life-event orientation, cross-canton comparison, English for expats, and deep links into the official sources (which also builds trust).

### Gaps
- No standalone ESTV "Steuerlexikon" was confirmed; the glossary function seems to live in the Dossier Steuerinformationen (category "Steuerbegriffe") and in steuern-easy's glossary.
- It was not checked which Dossier articles exist in IT and EN. The language switcher suggests they do, but this is unverified.
- The current ch.ch tax URL could not be confirmed (the fetch returned a 404); the page may have moved.

## 2. Private and commercial resources

### Takeaway
The private market has finance portals (moneyland, comparis, VZ) with good calculators and guides wrapped in heavy commercial and affiliate content, FIRE blogs (Mustachian Post, The Poor Swiss) with strong English and optimisation content, and many English-language tax-filing services for expats. No confirmed player is at once neutral, ad-free, plain-language and organised by life event across all cantons.

### Cited Findings
- **moneyland.ch** has a thorough guide to the Swiss tax return (dated 20 Jan 2026) in DE with EN and FR versions. It explains the four-page main form and the supplementary forms. The site around it carries a lot of commercial content: "Spezialangebote", affiliate-style links to UBS, Swissquote, Saxo, telecoms, and a "Deal des Tages". — [moneyland Steuererklärung](https://www.moneyland.ch/de/steuererklaerung-schweiz)
- **comparis.ch** runs a tax calculator. It partnered with Gryps.ch to connect users with about 350 tax advisors in 18 German-speaking cantons, so the service doubles as lead generation. — [IT-Magazine](https://www.itmagazine.ch/artikel/60144/Steuern_sparen_mit_Comparis_ch_und_Gryps_ch.html)
- **VZ VermögensZentrum** has an income and wealth tax calculator, including commune comparisons and the effect of moving. — [VZ Steuerrechner](https://www.vermoegenszentrum.ch/finanzrechner-und-vergleiche/einkommens-und-vermoegenssteuern-berechnen)
- **The Poor Swiss** has a long guide to Swiss taxes in EN, DE and FR, plus articles on Quellensteuer (tax at source). — [The Poor Swiss guide](https://thepoorswiss.com/guide-taxes-switzerland-reduce-taxes/comment-page-4/); [DE](https://thepoorswiss.com/de/leitfaden-steuern-schweiz-steuern-reduzieren/); [Quellensteuer](https://thepoorswiss.com/tax-at-source-in-switzerland/)
- **Mustachian Post** publishes a tax-return guide, and its forum hosts DIY tax-simulation threads and community tools such as "OpenSteuerAuszug", which builds eSteuerauszug files for IBKR/Schwab. — [Mustachian Post Steuererklärung](https://www.mustachianpost.com/de/steuererklarung-schweiz/); [MP forum tax simulation](https://forum.mustachianpost.com/t/tax-simulation-diy/7397); [OpenSteuerAuszug thread](https://forum.mustachianpost.com/t/opensteuerauszug-generate-your-own-etax-esteuerauszug-for-ibkr-schwab-and-others/18288)
- **Expat and online filing services seen in 2025–26 search results (English-language)**: taxea.ch (digital filing via portal or app, Zurich) — [taxea](https://www.taxea.ch/en); TaxRight.ch ("2'000+ expats across 12 Swiss cantons") — [taxright](https://taxright.ch/en/tax-return/); Taxolution (MyTaxolution portal) — [taxolution](https://www.taxolution.ch/); abrechnungen.ch (EN/DE/RU/UK) — [abrechnungen](https://www.abrechnungen.ch/tax-declaration-for-individuals/?lang=en); Zurich Tax Help — [zurichtaxhelp](https://zurichtaxhelp.ch/); expatservices.ch — [expatservices](https://www.expatservices.ch/en/tax-return); treuhand-suche.ch (advisor comparison) — [treuhand-suche](https://treuhand-suche.ch/blog/steuerberater-vergleich/). These sites were seen in search results only; their 2026 activity was not checked individually.
- **Taxfix is a German product.** The app and Taxfix SE target the German Finanzamt, and no evidence was found of a Swiss filing product. — [Taxfix App Store](https://itunes.apple.com/ch/app/id1163776422); [taxfix.de](https://taxfix.de/app/)
- **Beobachter** keeps a topic page on Treuhänder, and SRF Kassensturz has reported on a Treuhänder whose errors caused clients to pay too much tax. Consumer media cover the risks of outsourcing. — [Beobachter](https://www.beobachter.ch/stichworte/t/treuhander); [SRF Kassensturz](https://www.srf.ch/sendungen/kassensturz-espresso/geld-treuhaender-pfuscht-zu-hohe-steuern)

### Inferences
- The finance portals monetise traffic through affiliate deals and advisor leads, so their editorial neutrality is undermined by the surrounding business model. An ad-free, non-commercial site is a real differentiator.
- English content today comes mostly from FIRE blogs and from expat filing services that want to sell filing. Neutral English education is a gap.
- The calculators (ESTV, VZ, comparis) answer "how much?" but not "why?" or "what do I need to do in my situation?".

### Gaps
- No Swiss "TaxPal" or "Smart Tax" app was found. These names appear not to exist as Swiss consumer products (not proven absent).
- K-Tipp and bank tax guides (UBS, PostFinance, Raiffeisen) were not checked in this session.
- Tellco, Smartsteuer CH and other online Treuhand offers were not checked.

## 3. Reusable data and APIs

### Takeaway
There is **no officially documented public API** for the ESTV Steuerrechner. Community projects call its internal browser endpoints (export functions for tax scales, deductions and rates) without authentication, which is a de-facto, unsupported interface with unclear terms. Fedlex offers an official SPARQL/linked-data endpoint. Statute texts are free of copyright under URG Art. 5. ESTV editorial texts such as the Dossier may be reused only with the source named. opendata.swiss datasets carry per-dataset terms, often "OPEN BY ASK", where commercial use needs the owner's permission.

### Cited Findings
- **ESTV Steuerrechner**: the official ESTV pages and media release mention no API or data-reuse terms. — [admin.ch release](https://www.admin.ch/gov/en/start/documentation/media-releases.msg-id-79034.html); [ESTV Steuerbelastung](https://www.estv.admin.ch/de/steuerbelastung-in-der-schweiz)
- The open-source project **devbrains-com/swisstaxcalculator** (MIT) re-implements the calculation logic. It gets its raw data "by looking into the requests done by the browser" on the calculator's tax-data pages (`#/taxdata/tax-scales`, `#/taxdata/deductions`, `#/taxdata/tax-rates`). The export operations are named `API_exportManyTaxScales`, `API_exportManyDeductions` and `API_exportManySimpleRates`, and the import script has a `--download` flag. The README says nothing about ESTV terms of use. — [GitHub devbrains-com/swisstaxcalculator](https://github.com/devbrains-com/swisstaxcalculator)
- An MCP server (MIT) wraps the ESTV calculator "with no API key or authentication required". It covers income/wealth, capital payment, legal entity and inheritance calculations for tax years **2010–2026**, warns "Figures are from the official ESTV model — not a binding tax assessment", and caches results for a week. Exact endpoint paths are not documented on the listing. — [Glama estv-mcp](https://glama.ai/mcp/servers/vz22hup89f); [estv-mcp tools](https://glama.ai/mcp/servers/noaahh/estv-mcp/tools/get_tax_years)
- **Tax-burden data**: the ESTV offers tax-burden statistics (cantonal capitals, communes, Tax Freedom Day, international) as tables, maps and Excel, plus basic data (commune and canton rates, deductions). No licence is stated on the ESTV page. — [ESTV Steuerbelastung](https://www.estv.admin.ch/de/steuerbelastung-in-der-schweiz); [Steckbrief Steuerbelastung PDF](https://www.estv.admin.ch/dam/de/sd-web/R1HHFfLGKCEl/steckbrief-steuerbelastung-schweiz-de.pdf)
- **opendata.swiss terms**: each dataset carries usage symbols. "OPEN BY ASK" means non-commercial use is allowed, commercial use needs the owner's prior permission, and naming the source is recommended. Some datasets carry a mandatory source credit. — [opendata.swiss terms of use](https://opendata.swiss/en/terms-of-use)
- The ESTV tax-burden datasets are reported as open use with source credit, and commercial use requires the owner's permission (search-result summary; the specific dataset page was not fetched). — [ESTV Steuerbelastung](https://www.estv.admin.ch/de/steuerbelastung-in-der-schweiz)
- **ICTax Kursliste**: the ESTV publishes the Kursliste (year-end tax values of securities) at ictax.admin.ch. No public API documentation was found in this session. — [admin.ch ICTax release](https://www.admin.ch/gov/en/start/documentation/media-releases/media-releases-federal-council.msg-id-82186.html)
- **Fedlex**: the federal legislation platform (BBl, AS, SR, treaties, consultations) has an official SPARQL endpoint at `https://fedlex.data.admin.ch/sparqlendpoint`, which accepts GET or POST with SPARQL queries. It also offers a metadata viewer and URI templates (ELI). — [Fedlex platform description (LINDAS/agriculture.ld.admin.ch)](https://agriculture.ld.admin.ch/data-usage/fedlex); [fedlex-mcp PyPI](https://pypi.org/project/fedlex-mcp/2.0.0/)
- **Copyright (URG Art. 5)**: copyright does not protect laws, ordinances, international treaties and other official enactments, nor decisions, minutes and reports of authorities, nor official or legally required collections and translations of these works. — [URG Art. 5 (bilingual)](https://www.droit-bilingue.ch/en-de/2/23/231.1-5-8.html)
- **ESTV editorial content** (the Dossier Steuerinformationen) is copyrighted, but extracts or full texts may be used if the source is named. — [ESTV Steuerinformationen](https://www.estv.admin.ch/de/steuerinformationen)

### Inferences
- Statute texts (DBG, StHG and cantonal tax laws) and court decisions can be quoted freely. Explanatory brochures, Wegleitungen and Kreisschreiben are less clear: they are not "Erlasse", although Kreisschreiben and decisions may fall under "Berichte/Entscheide" of authorities. Linking and quoting with attribution is the safe path.
- Building a calculator on the undocumented ESTV endpoints carries operational risk (they can change without notice) and legal uncertainty, since no licence is stated. The safer options are to link or deep-link to swisstaxcalculator.estv.admin.ch, or to ask the ESTV for written permission before reusing its data.
- Fedlex SPARQL is a solid source for automatically tracking law versions and linking to the current text of DBG (SR 642.11) and StHG (SR 642.14).

### Gaps
- No official ESTV statement on reusing the Steuerrechner backend was found. A direct enquiry to the ESTV is advisable.
- The exact opendata.swiss dataset IDs and licence symbols for ESTV tax data were not confirmed in this session.
- No ICTax API was confirmed. Do not assume one exists.

## 4. Legal constraints for a publisher

### Takeaway
At federal level, tax advice is not a regulated profession. Only court representation falls under the cantonal Anwaltsmonopol, and **Ticino** requires a licence for professional tax advice and representation done for third parties ("fiduciario commercialista"). A purely educational, general-information site that does not advise individual cases falls outside these regimes, but it should use clear disclaimers. Other obligations: an Impressum (UWG Art. 3 lit. s) for any commercial offer, and the revDSG/FMG rules on cookies (in principle opt-out; opt-in for unexpected or high-risk processing).

### Cited Findings
- **Ticino, Legge sull'esercizio delle professioni di fiduciario (18 June 1984, RL 11.1.4.1)**: under Art. 1, the activities of fiduciario commercialista, immobiliare and finanziario "svolte per conto di terzi a titolo professionale" in Ticino need a licence, and the licence is personal. Under Art. 5, a fiduciario commercialista is anyone who does non-occasional work including "c) consulenza e rappresentanza fiscale". Under Art. 3, the term "fiduciario" may be used in offers to the public only by licence holders. Under Art. 4, lawyers within the monopoly and some bank and fund staff are exempt. — [lexfind TI 11.1.4.1](https://lexfind.ch/tolv/103331/it) (consolidated text; revised 2009 and 2021 per search summary)
- Ticino's supervisory authority keeps a register of licence holders and publishes it in the Foglio ufficiale every year. — search-result summary of [lexfind TI](https://www.lexfind.ch/tolv/103347/it)
- **Anwaltsmonopol**: professional representation before courts is reserved to cantonally licensed lawyers. The federal law (BGFA) does not set the scope of the cantonal monopoly, and in principle anyone may act for themselves before authorities. — search-result summaries of [UZH Anwaltsrecht](https://uzh.ch/cmsssl/ius/dam/jcr:ffffffff-dfba-cab6-ffff-ffffeb732f5f/Lehrbuch.pdf) and [SG Anwaltswesen](https://www.sg.ch/recht/gerichte/anwalts----notarwesen.html)
- Swiss Treuhänder work under the professional rules of associations such as TREUHAND|SUISSE and EXPERTsuisse, are liable for their own misconduct, and must hold liability insurance (association rules, not a state licence). — [WEKA](https://www.weka.ch/themen/recht/auftrag-und-werkvertrag/auftragsaehnliche-verhaeltnisse/article/schweizer-treuhaender-information-zu-den-rahmenbedingungen-fuer-den-profi/)
- **Impressum (UWG Art. 3 Abs. 1 lit. s)**: anyone offering goods, works or services in electronic commerce must state their identity and contact address, including e-mail, clearly and completely. Purely private websites with no commercial purpose are generally not covered. — [law.ch Impressumspflicht](https://law.ch/lawinfo/ict-law-informations-und-kommunikationstechnologie/impressumspflicht-e-commerce/); [Handelsverband.swiss](https://handelsverband.swiss/swiss-online-garantie-zertifizierung-impressum/)
- **Cookies and analytics**: according to the EDÖB Merkblatt (31.03.2026), cookies are governed by **FMG Art. 45c** (users must be told about the processing and its purpose, and that they can refuse it) and by the DSG. Swiss law focuses on transparency and the right to object: users must be told about non-necessary cookies and be able to opt out, in principle before the cookies are activated. **Prior express consent** is required if the processing is "unexpected", "high-risk", or involves particularly sensitive personal data. The EDÖB's detailed cookie guideline is dated 22 Jan 2025, updated 6 Oct 2025. — [EDÖB Merkblatt Cookies 2026](https://www.edoeb.admin.ch/dam/de/sd-web/ByXs53ExpIEy/20260327%20Merkblatt%20Cookies_DE.pdf); [EDÖB Leitfaden v1.1 (mirror)](https://steigerlegal.ch/wp-content/uploads/2025/10/edoeb-leitfaden-cookies-11-20251006.pdf)
- Secondary sources on the updated guideline: continuing to browse does not count as consent, and an easy "reject" option is recommended for analytics and marketing cookies. — [Handelsverband.swiss](https://handelsverband.swiss/cookie-banner-in-der-schweiz-reicht-ein-einfacher-cookie-banner-ohne-auswahloption-noch-aus/); [iubenda](https://www.iubenda.com/de/blog/swiss-authoritys-new-cookie-guidelines-what-you-need-to-know/)
- **revDSG/nDSG**: the Federal Act on Data Protection (SR 235.1) has been in force since 1 Sep 2023. — [Fedlex DSG](https://www.fedlex.admin.ch/eli/cc/2022/491/de) (statute reference; not fetched in this session)
- **Accessibility**: the BehiG (SR 151.3) binds federal bodies, and eCH-0059 is the Swiss accessibility standard (based on WCAG). For a private site, both are good practice rather than law. — [Fedlex BehiG](https://www.fedlex.admin.ch/eli/cc/2003/667/de); [eCH-0059](https://www.ech.ch/de/ech/ech-0059) (references; not fetched in this session)

### Inferences
- An educational site that explains rules in general terms does not fall under the Ticino law, which targets professional, non-occasional work "per conto di terzi". Offering individual tax-return preparation or personalised advice to Ticino residents would. Avoid the word "fiduciario" (and probably "Treuhand" as a self-description) in IT and offers.
- Disclaimers to use: "general information, not individual tax or legal advice; not binding; check the official cantonal Wegleitung or ask your tax office". Mirror the ESTV calculator's "not a binding assessment" wording. Liability for free information under the Swiss Code of Obligations (OR) is limited but not zero, and stating sources and dates lowers the risk.
- Privacy by design: if calculators run fully client-side (no inputs sent to a server, no accounts), most DSG obligations reduce to the privacy policy and the analytics setup. Cookieless, self-hosted analytics avoids consent questions under FMG 45c. Income, wealth and church affiliation are sensitive in substance (religious views are "besonders schützenswert" under the DSG). If such data is ever stored or sent, use explicit consent and minimise what is collected.
- Since meineSteuer will most likely be non-commercial, the strict UWG Impressum duty may not apply. Publishing full identity and contact details anyway is best practice and builds trust.

### Gaps
- No confirmed source shows whether cantons other than Ticino license tax advice. Some sources mention cantonal Treuhand rules elsewhere, but this was not verified.
- No Swiss case law was found on liability for free online tax information.
- The DSG, BehiG and eCH-0059 pages were cited by statute reference without being fetched in this session.

## 5. Audience insights

### Takeaway
Most Swiss residents file their own return: in a comparis survey (March 2024), 63% self-file and 21% use a Treuhänder. E-filing is now the norm in many cantons (96% of Solothurn taxpayers receive eTax access data only), and paying the tax bill is a real pain point (16% had payment difficulties). Self-filers are the core audience for education.

### Cited Findings
- **comparis survey (press release 12 March 2024)**: 63% fill in their tax return themselves and 21% use a Treuhänder (16% other or no answer). Self-filing is higher among men (68% vs 58%) and among people with high education (69% vs 58%). 16% had payment difficulties between 2017 and 2021: 7% borrowed money, 6% asked for a Stundung, 3% received a Betreibung. Among 18–35-year-olds, 12% had to borrow. Under CHF 4,000 gross per month, only 65% paid from their own funds and 8% received a Betreibung. — [presseportal comparis](https://www.presseportal.ch/de/pm/100003671/100916863)
- **Statista**: a 2015 survey on "Hilfe beim Ausfüllen der Steuererklärung in der Schweiz" exists, but its figures sit behind a paywall and were not fetched. — [Statista](https://de.statista.com/statistik/daten/studie/514751/umfrage/hilfe-beim-ausfuellen-der-steuererklaerung-in-der-schweiz)
- **E-filing**: in canton Solothurn, 96% of taxpayers receive only eTax access data, and about 4% (7,414) still get paper forms. The filing deadline is 31 March 2026, with a free extension to 31 July 2026 (press release 27 Jan 2026). — [so.ch](https://so.ch/verwaltung/staatskanzlei/medien/medienmitteilung/news/steuererklaerung-2025-96-prozent-digital-unterwegs/)
- In Basel-Landschaft, about two-thirds of submissions are electronic (per search-result summary; date unclear). — [BL e-tax PDF](https://bl-api.webcloud7.ch/politik-und-behorden/direktionen/finanz-und-kirchendirektion/fkd-medienordner/e-tax.pdf/@@download/file)
- For historical comparison, Zurich reported 11.5% online filing by end of May 2014. — [zh.ch 2014](https://www.zh.ch/de/news-uebersicht/medienmitteilungen/2014/07/online_steuererklaerungen.html)
- Common pain points in public guides: many people find the return complicated or time-consuming and hand it to a professional ([search summary of Mustachian Post](https://www.mustachianpost.com/de/steuererklarung-schweiz/)); each canton has its own rules and software ([The Poor Swiss](https://thepoorswiss.com/guide-taxes-switzerland-reduce-taxes/comment-page-4/)); expats deal with Quellensteuer, relocation, refunds and double taxation ([taxea Zurich](https://www.taxea.ch/en/location/zurich)).

### Inferences
- About 6 in 10 adults self-file, so the core audience is large. Women and people with lower education self-file less often, which suggests a need for confidence-building, plain-language content.
- Young adults and low earners struggle to pay, so content on provisional instalments (Akontozahlungen), Stundung, Steuererlass and budgeting for taxes meets a real need.
- With near-universal e-filing, content should follow each canton's e-tax software screens rather than the paper form.

### Gaps
- No national figure for e-filing share was found; data is per canton and incomplete.
- No Swiss Google Trends or search-volume data was gathered on tax questions.
- The comparis survey's sample size and method were not stated in the press release.
