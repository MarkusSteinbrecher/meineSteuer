## 1. Wealth-tax tax-free allowances (single / married / per child)

### Takeaway
There are three different mechanisms: (a) a deductible social deduction / Freibetrag (most cantons), (b) a "steuerfreies Minimum" or zero-rate bracket / non-taxation threshold (ZH, BE, BL, TI, VD, NE, JU), and (c) structural relief for couples via splitting (NE 52 % also on wealth). Married allowances range from CHF 18,000 (BE, plus a 100,000 threshold) to CHF 408,000 (ZG); child allowances from none (ZH, FR, BL, VD, VS, NE) to CHF 102,000 (ZG) and 100,000 (TG).

Further details:
- GE: an additional deduction of half the wealth invested in the taxpayer's own business, max CHF 533,042 (TY 2025). — [SM-1](https://www.estv2.admin.ch/stp/sm/persoenlicher-abzug-v-de-fr.pdf)
- GE child condition: minor child with income ≤ 16,346, or apprentice/student up to 25 with wealth ≤ 93,282. The child's own wealth is subtracted from the 43,816. — [SM-2](https://www.estv2.admin.ch/stp/sm/kinderabzug-v-de-fr.pdf)
- NE taxes couples' wealth at the rate for 52 % of the amount (divisor 1.92). Example: CHF 500,000 is taxed at the rate for CHF 260,000. — [ESTV Dossier Vermögenssteuer 2026](https://www.estv2.admin.ch/stp/ds/d-vermoegenssteuer-np-de.pdf)
- Proportional (flat) wealth tariffs: LU, UR, SZ, OW, NW, AI, SG, TG. All other cantons are progressive in per mille. — [ESTV Dossier Vermögenssteuer 2026](https://www.estv2.admin.ch/stp/ds/d-vermoegenssteuer-np-de.pdf)
- ZG: the wealth tariff is applied with indexed brackets of 254,000 from 2025 (statutory 250,000), rates 0.425 / 0.85 / 1.275 / 1.7 ‰. — [KB-ZG](https://www.estv2.admin.ch/stp/kb/zg-de.pdf)
- BE: in addition to the 100,000 Freigrenze, the tariff starts with a 0 bracket on the first 36,000. — [KB-BE](https://www.estv2.admin.ch/stp/kb/be-de.pdf)
- VD has no personal wealth deduction. Its only wealth-side allowance besides the minimum is the household-contents deduction of 50,000 (indexed 60,000 for 2026) per taxpayer, doubled for couples. — [KB-VD](https://www.estv2.admin.ch/stp/kb/vd-fr.pdf)
- **Conflict, VD:** the ESTV Steuermäppchen 2025 lists the VD minimum as 60,000 / 120,000. The VD Kantonsblatt states "n'atteint pas 50'000 (60'000)* francs", where * = "Montant indexé pour l'année fiscale 2026". So 60,000 / 120,000 appears to be the 2026 value and 50,000 / 100,000 the earlier one. The TY 2025 value is therefore uncertain (?). — [SM-3](https://www.estv2.admin.ch/stp/sm/steuerfreies-minimum-v-de-fr.pdf); [KB-VD](https://www.estv2.admin.ch/stp/kb/vd-fr.pdf)
- **Unverified, NE:** the married minimum of 93,000 comes from ESTV SM-3. With 52 % splitting one would mathematically expect about 96,154 (50,000 / 0.52). The Kantonsblatt states only "0 ‰ up to 50,000" plus splitting. Keep 93,000 (ESTV), but it is unverified. — [KB-NE](https://www.estv2.admin.ch/stp/kb/ne-fr.pdf)

### Inferences
- For a website, "wealth tax-free amount" must be modelled as two separate parameters: a deduction (reduces taxable wealth) and a threshold/zero bracket (BE and BL are Freigrenzen: once exceeded, the whole amount is taxable; ZH and NE are zero-rate brackets that every taxpayer keeps).
- ZG's figures look extreme (408,000 for couples) partly because they are expressed as indexed figures on top of a lower statutory base. Comparisons should use the applicable (indexed) figure.

### Gaps
- No single ESTV table for 2026 exists yet (Steuermäppchen 2026 not published as of these downloads). The 2026 values come from Kantonsblätter extraction and "none found" means no 2026 marker was found, not a positive confirmation.
- Special wealth deductions for AHV/IV pensioners (ESTV table "Abzug für Bezüger von AHV-IV-Renten") were not collected.

## 2. Childcare (Drittbetreuung), insurance premiums, two-earner deduction

### Takeaway
Childcare caps range from CHF 6,000 (SZ, 2025) to 26,700 (SG) per child under 14 (federal 25,800). The insurance/savings-interest flat rate ranges from about 1,700/3,300 (OW) to 5,500/10,900 (TI) with per-child supplements of 0–1,600. The two-earner deduction ranges from none (TG) or CHF 500 (FR, AI, SG) to 9,500 (BE) or 10,300 (GL), and its structure varies (fixed, percentage with min/max, deduction from both incomes).

### Inferences
- The insurance flat rate is the deduction most likely to change each year (AG and VS index it to health premiums; SZ, ZG and SO raised it for 2026). A website should store it per canton per year.
- "Zweiverdienerabzug" values are not comparable across cantons. Cantons with full splitting or a low fixed amount (FR, AI, SG, GR, AG) relieve two-earner couples mainly via the tariff. BE and GL use percentage-based deductions that scale with income.

### Gaps
- Insurance child supplement not stated for BS and AG (ESTV shows none).
- The "no 2/3a" variant is not stated for BS, BL, SH, AR, TG and AG; ESTV shows only one amount.
- Own-care (Eigenbetreuung) deductions are only noted where ESTV lists them (LU, NW, ZG, VS). They are not systematically collected.

## 3. Married-couple relief method and personal deductions (income tax)

### Takeaway
Cantons use four models: a double tariff (Doppeltarif: ZH, BE, LU, ZG, BS, AR, TI, JU), full splitting with divisor 2.0 (FR, BL, AI, SG, AG, TG, GE), partial splitting (SZ, SO, SH, GR at 1.9; NW 1.85; GL 1.7; NE 52 % ≈ 1.92), or a special system (VD family quotient 1.8; VS 35 % tax rebate; UR flat-rate tax with high social deductions; OW flat rate with a 20 % income deduction for couples).

- **Correction to 06 (Glarus):** 06 said Glarus raised its "Teilsplittingfaktor" in 2024. ESTV 2025 states the current GL divisor is 1.7. — [SM-7](https://www.estv2.admin.ch/stp/sm/persoenlicher-abzug-e-de-fr.pdf)
- VS 2026: the 35 % rebate footnote says "dès 2025". No separate 2026 rebate figures are shown. — [KB-VS](https://www.estv2.admin.ch/stp/kb/vs-fr.pdf)

### Inferences
- A tax calculator needs a per-canton "married model" enum: `double_tariff`, `splitting(divisor)`, `quotient(shares)`, `rebate(pct,min,max)`, `flat_rate`. Splitting divisors are 2.0, 1.9, 1.92 (NE), 1.85 and 1.7.
- Single parents get the married relief in most splitting and double-tariff cantons; VD gives 1.3 shares instead of 1.8.

### Gaps
- SM-7 only covers the base mechanism. Tariff tables themselves (rates per bracket) are in `06`/ESTV tariff sources, not here.

## 4. Flat-rate property maintenance deduction (Pauschalabzug Liegenschaftsunterhalt)

### Takeaway
Most cantons copy the federal 10 % (building ≤ 10 years) / 20 % (> 10 years) of gross rent or Eigenmietwert. The exceptions: a flat 20 % regardless of age in ZH, AI and SG; 15 / 25 % in SH and GE; 20 / 25 % in BL; and VD 20 / 30 % on the own home (other property 10 / 20 %), where the threshold is building age 20 years. NE caps the flat rate at 7,200 / 12,000.

- Moot for owner-occupied homes once the Eigenmietwert abolition takes effect (see 02: from 2029). It stays relevant for rented-out property.

### Gaps
- No further gaps for the flat rate. The VD age threshold is resolved: it is 20 years, per the cantonal law text.

## 5. Structural deviations and cross-check against earlier notes

### Takeaway
Several cantons deviate structurally: tax credits instead of deductions (BL child credit of 750), tax rebates (VS 35 %), income-dependent deductions (VD, FR wealth), Freigrenzen vs Freibeträge (BE, BL, TI, VD, JU wealth), splitting applied to wealth (NE), and flat-rate taxes (UR, OW). ESTV's "2025" Steuermäppchen occasionally already contain 2026 figures (VD), so every cell needs a year check.

### Cited Findings
- BL grants its income-tax child relief as a reduction of the tax bill (750 per child) rather than a deduction. — [ESTV SM Kinderabzug Einkommen 2025](https://www.estv2.admin.ch/stp/sm/kinderabzug-e-de-fr.pdf); [KB-BL](https://www.estv2.admin.ch/stp/kb/bl-de.pdf)
- BS reduces the wealth tax by 75 / 50 / 25 % for low-income taxpayers (taxable income ≤ 20,000 married / 14,000 others) with wealth up to 100,000 / 200,000 / 400,000. — [SM-1](https://www.estv2.admin.ch/stp/sm/persoenlicher-abzug-v-de-fr.pdf)
- Indexation: ZG, TG, VD, GE and JU index automatically every year. ZH indexes every two years. UR indexes social deductions yearly. BE, NW, GL, FR, GR and AG index when a threshold is reached. SZ, SH and AI index optionally. — [ESTV Dossier Vermögenssteuer 2026](https://www.estv2.admin.ch/stp/ds/d-vermoegenssteuer-np-de.pdf)
- **Commuting cross-check (not redone, contradiction resolution only):** `06` cites ZH 5,000 / BE 6,700 from an aggregator (goldblum.ch). `02` cites ESTV values ZH 5,200 / BE 7,000. The ESTV Fahrkosten table for TY 2025 confirms BE = 7,000. Use the ESTV values in `02`. — [ESTV SM Fahrkosten 2025](https://www.estv2.admin.ch/stp/sm/fahrkosten-de-fr.pdf)
- Child deduction cross-check: ZH income child deduction rises from 9,300 (2025) to 9,400 (2026). GR's child deduction also rose for 2026. — [KB-ZH](https://www.estv2.admin.ch/stp/kb/zh-de.pdf); [KB-GR](https://www.estv2.admin.ch/stp/kb/gr-de.pdf)

### Inferences
- The site should present each value with a tax-year tag and "Bund vs Kanton". It should also model deductions in at least four types: deduction, threshold (Freigrenze), zero bracket, and tax credit/rebate.
- Biggest 2026 movers in these columns: SZ (insurance, childcare, personal deduction, tariff), ZG (insurance), GR (childcare, two-earner, if the 2025 ESTV values are right), AG and VS (insurance), ZH (wealth bracket, childcare, two-earner), GE (indexation of everything).

### Gaps
- No ESTV Steuermäppchen 2026 was available. 2026 values come from cantonal Kantonsblätter (Stand Feb 2026), extracted by text search. "None found" means no 2026 marker was found, which is not a positive confirmation.
- Unresolved "?" cells: UR 2026 wealth values (no year label); LU childcare 20,200 (year ambiguous); GR 2025 childcare / two-earner (ESTV vs law text); VD TY 2025 values (ESTV appears to show 2026); NE married wealth minimum (93,000 vs splitting arithmetic).
- Cantonal Wegleitungen 2025/2026 were not consulted individually. They would be the tie-breaker for the "?" cells above.
