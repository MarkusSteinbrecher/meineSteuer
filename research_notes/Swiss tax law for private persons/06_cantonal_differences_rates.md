## 1. How cantonal and municipal tax is computed: einfache Steuer × Steuerfuss

### Takeaway
Most cantons do not put the tax you owe directly into the law. The law holds a progressive base tariff (Grundtarif, giving the *einfache Steuer* / *impôt de base*). Each year the canton, the municipality (Gemeinde / commune) and the church parish (Kirchgemeinde / paroisse) set their own multiplier (Steuerfuss, Steueranlage, jährliches Vielfaches; French *coefficient* / *centimes additionnels*). Each multiplier is applied to the same einfache Steuer and the results are added. The federal tax (direkte Bundessteuer, dBSt / IFD) and Valais's cantonal tax use directly applicable tariffs with no multiplier. In Geneva, and in Basel-Stadt's two small municipalities, the municipal tax is a percentage of the cantonal tax, not of the base tax.

### Cited Findings
- Mechanism: "Bei den meisten Kantons- und Gemeindesteuern ist der Steuertarif … nicht direkt anwendbar. Die geschuldete Steuer ergibt sich erst durch die Multiplikation des durch den Steuertarif bestimmten Steuerbetrags (einfache Steuer) mit einem jährlichen Vielfachen, dem Steuerfuss." The tax laws usually contain only the Grundtarif ("einfache Ansätze"), and the result is called the "einfache Steuer". — [ESTV-StF 2026](https://www.estv2.admin.ch/stp/ds/e-steuersatz-steuerfuss-de.pdf)
- Purpose: the Steuerfuss is set periodically, usually every year, by the legislature (cantonal parliament, municipal parliament or Gemeindeversammlung). It lets revenue be adjusted without changing the law. 100 % means the base tariff is enough; 110 % brings in 10 % more. In most cantons and municipalities the Steuerfuss decision is subject to an optional or mandatory referendum, which gives citizens direct control over their own tax burden. — [ESTV-StF 2026](https://www.estv2.admin.ch/stp/ds/e-steuersatz-steuerfuss-de.pdf)
- There are two ways to express the multiplier. Most cantons use a **percentage** (e.g. 115 %). Some use **units** (Einheiten, e.g. 2.14). BE, LU, OW, NW, AR and JU use units, as the 2026 table below shows. — [ESTV-StF 2026](https://www.estv2.admin.ch/stp/ds/e-steuersatz-steuerfuss-de.pdf)
- ESTV worked example, percentage form: taxable income CHF 50,000; tariff rate 3.322 % gives einfache Steuer CHF 1,661. Canton 100 % = 1,661.00, municipality 122 % = 2,026.40, church 11 % = 182.70. **Total CHF 3,870.10.** — [ESTV-StF 2026](https://www.estv2.admin.ch/stp/ds/e-steuersatz-steuerfuss-de.pdf)
- ESTV worked example, units form: taxable income CHF 50,000; rate 1.3285 % gives einfache Steuer CHF 664.25. Canton 2.40 = 1,594.20, municipality 2.58 = 1,713.75, church 0.28 = 186.00. **Total CHF 3,493.95.** — [ESTV-StF 2026](https://www.estv2.admin.ch/stp/ds/e-steuersatz-steuerfuss-de.pdf)
- Exceptions:
  - **Valais (VS)** has directly applicable cantonal tariffs and no cantonal multiplier. Valais municipalities have their own progressive base tariff, to which they apply a multiplier in units.
  - In **UR and VS** municipalities may have their own tariffs.
  - In some cantons, mainly in French-speaking Switzerland, the municipal multiplier applies to the cantonal tax actually owed, not to the einfache Steuer. These are the "centimes additionnels".
  - — [ESTV-StF 2026](https://www.estv2.admin.ch/stp/ds/e-steuersatz-steuerfuss-de.pdf)
- **Basel-Stadt**: the city of Basel levies no municipal tax because it is included in the cantonal tax. Only Riehen and Bettingen levy a municipal tax, as a percentage of the statutory income and wealth tax (centimes additionnels). BS also has no multiplier for wealth tax. — [ESTV-StF 2026](https://www.estv2.admin.ch/stp/ds/e-steuersatz-steuerfuss-de.pdf)
- **Graubünden**: municipalities levy income and wealth tax as a percentage of the einfache Kantonssteuer and set it every year. — [ESTV-StF 2026](https://www.estv2.admin.ch/stp/ds/e-steuersatz-steuerfuss-de.pdf)
- **Church tax multiplier**: where church tax exists, it is usually also a multiplier. Depending on the canton, it is set by religious bodies (Kirchgemeindeversammlung or Kirchgemeinderat) or by political bodies. It usually applies to the cantonal base tariff, sometimes to the cantonal or municipal tax owed (as centimes additionnels). Some parishes have their own tariffs. — [ESTV-StF 2026](https://www.estv2.admin.ch/stp/ds/e-steuersatz-steuerfuss-de.pdf)
- **Who sets the cantonal multiplier**:
  - In most cantons the parliament (Grosser Rat), subject to optional referendum.
  - Mandatory referendum: GL (Landsgemeinde).
  - Referendum only above a statutory threshold: mandatory in UR and SO, optional in BE, LU and FR.
  - Optional referendum only if the multiplier differs from the previous year: TI and GE.
  - Final decision by parliament, no referendum: ZH, SZ, AR, AI, SG, GR, TG, JU.
  - NW sets its cantonal multiplier only every three years.
  - — [ESTV-StF 2026](https://www.estv2.admin.ch/stp/ds/e-steuersatz-steuerfuss-de.pdf)
- In Geneva the communal multiplier is set every year by the municipal council, is subject to optional referendum and needs approval from the Conseil d'État. In Schwyz there is an additional **district** level (Bezirkssteuer), set by the Bezirksgemeinde. — [ESTV-StF 2026](https://www.estv2.admin.ch/stp/ds/e-steuersatz-steuerfuss-de.pdf)
- **Nidwalden** allows temporary "Steuerrabatte", which lower the multiplier for a single year. Expiry of such a rebate raised burdens in 2024. — [BAK 2025](https://www.zh.ch/content/dam/zhweb/bilder-dokumente/themen/steuern-finanzen/kantonsfinanzen/steuerbelastungsmonitor/BAK%20Economics_Z%C3%BCrcher_Steuerbelastungsmonitor_2025.pdf)
- **Load caps (Belastungsobergrenzen)**:
  - Federal: maximum dBSt rate 11.5 % on income, set in BV Art. 128.
  - LU: total income tax of canton, municipality and church ≤ 22.8 % (single tariff) / 22.4 % (married tariff) of taxable income; wealth tax ≤ 3.0 ‰.
  - VD: canton and municipality income tax ≤ 30 % of taxable income and ≤ 10 ‰ of wealth; combined ≤ 60 % of adjusted net income.
  - TI and GE: income and wealth tax including centimes additionnels ≤ 60 % of net income, with any reduction applied only to wealth tax.
  - BE: wealth tax reduced if it exceeds 25 % of wealth income, floor 2.4 ‰.
  - BS: floor 5 ‰ of wealth.
  - AG: reduced to 70 % of net income.
  - VS: wealth-tax shield.
  - — [ESTV-StF 2026](https://www.estv2.admin.ch/stp/ds/e-steuersatz-steuerfuss-de.pdf)

### Inferences
- When teaching the calculation, note that "Steuerfuss 119 %" in Zurich and "Steuerfuss 1.54" in Bern mean the same thing, a multiple of the einfache Steuer. The multipliers cannot be compared *across* cantons, because each canton's base tariff differs. A low Steuerfuss in Geneva (48.5 %) or Glarus (58 %) does not mean low tax. Only the combination tariff × multipliers is comparable.
- Within a canton, the municipal multiplier is the main driver of differences between municipalities, because all municipalities share the cantonal tariff and deductions (except UR and VS). A site therefore needs per-canton tariffs plus per-municipality multipliers and church rates per denomination.
- Geneva's structure is: cantonal impôt de base → cantonal centimes additionnels (147.5 % minus a 12 % reduction, per the ESTV footnote) → communal centimes additionnels (45.49 % in the city of Geneva). Communal centimes are levied on the impôt de base. The exact Geneva formula should be checked in the cantonal LIPP before implementing (see Gaps).

### Gaps
- The ESTV table rows for UR (church 115 % / 82 %) are hard to read in the extracted text. Uri church tax is probably expressed in a different base, so it should be checked on the ESTV Kantonsblatt UR.
- The exact Geneva computation (the meaning of "48.5 %" versus "Abzug von 12 % der Kantonssteuer zu 147,5 %") was not verified against the Geneva LIPP or Loi sur les centimes additionnels.
- No per-canton base tariffs (Grundtarif tables) were collected in this note. They are available in the ESTV Steuermäppchen "Einkommenssteuertarife".

## 2. Church tax (Kirchensteuer / impôt ecclésiastique), Personalsteuer, Feuerwehrpflichtersatz

### Takeaway
Church tax is levied by the recognised churches (usually Reformed, Roman Catholic and Christ-Catholic, plus some Jewish communities) on their members. In most cantons it is a multiplier on the cantonal base tax, typically about 5–26 % of the einfache Steuer in 2026. It is mandatory for members everywhere except Geneva, Neuchâtel and Ticino, where it is calculated but voluntary. **Vaud has no church tax** because worship costs are paid from the general cantonal budget. Members avoid it only by formally leaving the church (Kirchenaustritt), which is a written declaration. Some cantons and municipalities also charge a small fixed head tax (Personalsteuer / Kopfsteuer, CHF 10–70) and municipal fire-service substitute levies.

### Cited Findings
- Church tax payment is mandatory in all cantons except **Ticino, Neuchâtel and Geneva**, where taxpayers decide whether to pay. The amount is calculated but cannot be enforced. — [Universität Freiburg, Religionsrecht FAQ "Kirchensteuern"](https://www.unifr.ch/ius/religionsrecht/de/faq/kirchensteuern.html)
- **Vaud** has no church tax; worship expenditure is part of the cantonal budget. — [Universität Freiburg, Religionsrecht FAQ](https://www.unifr.ch/ius/religionsrecht/de/faq/kirchensteuern.html)
- ESTV 2026 capital-city table shows "–" for church tax in TI, VD, NE and GE, consistent with no mandatory church multiplier there. — [ESTV-StF 2026](https://www.estv2.admin.ch/stp/ds/e-steuersatz-steuerfuss-de.pdf)
- The church-tax base varies:
  - BS: 8 % of the cantonal income tax.
  - BL: Reformed 0.55 % of taxable income, Catholic 6.75 % of the cantonal tax.
  - VS: 3 % of the communal tax.
  - GR: % of the einfache Kantonssteuer.
  - Elsewhere: % or units of the einfache Steuer (e.g. ZH 10 %, SG 25/26 %, ZG 7–7.5 %).
  - — [ESTV-StF 2026](https://www.estv2.admin.ch/stp/ds/e-steuersatz-steuerfuss-de.pdf)
- Legal entities: in NE church tax is optional for companies. In TI companies are liable by law but can opt out by a simple declaration. — [Universität Freiburg, Religionsrecht FAQ](https://www.unifr.ch/ius/religionsrecht/de/faq/kirchensteuern.html)
- **Personalsteuer / Kopfsteuer (impôt personnel / taxe personnelle)**: a fixed annual amount per adult resident. Married couples living together often pay once. There is no federal equivalent and no StHG rule. Amounts for 2025:

### Inferences
- For the calculator, church tax should be a per-denomination multiplier per municipality. For a "konfessionslos" user it is zero. In GE, NE and TI it should be shown as optional or excluded by default. In VD it does not exist.
- Leaving the church (Kirchenaustritt / sortie d'Église) is the only legal way to avoid mandatory church tax for natural persons. The practical rule is a written declaration to the parish or church administration, effective from receipt or year-end depending on the canton. This was not verified per canton in this round (see Gaps).

### Gaps
- Per-canton Kirchenaustritt procedure and timing (pro-rata versus full year) were not collected.
- Feuerwehrpflichtersatz: amounts and rules are municipal or cantonal (e.g. a percentage of the einfache Steuer with a min/max). No cantonal overview table was retrieved, so specific figures were not verified.
- Christ-Catholic and Jewish community multipliers were not collected.

## 3. Tax burden comparisons (ESTV Steuerbelastung)

### Takeaway
On ESTV 2025 data (the latest published; the 2026 statistics are normally released mid-year after this), the same gross salary costs very different amounts of cantonal, municipal and church tax in different capitals. **Zug** is cheapest at every income for every household type. **Neuchâtel and Bern** are the most expensive across the board, and **Geneva and Vaud** are the most expensive at the very top. For a single person on CHF 100k gross, the capitals range from **4.04 % (Zug) to 17.34 % (Neuchâtel)**, a factor of about 4.3. Federal tax comes on top and is the same everywhere.

In 2025, 25 of 160 Zurich municipalities raised their Steuerfuss and 28 lowered it. — [BAK 2026](https://www.zh.ch/content/dam/zhweb/bilder-dokumente/themen/steuern-finanzen/kantonsfinanzen/steuerbelastungsmonitor/BAK%20Economics_Zürcher_Steuerbelastungsmonitor_2026.pdf)
- Secondary/aggregator figures, lower confidence: "For a gross income of CHF 100,000 you pay CHF 7,800 in Zug and CHF 19,400 in Geneva" (presumably including federal tax). — [statistik-journal.ch](https://statistik-journal.ch/steuerlandschaft-schweiz-ueberblick) (aggregator; method and year not verified)

### Inferences
- Federal tax (dBSt) is uniform, so it narrows the *relative* spread but not the absolute CHF gap. A site should show both "cantonal + municipal + church" and "total including dBSt".
- Rankings depend strongly on household type and income. Geneva is cheap for families on modest incomes (high child deductions and tariff structure) but among the most expensive for singles and top incomes. Basel-Stadt is similar. Any "cheapest canton" claim must specify the case.
- The ESTV percentages are of *gross employment income* with standard deductions assumed. Real-world results differ with actual deductions.

### Gaps
- ESTV's own 2025/2026 publication page content (exact release date, whether 2026 figures are out by October 2026) was not fetched. One search snippet claims a publication on 21 July 2025 (presumably the 2024 data). Whether ESTV 2026 burden statistics were published in mid-2026 was not verified, so treat 2025 as the latest confirmed.
- Lowest-burden *municipalities* nationally (e.g. Freienbach, Wollerau, Feusisberg SZ; Walchwil ZG) were not verified with 2025 figures in this round.
- Wealth-tax franc amounts per capital (e.g. CHF 1m and 10m) were not extracted, only the index ranking.

## 4. Cantonal differences in deductions and tariff models

### Takeaway
Cantonal tax laws are harmonised in *structure* (StHG / LHID) but not in *amounts*. Caps on commuting costs, childcare, insurance-premium flat rates, second-earner deductions, child deductions, wealth allowances and the way married couples are relieved (full or partial splitting versus a double tariff) all vary. These are major drivers of the burden differences shown in section 3.

### Cited Findings
- **Commuting-cost cap (Fahrkostenabzug)**, as reported in March 2026:

  | Canton | Cap (CHF) | Notes |
  |---|---|---|
  | Federal | 3,300 | |
  | Basel-Stadt | 3,200 | |
  | Geneva | 534 | |
  | Fribourg | 8,000 | reduction from 12,000 planned |
  | Lucerne | 6,500 | Greens/GLP want 3,300 |
  | St. Gallen | 8,000 | since 2024 |
  | Glarus | 6,000 | planned |
  | Solothurn | capped | amount not given |
  | No cap | – | AI, GR, JU, NE, TI, VD, VS (and GL before its change) |

  — [Blick, 7/8 March 2026](https://www.blick.ch/politik/steuerabzug-fuers-pendeln-die-grosse-liste-dem-auto-bonus-geht-es-in-den-kantonen-an-den-kragen-id21759204.html)
- Other cited caps from secondary sources: Zurich CHF 5,000, Bern CHF 6,700, Basel-Stadt CHF 3,000 (older figure), Geneva CHF 500 (older figure). — [goldblum.ch](https://goldblum.ch/de/wissensdatenbank/steuerabzuege-fur-berufspendler-fahrtkosten) (aggregator; year unclear). Geneva's cap was introduced by popular vote. — [SRF](https://www.srf.ch/news/schweiz/abstimmungen-ge-genf-begrenzt-pendlerabzug)
- ESTV maintains an official per-canton Fahrkosten comparison as a Steuermäppchen (fahrkosten-de-fr.pdf), but the file could not be downloaded in this session. — [ESTV Steuermäppchen Fahrkosten](https://www.estv.admin.ch/dam/estv/de/dokumente/estv/steuersystem/steuermaeppchen/fahrkosten-de-fr.pdf.download.pdf/fahrkosten-de-fr.pdf)
- **Zurich child deduction**: raised from CHF 9,000 to 9,300 in 2024 (cold-progression indexing). — [BAK 2025](https://www.zh.ch/content/dam/zhweb/bilder-dokumente/themen/steuern-finanzen/kantonsfinanzen/steuerbelastungsmonitor/BAK%20Economics_Z%C3%BCrcher_Steuerbelastungsmonitor_2025.pdf)
- **Partial splitting**: Glarus raised its "Teilsplittingfaktor" in 2024, which relieves married couples. — [BAK 2025](https://www.zh.ch/content/dam/zhweb/bilder-dokumente/themen/steuern-finanzen/kantonsfinanzen/steuerbelastungsmonitor/BAK%20Economics_Z%C3%BCrcher_Steuerbelastungsmonitor_2025.pdf)
- **Wealth-tax allowance changes**:
  - Valais raised its married-person wealth deduction (2025).
  - Bern's planned 2027 revision doubles the tax-free wealth threshold for married couples (from CHF 100,000 to remove the "marriage penalty") and abolishes the CHF 18,000 social wealth deduction.
  - — [BAK 2026](https://www.zh.ch/content/dam/zhweb/bilder-dokumente/themen/steuern-finanzen/kantonsfinanzen/steuerbelastungsmonitor/BAK%20Economics_Zürcher_Steuerbelastungsmonitor_2026.pdf); [Berner KMU, Steuergesetzrevision 2027](https://www.bernerkmu.ch/uploads/bernerkmu/Vernehmlassungen/Steuergesetzrevision-2027.pdf)
- **Aargau** plans (2027) to integrate its "Kleinverdienerabzug" (low-earner deduction) into the tariff and to cut the top tariff bracket. — [BAK 2026](https://www.zh.ch/content/dam/zhweb/bilder-dokumente/themen/steuern-finanzen/kantonsfinanzen/steuerbelastungsmonitor/BAK%20Economics_Zürcher_Steuerbelastungsmonitor_2026.pdf)
- **Federal reference**: the dBSt applies automatic cold-progression indexing every year, based on the index on 30 June before the tax period. It was applied in 2012 and again from 2023. Example: single, taxable income CHF 60,000, dBSt 2026 = CHF 671.40 (1.119 %). — [ESTV-StF 2026](https://www.estv2.admin.ch/stp/ds/e-steuersatz-steuerfuss-de.pdf)

### Inferences
- A calculator that only swaps Steuerfüsse while keeping one canton's deductions is wrong. Each canton needs its own deduction parameters and its own married-tariff model.

### Gaps
- No verified per-canton table was collected (in this round) for: childcare caps (Kinderdrittbetreuung), insurance-premium flat rates (Versicherungsprämienabzug), second-earner deductions (Zweitverdienerabzug), splitting divisors (e.g. full splitting with divisor 2.0 in BS/FR/SG/AG? and partial splitting 1.9 in some cantons), or double-tariff cantons (e.g. ZH, BE, ZG, with separate Grundtarif / Verheiratetentarif). The authoritative source is the ESTV Steuermäppchen series (estv2.admin.ch/stp/sm/…, e.g. "abzuege", "kinderdrittbetreuung", "versicherungspraemien", "tarifmodelle"). These should be fetched in a follow-up. My training knowledge suggests examples (e.g. ZH and ZG double tariffs; SG, AG and FR splitting), but they are unverified here and must not be published without checking.
- ESTV's official Fahrkosten table was not retrieved. The Zurich (5,000) and Bern (6,700) caps come from an aggregator.

## 5. Official and public tools and data: ESTV Steuerrechner API, opendata.swiss

### Takeaway
The ESTV tax calculator (swisstaxcalculator.estv.admin.ch) runs on an **undocumented but publicly reachable JSON API**. It covers every municipality, tax years 2010–2026, income, wealth and other taxes. Several open-source wrappers exist. However, ESTV's default terms require prior consent for reuse of the data, so it is not an open-data licence. Canton Zurich publishes its municipal Steuerfüsse as genuine open data (CSV on opendata.swiss).

### Cited Findings
- API base URL: `https://swisstaxcalculator.estv.admin.ch/delegate/ost-integration/v1/lg-proxy/operation/c3b67379_ESTV/<OPERATION>`. Every operation is a POST with a JSON body and returns `{"response": …}`. It is undocumented, exposes income, wealth, lump-sum, inheritance and corporate figures for every municipality for 2010–2026, and bad input returns an HTML 500 page. — [estv-mcp (glama.ai)](https://glama.ai/mcp/servers/noaahh/estv-mcp); [ruegerj/swiss-tax-api (Go SDK)](https://github.com/ruegerj/swiss-tax-api); [Aiyatullah/swiss-tax-calculator-mcp](https://github.com/Aiyatullah/swiss-tax-calculator-mcp); [devbrains-com/swisstaxcalculator](https://github.com/devbrains-com/swisstaxcalculator)
- **Verified live on 3 Oct 2026**: `POST …/API_searchLocation` with `{"Search":"Zug"}` returned JSON with `TaxLocationID` (e.g. 630000000), `BfsID` 1711, ZipCode, City and Canton. — direct test against the endpoint above.
- The ESTV web UI exposes tax rates, multipliers and deductions at federal, cantonal and communal level, but exports only one subset at a time as .xlsx, although JSON is sent to the browser internally. "The default terms and conditions … require prior written consent of the copyright holder (the Swiss federal authorities), rather than offering them in a customary open data license." — [gendx/fetch-ch-tax-rates](https://github.com/gendx/fetch-ch-tax-rates); see also [admin.ch terms](https://www.admin.ch/gov/en/start/terms-and-conditions.html)
- ESTV publications such as the Steuerinformationen dossier may be quoted for non-commercial purposes without prior consent if the source is given. — [ESTV-StF 2026](https://www.estv2.admin.ch/stp/ds/e-steuersatz-steuerfuss-de.pdf)
- **opendata.swiss, canton Zurich**: "Steuerfüsse der Zürcher Gemeinden für natürliche und juristische Personen" from the Statistisches Amt ZH, as CSV time series 2012–2025 (https://www.web.statistik.zh.ch/ogd/data/steuerfuesse/kanton_zuerich_stf_timeseries.csv) plus a "current" file updated December–March (https://www.web.statistik.zh.ch/ogd/data/steuerfuesse/kanton_zuerich_stf_aktuell.csv). Licence: open use, source attribution required. — [opendata.swiss](https://ckan.opendata.swiss/dataset/steuerfusse-der-zurcher-gemeinden-fur-naturliche-und-juristische-personen)
- ESTV Steuerdokumentation publishes the capital-city multiplier tables (PDF) and the "Steuermäppchen" per topic (deductions, tariffs, Personalsteuer, etc.) for each tax period. — [ESTV-StF 2026](https://www.estv2.admin.ch/stp/ds/e-steuersatz-steuerfuss-de.pdf); [ESTV-PS 2025](https://www.estv2.admin.ch/stp/sm/personalsteuer-de-fr.pdf)
- Federal tariff source: ESTV "Form. 58c – Steuertarife direkte Bundessteuer natürliche Personen 2026". — [ESTV-StF 2026](https://www.estv2.admin.ch/stp/ds/e-steuersatz-steuerfuss-de.pdf)

### Inferences
- For an educational website, the cleanest legal route is to (a) link out to or embed the official ESTV calculator, (b) use ESTV figures with attribution for teaching, and (c) ask ESTV for permission before redistributing bulk API data or running a production proxy. Cantons with OGD multiplier datasets (ZH confirmed) can be used freely.
- For the API, the main entities are `TaxLocationID` (municipality + postcode), tax year, household inputs and income/wealth. Operations seen in community SDKs include location search, simple income/wealth calculation and rate/multiplier exports. Exact operation names beyond `API_searchLocation` should be taken from the SDK repos.

### Gaps
- No official ESTV API documentation, SLA or licence statement for the JSON API was found.
- Other cantons' OGD multiplier datasets (BE, LU, SG, AG, etc.) were not surveyed. BFS also publishes "Steuerbelastung"-related indicators, but those were not checked.
- Cantonal calculators (e.g. ZH "Steuerrechner", BE "Taxme", GE "Estimer ses impôts") were not catalogued.

## 6. Recent cantonal tax cuts and reforms, 2024–2026 (and announced for 2027)

### Takeaway
2025–2027 brought a wave of cuts for natural persons:
- **Zurich**: canton multiplier 98 → 95 % for 2026–27.
- **Zug**: 82 → 78 % for 2026–29.
- **Schwyz**: 115 → 110 % plus higher deductions in 2026.
- **Aargau**: 111 → 103 % in 2026.
- **Geneva**: average 8.7 % income-tax cut from 2025, approved by voters in November 2024.
- **Vaud**: voters accepted a 12 % cantonal tax cut on 27 September 2026, effective 2027.
- **Bern**: tariff flattening and lower tax planned from 2027.

### Cited Findings
- **Zurich (ZH)**: canton Steuerfuss lowered from 98 % to 95 % for 2026 and 2027, the largest cut in over 20 years (Kantonsrat budget debate, December 2025). This came on top of 99 → 98 % in 2024 and cold-progression indexing. New property valuation raises taxable wealth values. — [moneycab](https://www.moneycab.com/schweiz/zuercher-kantonsrat-senkt-den-steuerfuss-um-3-prozentpunkte/); [Tages-Anzeiger](https://www.tagesanzeiger.ch/zuerich-kantonsrat-stimmt-ueber-steuerfuss-und-budget-2026-ab-583050897244); [BAK 2026](https://www.zh.ch/content/dam/zhweb/bilder-dokumente/themen/steuern-finanzen/kantonsfinanzen/steuerbelastungsmonitor/BAK%20Economics_Zürcher_Steuerbelastungsmonitor_2026.pdf)
- **Zug (ZG)**: cantonal Steuerfuss cut from 82 % to 78 % for 2026–2029 (natural and legal persons). In 2024 Zug lowered wealth-tax rates and adjusted brackets, which made it rank 1 for wealth tax. — [abafin-treuhand](https://www.abafin-treuhand.ch/single-post/steuerliche-entlastungen-im-kanton-zug-ab-2026-was-sie-wissen-m%C3%BCssen); [BAK 2026](https://www.zh.ch/content/dam/zhweb/bilder-dokumente/themen/steuern-finanzen/kantonsfinanzen/steuerbelastungsmonitor/BAK%20Economics_Zürcher_Steuerbelastungsmonitor_2026.pdf); [BAK 2025](https://www.zh.ch/content/dam/zhweb/bilder-dokumente/themen/steuern-finanzen/kantonsfinanzen/steuerbelastungsmonitor/BAK%20Economics_Z%C3%BCrcher_Steuerbelastungsmonitor_2025.pdf)
- **Schwyz (SZ)**: canton Steuerfuss for natural persons cut from 115 % to 110 % plus higher deductions (2026). — [BAK 2026](https://www.zh.ch/content/dam/zhweb/bilder-dokumente/themen/steuern-finanzen/kantonsfinanzen/steuerbelastungsmonitor/BAK%20Economics_Zürcher_Steuerbelastungsmonitor_2026.pdf)
- **Aargau (AG)**: canton Steuerfuss for natural persons cut from 111 % to 103 % (2026). A 2027 revision (Steuerstrategie 2022–2030) is in parliament: low-earner deduction integrated into the tariff and top bracket reduced. Wealth-tariff cuts already moved AG up 8 places in the 2025 wealth ranking. — [BAK 2026](https://www.zh.ch/content/dam/zhweb/bilder-dokumente/themen/steuern-finanzen/kantonsfinanzen/steuerbelastungsmonitor/BAK%20Economics_Zürcher_Steuerbelastungsmonitor_2026.pdf)
- **Geneva (GE)**: an amendment to the LIPP cuts income tax by **8.7 % on average** from tax year 2025: up to 11.4 % for the middle class, 5.3 % for the highest incomes and 8.8 % for the lowest taxable incomes. Barème: maximum rate 11.54 % → 11.4 %, minimum 5.45 % → 5.3 %. Accepted in the November 2024 vote, it is the largest cut for natural persons since 1999. Wealth tariffs were also lowered (GE +2 places in the 2025 wealth index). — [Radio Lac](https://www.radiolac.ch/politique/les-genevois-paieront-moins-dimpots-en-2025/); [allnews.ch](https://allnews.ch/node/142737); [BAK 2026](https://www.zh.ch/content/dam/zhweb/bilder-dokumente/themen/steuern-finanzen/kantonsfinanzen/steuerbelastungsmonitor/BAK%20Economics_Zürcher_Steuerbelastungsmonitor_2026.pdf)
- **Vaud (VD)**:
  - The popular initiative "Baisse d'impôts pour toutes et tous" (German media: "Steuersenkungen für alle – Kaufkraft der Mittelschicht wiederherstellen") demands a **12 % cut in cantonal taxes** for natural persons (income, wealth and the tax on capital benefits from pensions). It was **accepted on 27 September 2026 with 53.1 % yes**, applicable from the 2027 tax period.
  - The government had pointed to an already-planned phased cantonal income-tax reduction (reported as "to 7 %", details unclear) from 2027.
  - — [watson.ch, 27 Sept 2026](https://www.watson.ch/waadt/abstimmungen-2026/495049760-abstimmung-im-kanton-waadt-alle-resultate-vom-27-september-2026)
  - Note: one search summary said "just over 50 %". The watson results page gives 53.1 %. The official canton result was not checked.
- **Bern (BE)**:
  - No new measure for 2026.
  - The Steuergesetzrevision 2027 relieves lower and middle incomes through higher deductions and a flatter tariff, and plans a lower cantonal tax rate from 2027.
  - Two variants were consulted: about CHF 190m relief at cantonal level (about 300m including municipalities), or CHF 130m (about 200m).
  - Wealth: married threshold doubled, CHF 18,000 social deduction abolished.
  - First reading in the Grand Council in autumn 2025.
  - — [Berner KMU, Vernehmlassung StG-Revision 2027](https://www.bernerkmu.ch/uploads/bernerkmu/Vernehmlassungen/Steuergesetzrevision-2027.pdf); [swissinfo](https://www.swissinfo.ch/ger/kanton-bern-budgetiert-%c3%9cberschuss-und-will-steuern-weiter-senken/89876892); [KPMG Berner Steuermonitor 2025](https://kpmg.com/ch/de/medien/medienmitteilungen/2025/10/bern-tax-monitor.html)
- **Other 2024–2025 moves** (from the BAK rankings):
  - Ticino (2024): tariff reform, lower top cantonal rate, higher allowances.
  - St. Gallen (2024): tariff shift, higher child deductions.
  - Glarus (2024): higher partial-splitting factor and child deduction.
  - Schaffhausen (2024): lower cantonal Steuerfuss.
  - Lucerne (2024): lower wealth rate; 2025 tariff and deductions.
  - Obwalden (2025): lower cantonal Steuerfuss, but Sarnen's municipal rate rose in 2024 for flood protection.
  - Nidwalden (2024): expiry of the Steuerrabatt.
  - — [BAK 2025](https://www.zh.ch/content/dam/zhweb/bilder-dokumente/themen/steuern-finanzen/kantonsfinanzen/steuerbelastungsmonitor/BAK%20Economics_Z%C3%BCrcher_Steuerbelastungsmonitor_2025.pdf); [BAK 2026](https://www.zh.ch/content/dam/zhweb/bilder-dokumente/themen/steuern-finanzen/kantonsfinanzen/steuerbelastungsmonitor/BAK%20Economics_Zürcher_Steuerbelastungsmonitor_2026.pdf)

### Inferences
- Because ESTV burden statistics lag (2025 is the latest), a site showing "current" comparisons should either compute 2026 figures via the calculator API or label figures by year. The 2026 cuts in ZH, ZG, SZ and AG are not in the 2025 rankings.
- The 2027 changes in Vaud (12 %) and Bern will likely move both cantons up from the bottom of the ranking, but this cannot be quantified yet.

### Gaps
- The Bern Grand Council's final decision (second reading, 2026) on the 2027 revision and the new Steueranlage was not verified.
- The details and cost of the Vaud implementation (whether via Steuerfuss/coefficient or tariff) and its interaction with the government's own counter-plan were not verified on vd.ch.
- Basel-Stadt, Lucerne and St. Gallen 2026/27 natural-person reforms were not specifically researched.
- The exact date of Zurich's Kantonsrat decision (December 2025) comes from news headlines; the date of the official Kantonsrat protocol was not checked.
