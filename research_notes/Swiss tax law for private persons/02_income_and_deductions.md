# Swiss income taxation of private persons: taxable income and the deduction catalogue

Status: research as of 3 October 2026. "TY" = tax year (Steuerjahr / Steuerperiode). The federal level is the direct federal tax (direkte Bundessteuer, DBSt), governed by the DBG (SR 642.11). Cantonal and communal taxes follow the StHG framework but set their own amounts. ## 1. Taxable income types (DBG Art. 16–23) and tax-free income (Art. 24)

### Takeaway
Swiss income tax works on a "Reineinkommen" principle. All recurring and one-off income is taxable (Art. 16), including payments in kind valued at market value. The main exceptions are private capital gains, inheritances and gifts, and a closed list of exemptions in Art. 24. For employees, the Lohnausweis (Form 11) is the core document. Its numbered fields (Ziffern 1–15) and letter boxes (A–I, especially F and G) feed directly into the tax return and decide which deductions are allowed.

### Cited Findings

**General principle (Art. 16 DBG)**
- Art. 16 Abs. 1: "Der Einkommenssteuer unterliegen alle wiederkehrenden und einmaligen Einkünfte." Abs. 2: payments in kind (Naturalbezüge), e.g. free board and lodging, are income valued at market value. Abs. 3: "Die Kapitalgewinne aus der Veräusserung von Privatvermögen sind steuerfrei." — [DBG, Fedlex](https://www.fedlex.admin.ch/eli/cc/1991/1184_1184_1184/de)

**Employment income (Unselbständige Erwerbstätigkeit, Art. 17–17d)**
- Art. 17 Abs. 1: all income from private- or public-law employment is taxable, "mit Einschluss der Nebeneinkünfte wie Entschädigungen für Sonderleistungen, Provisionen, Zulagen, Dienstalters- und Jubiläumsgeschenke, Gratifikationen, Trinkgelder, Tantiemen, geldwerte Vorteile aus Mitarbeiterbeteiligungen und andere geldwerte Vorteile" (bonuses, commissions, allowances, tips, fringe benefits). — [DBG](https://www.fedlex.admin.ch/eli/cc/1991/1184_1184_1184/de)
- Art. 17 Abs. 1bis: employer-paid costs of job-related training and retraining (berufsorientierte Aus- und Weiterbildung, Umschulung) are, regardless of amount, not a taxable benefit. — [DBG](https://www.fedlex.admin.ch/eli/cc/1991/1184_1184_1184/de)
- Art. 17 Abs. 2: lump-sum payments from an employer-linked pension fund are taxed separately under Art. 38. — [DBG](https://www.fedlex.admin.ch/eli/cc/1991/1184_1184_1184/de)
- Employee shares and options (Art. 17a–17d):
  - Unrestricted employee shares are taxed on acquisition, on market value minus the price paid.
  - Blocked shares get a 6% discount per blocking year, for at most 10 years (Art. 17b Abs. 2).
  - Blocked or unlisted options are taxed when exercised (Art. 17b Abs. 3).
  - Phantom or "unechte" participations are taxed when they flow to the employee (Art. 17c).
  — [DBG](https://www.fedlex.admin.ch/eli/cc/1991/1184_1184_1184/de)

**Lohnausweis (salary certificate, Formular 11), per the official Wegleitung valid from 1.1.2026**
- **Box F (Unentgeltliche Beförderung zwischen Wohn- und Arbeitsort):** ticked when the employee has no commuting costs. Typical cases:
  - a company car, unless the employee pays at least 75 Rappen per km (or at least cost) for the commute;
  - group transport;
  - field staff driving from home directly to customers;
  - a GA travelcard needed for business.

  A GA given without business need is declared at market value in Ziffer 2.3, and F is then not ticked. A Halbtax (half-fare card) paid by the employer need not be declared. — [Wegleitung 2026, Rz 9](https://www.estv.admin.ch/dam/de/sd-web/afP1GDFr8gE3/dbst-form-lohna-wegleitung-2026-de.pdf)
- **Box G (Kantinenverpflegung/Lunch-Checks):**
  - Ticked if Lunch-Checks are given, or if the employer offers subsidised meals in a staff restaurant, "auch dann, wenn nicht bekannt ist, ob der Arbeitnehmer davon tatsächlich Gebrauch macht".
  - Also ticked for employees who work 40–60% of their time away from their usual workplace and receive a lunch allowance.
  - Above 60% away, only the Ziffer 15 note "Mittagessen durch Arbeitgeber bezahlt" is used.
  - Lunch-Checks up to the AHV limit (CHF 180/month, as of 1.1.2022) are flagged by G; any excess is added to Ziffer 1.

  — [Wegleitung 2026, Rz 10, 18](https://www.estv.admin.ch/dam/de/sd-web/afP1GDFr8gE3/dbst-form-lohna-wegleitung-2026-de.pdf)
- **Ziffer 1 (Lohn):** salary plus all allowances (family/child allowances, shift, on-call, night and Sunday allowances), commissions, cash lunch allowances, insurance daily allowances paid via the employer, and commuting reimbursements. Commuting reimbursements are declared as a professional-cost allowance in 2.3, and then F is not ticked. — [Wegleitung 2026, Rz 13–18](https://www.estv.admin.ch/dam/de/sd-web/afP1GDFr8gE3/dbst-form-lohna-wegleitung-2026-de.pdf)
- **Ziffer 2 (Gehaltsnebenleistungen / fringe benefits):** valued at market value.
  - 2.1: free board and lodging, valued per ESTV Merkblatt N2.
  - 2.2 (Privatanteil Geschäftsfahrzeug): 0.9% of the purchase price (excl. VAT, incl. extras) per month, minimum CHF 150/month. Example: car worth CHF 43,000 gives CHF 4,644 per year (12 × 387). Alternatively, with a logbook: private km including the commute × 75 Rp (e.g. 8,500 km × 0.75 = CHF 6,375). No add-back if the employee bears substantial costs (paying only fuel or charging does not count), or if private use is substantially restricted. "In allen Fällen ist … das Feld F … anzukreuzen."
  - 2.3: other benefits, e.g. a free flat at local market rent, or a GA without business need.

  — [Wegleitung 2026, Rz 19–26](https://www.estv.admin.ch/dam/de/sd-web/afP1GDFr8gE3/dbst-form-lohna-wegleitung-2026-de.pdf)
- **Ziffer 3 (Unregelmässige Leistungen):** bonuses, sign-on and exit payments, loyalty and long-service gifts, flat-rate moving allowances. Contractual 13th/14th salaries belong in Ziffer 1. **Ziffer 4:** lump sums (Kapitalleistungen), e.g. severance pay with pension character and back-pay. Pension-fund lump sums are reported on Form 563, not on the Lohnausweis. **Ziffer 5:** employee participations. **Ziffer 6:** board fees (Verwaltungsratsentschädigungen). **Ziffer 7:** other benefits, e.g.:
  - tips, if a substantial part of pay;
  - insurance daily allowances and ALV/EO benefits paid via the employer;
  - employee BVG contributions paid by the employer;
  - employer-paid health or 3b insurance premiums;
  - employer 3a contributions;
  - source taxes paid by the employer;
  - school fees for children.

  — [Wegleitung 2026, Rz 27–40](https://www.estv.admin.ch/dam/de/sd-web/afP1GDFr8gE3/dbst-form-lohna-wegleitung-2026-de.pdf)
- **Ziffer 8** = gross total (Ziffern 1–7). **Ziffer 9** = employee AHV/IV/EO/ALV/NBUV contributions. Daily-sickness insurance and UVG top-up premiums may not be deducted here. **Ziffer 10.1** = ordinary BVG contributions. **Ziffer 10.2** = BVG buy-ins deducted from salary. Buy-ins paid privately are certified by the pension fund on Form 21 EDP. **Ziffer 11** = net salary (the figure carried to the tax return). **Ziffer 12** = source tax withheld. — [Wegleitung 2026, Rz 41–48](https://www.estv.admin.ch/dam/de/sd-web/afP1GDFr8gE3/dbst-form-lohna-wegleitung-2026-de.pdf)
- **Ziffer 13 (Spesenvergütungen), not part of gross salary:**
  - 13.1 Effektive Spesen. No amount is needed (only a cross in 13.1.1) if all of these hold:
    - overnight stays are reimbursed against receipt;
    - meals cost at most CHF 35 effective, or at most CHF 30 as a flat rate per main meal;
    - customer invitations are settled against receipts;
    - public transport is reimbursed against receipt;
    - private car use is paid at most 75 Rp/km;
    - small expenses are paid at most CHF 20/day.
  - 13.1.2: other actual expenses. This explicitly includes reimbursements for an external workplace such as home office or co-working, with the note «Spesen für externen Arbeitsplatz».
  - 13.2 Pauschalspesen: must always be stated as an amount, even under an approved expense policy. Sub-fields: 13.2.1 representation, 13.2.2 car, 13.2.3 other (e.g. home-office flat contribution).
  - 13.3: employer payments for training made to the employee. Payments made directly to the training institute are not stated, unless the invoice is in the employee's name.
  - Commuting allowances are never Spesen. They are always added to gross salary.

  — [Wegleitung 2026, Rz 49–61](https://www.estv.admin.ch/dam/de/sd-web/afP1GDFr8gE3/dbst-form-lohna-wegleitung-2026-de.pdf)
- **Ziffer 14** = non-valued fringe benefits (no amount); discounts up to CHF 2,500/year need no mention. **Ziffer 15** = remarks, e.g.:
  - approved expense policy («Spesenreglement durch Kanton XY … genehmigt»);
  - days with income-replacement allowances;
  - part-time work;
  - number of certificates.

  Not to be declared at all (Rz 72):
  - free Halbtax;
  - discounts on third-party products up to 20% and CHF 600/year;
  - customary in-kind gifts up to CHF 600/year (cash gifts are always salary).

  — [Wegleitung 2026, Rz 62–72](https://www.estv.admin.ch/dam/de/sd-web/afP1GDFr8gE3/dbst-form-lohna-wegleitung-2026-de.pdf)
- The 2026 Wegleitung was updated mainly for the higher km rate (70 → 75 Rp) and for clarifications on Box F. — [law.ch summary, Jan 2026](https://law.ch/lawnews/2026/01/autokilometeransatz-regeln-ab-01-01-2026/)

**Self-employment (Selbständige Erwerbstätigkeit, Art. 18–19, 27–31), overview only**
- Business expenses that are commercially justified are deductible (Art. 27). This includes:
  - depreciation (Art. 28) and provisions (Art. 29);
  - losses on business assets;
  - business interest;
  - staff training.

  Fines with a penal purpose are not deductible. Losses can be carried forward for 7 years (Art. 31). When a self-employed person aged 55+ (or invalid) gives up the business, the liquidation gain is taxed separately at reduced rates (Art. 37b). — [DBG](https://www.fedlex.admin.ch/eli/cc/1991/1184_1184_1184/de)

**Investment income (Ertrag aus beweglichem Vermögen, Art. 20; unbewegliches Vermögen, Art. 21)**
- Taxable (Art. 20 Abs. 1):
  - interest on deposits;
  - income from single-premium insurance, unless it serves pension purposes (paid at age 60+, contract at least 5 years, begun before age 66: then tax-free);
  - income from discount and global bonds;
  - dividends, liquidation surpluses and bonus shares;
  - fund income (unless it comes from direct real estate);
  - rental of movable property and income from IP.

  Dividends from holdings of at least 10% are taxable at 70% (Art. 20 Abs. 1bis). Repayments of capital-contribution reserves (KER) are treated like a return of capital (Abs. 3). — [DBG](https://www.fedlex.admin.ch/eli/cc/1991/1184_1184_1184/de)
- Real estate (Art. 21): taxable are rental income and the imputed rental value of owner-occupied property (Eigenmietwert, Abs. 1 lit. b, set with regard to local conditions and actual use, Abs. 2), plus building-right fees and gravel extraction. — [DBG](https://www.fedlex.admin.ch/eli/cc/1991/1184_1184_1184/de)
- **Abolition of the Eigenmietwert:** voters accepted it on 28 Sep 2025 (57.7% yes). According to press reports, the Federal Council set entry into force for 1 Jan 2029. Until the end of 2028 the Eigenmietwert stays taxable, and mortgage-interest and maintenance deductions remain. From 2029:
  - maintenance deductions on owner-occupied property are dropped;
  - mortgage-interest deductions are heavily restricted.

  — [law.ch](https://law.ch/lawnews/2025/09/abschaffung-des-eigenmietwertes-fuer-selbstbewohntes-grundeigentum/); [Bilanz](https://www.bilanz.ch/invest/immobilien/eigenmietwert-wird-2029-abgeschafft/zdkh62c); [ZKB fact sheet](https://www.zkb.ch/media/zkb/dokumente/merkblaetter/FS_Systemwechsel_Wohneigentumsbesteuerung_09-25%20(1).pdf)

**Pension and annuity income (Einkünfte aus Vorsorge, Art. 22)**
- Art. 22 Abs. 1: taxable are "alle Einkünfte aus der Alters-, Hinterlassenen- und Invalidenversicherung [AHV/IV], aus Einrichtungen der beruflichen Vorsorge [BVG] und aus anerkannten Formen der gebundenen Selbstvorsorge [3a], mit Einschluss der Kapitalabfindungen und Rückzahlungen". AHV/IV and BVG pensions are therefore 100% taxable as ordinary income. — [DBG](https://www.fedlex.admin.ch/eli/cc/1991/1184_1184_1184/de)
- Lump-sum withdrawals from the 2nd pillar and pillar 3a (and payments on death or permanent injury) are taxed separately from other income, at one fifth of the Art. 36 tariff, with no social deductions (Art. 38 Abs. 1–3). — [DBG](https://www.fedlex.admin.ch/eli/cc/1991/1184_1184_1184/de)
- **Life annuities (Leibrenten, Art. 22 Abs. 3, new from 1.1.2025):** before this, a flat 40% was taxable. Now only the "Ertragsanteil" (income share) is taxable:
  - guaranteed Swiss VVG annuity benefits: share follows the maximum technical interest rate at contract signing (0% if that rate is 0 or negative);
  - surplus benefits: 70%;
  - foreign annuities and private annuity contracts: share based on the 10-year Swiss federal bond yield plus 0.5 percentage points, averaged over 10 years.

  The payer may deduct the same Ertragsanteil (Art. 33 Abs. 1 lit. b). — [DBG](https://www.fedlex.admin.ch/eli/cc/1991/1184_1184_1184/de); [Kanton AG Merkblatt Renten 2025](https://ag.ch/media/kanton-aargau/dfr/dokumente/steuern/natuerliche-personen/merkblaetter-zu-steuern-natuerliche-personen/besteuerung-von-renten-und-pensionen-2025.pdf); ESTV page on rates: https://www.estv.admin.ch/de/steuerbarer-ertragsanteil-von-auslaendischen-leibrentenversicherungen-sowie-von-leibrenten-und-verpfruendungsvertraegen

**Other income (Art. 23)**
- Taxable:
  - all income replacing employment income (lit. a), e.g. unemployment, sickness, accident and maternity daily allowances (Taggelder);
  - payments on death or for permanent injury (lit. b);
  - compensation for giving up an activity or a right (lit. c, d);
  - **alimony received** by a divorced or separated spouse for themselves, and child alimony received by a parent for children under their parental care (lit. f).

  — [DBG](https://www.fedlex.admin.ch/eli/cc/1991/1184_1184_1184/de)
- The Lohnausweis Wegleitung confirms that daily allowances (KTG, UVG, IV, maternity), ALV benefits (incl. short-time work) and EO are reported as salary in Ziffer 1 or 7 if paid via the employer. — [Wegleitung 2026, Rz 14, 33–35](https://www.estv.admin.ch/dam/de/sd-web/afP1GDFr8gE3/dbst-form-lohna-wegleitung-2026-de.pdf)

**Tax-free income (Steuerfreie Einkünfte, Art. 24)**
- Tax-free are:
  - (a) inheritance, legacy, gift and matrimonial-property settlements (these are subject to cantonal inheritance/gift tax instead);
  - (b) payouts from surrenderable private capital insurance (except vested-benefit policies);
  - (c) vested-benefit payments on job change, if reinvested within a year;
  - (d) support payments from public or private funds;
  - (e) payments fulfilling family-law duties (except alimony under Art. 23 f);
  - (f) **military and civil-protection pay (Sold) and civil-service pocket money**;
  - (fbis) **militia firefighter pay** for core duties up to **CHF 5,300 (TY 2025) / CHF 5,400 (TY 2026)**;
  - (g) compensation for pain and suffering (Genugtuung);
  - (h) supplementary benefits (Ergänzungsleistungen AHV/IV);
  - (i) casino winnings in licensed Swiss casinos;
  - (ibis) single winnings from licensed large-scale games (lotteries) and online casino games up to **CHF 1,070,400 (TY 2025) / CHF 1,071,000 (TY 2026)**;
  - (iter) winnings from licensed small games;
  - (j) promotional lottery winnings up to **CHF 1,100**;
  - (k) bridging benefits for older unemployed people.

  — [DBG](https://www.fedlex.admin.ch/eli/cc/1991/1184_1184_1184/de); [ESTV circular TY 2025](https://www.estv.admin.ch/dam/de/sd-web/zkUbFt0fCQiy/dbst-rs-2-210-d-2024-de.pdf); [ESTV circular TY 2026](https://www.estv.admin.ch/dam/de/sd-web/vFK3ntWLQ4s4/2-215-D-2025-d.pdf)
- Taxable gambling winnings above the exemption get a stake-cost deduction (Art. 33 Abs. 4): 5% of the win, capped at CHF 5,400 (2025 and 2026). For online casino games, actual stakes are deductible up to CHF 26,800 (2025 and 2026). — [DBG](https://www.fedlex.admin.ch/eli/cc/1991/1184_1184_1184/de); [ESTV circular TY 2026](https://www.estv.admin.ch/dam/de/sd-web/vFK3ntWLQ4s4/2-215-D-2025-d.pdf)

### Inferences
- Scholarships (Stipendien) are not named explicitly in Art. 24. In practice they are generally exempt as "Unterstützungen aus öffentlichen oder privaten Mitteln" (lit. d). Treat this as interpretation; cantonal guides should confirm it.
- Box F matters for the return. Combined with Berufskostenverordnung Art. 5a (see Section 2), a ticked F means the taxpayer generally cannot claim a separate commuting deduction for the company-car commute. Box G cuts the meal deduction in half (BKV Art. 6 Abs. 2).
- For a "line by line" site, the natural mapping is: Lohnausweis Ziffer 11 → salary line on the return. Ziffer 10.2 shows buy-ins already deducted from salary, which must not be claimed twice. Ziffer 13 Spesen are not income but may lower the professional-expense deductions.

### Gaps
- Exact Ertragsanteil percentages for foreign annuities for 2025 and 2026 were not retrieved; see the ESTV page linked above.
- Transitional rules for BVG pensions that started before 1987 or 2002 (partial taxation under DBG Art. 204) were not checked.
- The full Art. 18 (self-employment) text was not extracted. Only the expense rules (Art. 27–31) were verified.

## 2. Deductions with current amounts (TY 2025 and TY 2026; federal, with cantonal ranges)

### Takeaway
At federal level, nearly all amounts are identical for TY 2025 and TY 2026. Inflation from June 2024 to June 2025 was only 0.06%, so deductions were not adjusted for 2026. There are three exceptions:
- the car km rate for commuting rises from CHF 0.70 to **CHF 0.75**;
- the firefighter-pay exemption rises to CHF 5,400;
- the lottery exemption rises to CHF 1,071,000.

The 2025 inflation adjustment (+1.31%) had raised most amounts compared with 2024, e.g. the commuting cap from 3,200 to 3,300 and the child deduction from 6,700 to 6,800. Cantonal amounts differ widely. Commuting caps, for example, run from CHF 3,200 (BS) to unlimited (GL, AI, GR, TI, VD, VS, NE, JU).

**Berufskosten (professional expenses of employees), Art. 26 DBG and Berufskostenverordnung (BKV)**
- Fahrkosten (BKV Art. 5):
  - Public transport costs are deductible.
  - A private vehicle may be claimed per km only "sofern kein öffentliches Verkehrsmittel zur Verfügung steht oder dessen Benützung objektiv nicht zumutbar ist".
  - The federal cap is CHF 3,300.
  - Company car (Art. 5a): for free private use including the commute, a flat-rate method may replace the actual-cost method. Under it, 0.9% of the purchase price per month counts as income, and there is no separate commuting deduction.

  — [BKV](https://www.fedlex.admin.ch/eli/cc/1993/1363_1363_1363/de)
- Verpflegung (BKV Art. 6): only the flat rate is allowed, and only if the taxpayer cannot eat the main meal at home (distance or short break) or works continuous shift or night work. Only half is allowed if the employer subsidises meals (vouchers, canteen; this corresponds to Box G). Nothing is allowed if meals are priced below the official in-kind rates. It cannot be combined with the Wochenaufenthalt meal deduction. — [BKV](https://www.fedlex.admin.ch/eli/cc/1993/1363_1363_1363/de)
- Übrige Berufskosten (BKV Art. 7): one flat rate of 3% of net salary (min. 2,000, max. 4,000) covers "Berufswerkzeuge (inkl. EDV-Hard- und -Software), Fachliteratur, **privates Arbeitszimmer**, Berufskleider, besonderen Schuh- und Kleiderverschleiss sowie Schwerarbeit". Higher actual costs may be claimed with full proof (Art. 4). The flat rate is reduced for part-year or part-time work. — [BKV](https://www.fedlex.admin.ch/eli/cc/1993/1363_1363_1363/de)
- Home office: at federal level, the home office is part of "übrige Berufskosten" (BKV Art. 7). An actual-cost deduction requires proof. Employer reimbursements for a home office appear in Lohnausweis Ziffer 13.1.2 or 13.2.3. Canton FR reduces the commuting deduction pro rata for telework days. — [BKV](https://www.fedlex.admin.ch/eli/cc/1993/1363_1363_1363/de); [Wegleitung 2026 Rz 54, 58](https://www.estv.admin.ch/dam/de/sd-web/afP1GDFr8gE3/dbst-form-lohna-wegleitung-2026-de.pdf); [Steuermäppchen Fahrkosten 2025](https://www.estv2.admin.ch/stp/sm/fahrkosten-de-fr.pdf)
- Weekly residence away from home (Wochenaufenthalt, BKV Art. 9): deductible are local room costs, travel home, and meal flat rates (see table). — [BKV](https://www.fedlex.admin.ch/eli/cc/1993/1363_1363_1363/de)
- Training (Aus-/Weiterbildung, Art. 33 Abs. 1 lit. j): job-related training and retraining up to CHF 13,000 (2025/2026). Condition: a first upper-secondary qualification (Sek II, e.g. apprenticeship or Matura) exists, or the person is 20+ and the costs are not for reaching that first Sek II qualification. — [DBG](https://www.fedlex.admin.ch/eli/cc/1991/1184_1184_1184/de)

**Pillar 3a (gebundene Selbstvorsorge, Art. 33 Abs. 1 lit. e; BVV 3)**
- Maximum deductible contributions for **2025 and 2026**: **CHF 7,258** with a pension fund (8% of the BVG upper limit of CHF 90,720). Without a pension fund: **CHF 36,288**, capped at 20% of net earned income. These are unchanged from 2025 to 2026; in 2024 they were 7,056 / 35,280. — [BSV BPP Zahlen 2026](https://www.bsv.admin.ch/dam/de/sd-web/3jZGqTLgADbl/BPP_Zahlen_85_2026.pdf); [UBS](https://www.ubs.com/ch/de/help/pension/maximal-contribution-2026.html); [comparis](https://www.comparis.ch/altersvorsorge/saeule-3a/info/maximalbetrag)
- **Retroactive 3a purchases (Nachzahlung/Einkauf), BVV 3 amendment in force 1.1.2025:**
  - Gaps can be filled for up to 10 years back, but only for gap years from 2025 onwards. The first possible purchase is in 2026, for 2025.
  - Per gap year, at most the "small" maximum (CHF 7,258) may be bought in.
  - The current year's full ordinary contribution must be paid first.
  - AHV-liable earned income in Switzerland is required both in the gap year and in the purchase year.
  - The purchase is fully deductible.
  - Example: someone who last paid in 2020 can close only the 2025 gap in 2026, not 2021–2024.

  — [law.ch Nov 2024](https://law.ch/lawnews/2024/11/nachtraegliche-einkaufsmoeglichkeit-in-die-saeule-3a-inkrafttreten-am-01-01-2025/); [Raiffeisen](https://www.raiffeisen.ch/winterthur/de/ueber-uns/news/rb/3a-einkaufsmoeglichkeit-202411.html); [weka](https://www.weka.ch/themen/personal/sozialversicherungen/berufliche-vorsorge/article/saeule-3a-einkauf-so-funktioniert-die-nachzahlung/)
- Employer-paid 3a contributions are salary (Lohnausweis Ziffer 7). The foundation certifies them on Form 21 EDP, and the employee deducts them in the return. — [Wegleitung 2026, Rz 38](https://www.estv.admin.ch/dam/de/sd-web/afP1GDFr8gE3/dbst-form-lohna-wegleitung-2026-de.pdf)

**2nd pillar contributions and buy-ins (Einkäufe in die 2. Säule, Art. 33 Abs. 1 lit. d)**
- Contributions to AHV/IV and to occupational pension funds paid "gemäss Gesetz, Statut oder Reglement" are fully deductible. This includes voluntary buy-ins up to the regulatory maximum. — [DBG](https://www.fedlex.admin.ch/eli/cc/1991/1184_1184_1184/de)
- **3-year lock (BVG Art. 79b Abs. 3):** benefits resulting from a buy-in may not be withdrawn as capital within 3 years. Under tax practice, the deduction is denied (or the assessment revised) if capital is withdrawn within that period. Repaying an early withdrawal for home ownership (WEF) is an exception under the law. — [Zug Steuerbuch BVG](https://zgchtest.webcloud7.ch/izug/platform/behoerden/finanzdirektion/steuerverwaltung/steuerbuch-zug/erlaeuterung-zu-a7-30-allgemeine-abzuege-unabhaengig-von-der-einkommenshoehe/beitraege-an-die-berufliche-vorsorge-saeule-2a-und-2b/export_pdf); [Aargau Merkblatt Einkauf 2026](https://www.ag.ch/media/kanton-aargau/dfr/dokumente/steuern/natuerliche-personen/merkblaetter-zu-steuern-natuerliche-personen/einkauf-von-beitragsjahren-s-ule-2-2026.pdf)
- Also deductible: EO, ALV and compulsory accident-insurance premiums (lit. f). These are normally already netted on the Lohnausweis. — [DBG](https://www.fedlex.admin.ch/eli/cc/1991/1184_1184_1184/de)

**Insurance premiums and savings interest (Versicherungsprämien und Sparzinsen, Art. 33 Abs. 1 lit. g, Abs. 1bis)**
- A single combined cap covers life, health and non-compulsory accident insurance premiums plus interest on savings capital. Caps for 2025/2026:
  - married: 3,700 (or 5,550 without 2nd/3a contributions);
  - others: 1,800 (or 2,700 without);
  - plus 700 per child or supported person.

  The "without" amounts are 50% higher (Abs. 1bis lit. a). — [DBG](https://www.fedlex.admin.ch/eli/cc/1991/1184_1184_1184/de); [ESTV circular TY 2026](https://www.estv.admin.ch/dam/de/sd-web/vFK3ntWLQ4s4/2-215-D-2025-d.pdf)

**Debt interest (Schuldzinsen, Art. 33 Abs. 1 lit. a)**
- Private debt interest (e.g. mortgage or consumer loans) is deductible up to the amount of taxable investment income (Art. 20, 20a, 21, which includes the Eigenmietwert) **plus CHF 50,000**. Loans from a closely held company on non-arm's-length terms are excluded. — [DBG](https://www.fedlex.admin.ch/eli/cc/1991/1184_1184_1184/de)

**Alimony paid (Unterhaltsbeiträge, Art. 33 Abs. 1 lit. c)**
- Deductible: alimony to a divorced or separated spouse, and child alimony paid to the other parent for children under that parent's parental care. Other family-law support obligations are not deductible (e.g. support for adult children or parents; for these, see the Unterstützungsabzug). This mirrors taxation at the recipient (Art. 23 lit. f). — [DBG](https://www.fedlex.admin.ch/eli/cc/1991/1184_1184_1184/de)

**Family deductions**
- Child deduction (Kinderabzug, Art. 35 Abs. 1 lit. a): CHF 6,800 (2025/2026) per minor child or child in education whom the taxpayer supports. Parents taxed separately split it 50/50 under joint custody when no child alimony is deducted. Social deductions follow the situation at the end of the tax period (Art. 35 Abs. 2). — [DBG](https://www.fedlex.admin.ch/eli/cc/1991/1184_1184_1184/de)
- Support deduction (Unterstützungsabzug): CHF 6,800 per person unable or partly unable to work whom the taxpayer supports with at least that amount. Not available for a spouse, or for children already covered by the child deduction. — [DBG](https://www.fedlex.admin.ch/eli/cc/1991/1184_1184_1184/de)
- Married-couple deduction (Verheiratetenabzug): CHF 2,800 (2025/2026). — [DBG](https://www.fedlex.admin.ch/eli/cc/1991/1184_1184_1184/de)
- Two-earner deduction (Zweiverdienerabzug, Art. 33 Abs. 2): 50% of the lower earned income, minimum 8,600 and maximum 14,100 (2025/2026). Earned income here means income after professional costs and lit. d–f deductions. If one spouse works substantially in the other's business, the joint earned income is split half each. — [DBG](https://www.fedlex.admin.ch/eli/cc/1991/1184_1184_1184/de)
- Third-party childcare (Kinderdrittbetreuungskosten, Art. 33 Abs. 3): proven costs up to **CHF 25,800 per child** (2025/2026) for children under 14 living in the household. The costs must be causally linked to the parent's work, education or inability to work. — [DBG](https://www.fedlex.admin.ch/eli/cc/1991/1184_1184_1184/de)
- Parent tariff and child credit (Elterntarif and Abzug vom Steuerbetrag, Art. 36 Abs. 2bis): single parents and married couples living with children they mainly support use the married tariff. The resulting tax is then reduced by **CHF 263 per child or supported person** (2025 and 2026; CHF 259 in 2024). — [DBG](https://www.fedlex.admin.ch/eli/cc/1991/1184_1184_1184/de); [ESTV circular TY 2025](https://www.estv.admin.ch/dam/de/sd-web/zkUbFt0fCQiy/dbst-rs-2-210-d-2024-de.pdf)

**Health, disability, donations, parties**
- Sickness and accident costs (Krankheits- und Unfallkosten, Art. 33 Abs. 1 lit. h): self-borne costs for the taxpayer and dependants are deductible only above **5%** of net income after deductions under Art. 26–33. Disability costs (Behinderungskosten, lit. hbis) are deductible in full, with no threshold. — [DBG](https://www.fedlex.admin.ch/eli/cc/1991/1184_1184_1184/de)
- Donations (freiwillige Leistungen / Spenden, Art. 33a): gifts to tax-exempt Swiss charitable or public-purpose entities, and to the Confederation, cantons and communes, are deductible if they reach **CHF 100** in the year, up to **20%** of net income after Art. 26–33 deductions. — [DBG](https://www.fedlex.admin.ch/eli/cc/1991/1184_1184_1184/de)
- Political parties (Parteispenden, Art. 33 Abs. 1 lit. i): membership fees and donations up to **CHF 10,600** (2025/2026). The party must be registered, represented in a cantonal parliament, or have reached at least 3% of the vote in the last cantonal election. — [DBG](https://www.fedlex.admin.ch/eli/cc/1991/1184_1184_1184/de)

**Real estate maintenance (Liegenschaftsunterhalt, Art. 32 DBG)**
- Deductible for private real estate:
  - maintenance costs;
  - costs of repairing newly acquired property;
  - insurance premiums;
  - third-party management costs;
  - energy-saving and environmental investments (as defined by the EFD), with a carry-forward over 2 further periods;
  - demolition costs for a replacement new build;
  - mandatory heritage-protection work.

  Instead of actual costs, a flat rate (Pauschalabzug) may be chosen per property (Abs. 4). At federal level it is **10%** of gross rent or Eigenmietwert for buildings up to 10 years old and **20%** for older buildings. The choice can be made per property and per year. Business property allows only actual costs. — [DBG](https://www.fedlex.admin.ch/eli/cc/1991/1184_1184_1184/de); [ESTV Steuermäppchen Liegenschaftsunterhalt](https://www.estv.admin.ch/dam/estv/de/dokumente/estv/steuersystem/steuermaeppchen/steuermaeppchen-2019-liegenschaftsunterhaltskosten.pdf.download.pdf/steuermaeppchen-2019-liegenschaftsunterhaltskosten.pdf); [AI Merkblatt](https://www.ai.ch/themen/steuern/publikationen/merkblaetter-gueltig-ab/merkblatt-liegenschaftsunterhalt.pdf)
- Movable private assets: third-party asset-management costs (e.g. custody fees) and foreign withholding taxes that are neither refundable nor creditable are deductible. — [DBG](https://www.fedlex.admin.ch/eli/cc/1991/1184_1184_1184/de)
- Not deductible (Art. 34):
  - living costs;
  - debt repayment;
  - acquisition or value-enhancing costs;
  - income and wealth taxes.

  — [DBG](https://www.fedlex.admin.ch/eli/cc/1991/1184_1184_1184/de)

### Inferences
- For TY 2025 vs 2026 the site can show one federal table with only three changed cells (car km rate, firefighter exemption, lottery exemption). Because the km rate rose, cars hit the CHF 3,300 cap slightly sooner in 2026: after about 4,400 km per year (3,300 / 0.75) instead of about 4,714 km (3,300 / 0.70). That is only about 10 km one way over 220 working days, so most car commuters hit the federal cap. The higher rate matters more for the cantonal tax where caps are higher or unlimited.
- The Versicherungsabzug (1,800 single / 3,700 married) is usually used up by compulsory health-insurance premiums alone. That makes savings interest effectively irrelevant for most people.
- The 2nd pillar buy-in is the largest lever: unlimited in amount up to the regulatory gap, and fully deductible. The 3-year lock means that people planning a capital withdrawal at retirement must time buy-ins at least 3 years earlier.

- The official ESTV/BSV wording on the Art. 7a BVV 3 retroactive-purchase conditions was not fetched directly. Secondary sources (law.ch, banks) were used. Not verified: whether a 3a purchase is barred after drawing an old-age benefit or within 5 years of retirement.
- There is no federal Kreisschreiben on home office. The quantitative criteria some cantons use for a separate "Arbeitszimmer" deduction could not be confirmed from an official source; one search snippet mentioned "two full days / 40%", but its source was unclear.
- Cantonal 3a, Zweiverdiener, Kinderbetreuung and donation caps were not collected. Only commuting, child and sickness costs were sampled across cantons.

## 3. Federal income tax tariff (DBG Art. 36), TY 2025 and TY 2026

### Takeaway
The DBSt tariff is progressive. It starts at CHF 0 tax up to CHF 15,200 of taxable income (single) or CHF 29,700 (married). It reaches the flat maximum marginal rate of 11.5%, applied to the whole income, from CHF 794,000 (single, TY 2026) or CHF 941,400 (married/parents, TY 2026). For TY 2025 the thresholds were CHF 793,400 and CHF 940,900. Tax amounts below CHF 25 are not collected.

## 4. Common mistakes and frequently missed deductions

### Takeaway
Most errors come from the interaction between the Lohnausweis and the return. Typical cases:
- claiming commuting costs despite Box F;
- claiming the full meal deduction despite Box G;
- double-counting buy-ins already in Ziffer 10.2;
- forgetting expense allowances that reduce actual-cost claims.

Frequently missed items include:
- the separate commuting and meal deductions for each working spouse;
- childcare costs up to CHF 25,800 per child;
- training costs up to CHF 13,000;
- donations (from CHF 100);
- the new retroactive 3a purchases (from 2026);
- disability costs (no threshold);
- the Elterntarif CHF 263 credit for single parents.

### Cited Findings
- A ticked Box F (company car, free transport, GA for business) signals no commuting costs. Under BKV Art. 5a the 0.9%/month flat rate replaces both the private-use accounting and the commuting deduction. — [Wegleitung 2026 Rz 9, 25](https://www.estv.admin.ch/dam/de/sd-web/afP1GDFr8gE3/dbst-form-lohna-wegleitung-2026-de.pdf); [BKV Art. 5a](https://www.fedlex.admin.ch/eli/cc/1993/1363_1363_1363/de)
- Box G (canteen or Lunch-Checks) allows only the half meal deduction (CHF 7.50/day, max CHF 1,600). It is ticked even if the employee does not actually use the canteen. — [Wegleitung 2026 Rz 10](https://www.estv.admin.ch/dam/de/sd-web/afP1GDFr8gE3/dbst-form-lohna-wegleitung-2026-de.pdf); [BKV Art. 6 Abs. 2](https://www.fedlex.admin.ch/eli/cc/1993/1363_1363_1363/de)
- Private car commuting is deductible only if public transport is unavailable or objectively unreasonable. Otherwise only public-transport costs count. — [BKV Art. 5 Abs. 2](https://www.fedlex.admin.ch/eli/cc/1993/1363_1363_1363/de)
- Employer commuting reimbursements are added to salary, not treated as expenses. The employee may then deduct actual commuting costs. — [Wegleitung 2026 Rz 17, 50](https://www.estv.admin.ch/dam/de/sd-web/afP1GDFr8gE3/dbst-form-lohna-wegleitung-2026-de.pdf)
- BVG buy-ins deducted via payroll already appear in Ziffer 10.2 and are already reflected in net salary (Ziffer 11). Buy-ins paid privately are certified separately on Form 21 EDP and must be claimed in the return. — [Wegleitung 2026 Rz 45–46](https://www.estv.admin.ch/dam/de/sd-web/afP1GDFr8gE3/dbst-form-lohna-wegleitung-2026-de.pdf)
- Employer 3a contributions appear as salary (Ziffer 7), and the deduction must be claimed with the 3a certificate. — [Wegleitung 2026 Rz 38](https://www.estv.admin.ch/dam/de/sd-web/afP1GDFr8gE3/dbst-form-lohna-wegleitung-2026-de.pdf)
- Professional-expense deductions are available to each employed spouse separately (BKV Art. 2). The two-earner deduction applies when both spouses earn income. — [BKV](https://www.fedlex.admin.ch/eli/cc/1993/1363_1363_1363/de); [DBG Art. 33 Abs. 2](https://www.fedlex.admin.ch/eli/cc/1991/1184_1184_1184/de)
- The flat rate for "übrige Berufskosten" must be reduced for part-time or part-year employment. Claiming higher actual costs requires proof of all costs. — [BKV Art. 4, 7](https://www.fedlex.admin.ch/eli/cc/1993/1363_1363_1363/de)
- Sickness costs count only above 5% of net income. Disability-related costs count in full. — [DBG Art. 33 Abs. 1 lit. h, hbis](https://www.fedlex.admin.ch/eli/cc/1991/1184_1184_1184/de)
- Donations count only if the year's total reaches CHF 100, up to 20% of net income. — [DBG Art. 33a](https://www.fedlex.admin.ch/eli/cc/1991/1184_1184_1184/de)
- A buy-in followed by a capital withdrawal within 3 years can lead to the deduction being reversed. — [Zug Steuerbuch](https://zgchtest.webcloud7.ch/izug/platform/behoerden/finanzdirektion/steuerverwaltung/steuerbuch-zug/erlaeuterung-zu-a7-30-allgemeine-abzuege-unabhaengig-von-der-einkommenshoehe/beitraege-an-die-berufliche-vorsorge-saeule-2a-und-2b/export_pdf)
- Property owners can choose actual costs or the flat rate per property each year. This choice disappears for owner-occupied homes from 2029, when the Eigenmietwert is abolished. — [ESTV Steuermäppchen](https://www.estv.admin.ch/dam/estv/de/dokumente/estv/steuersystem/steuermaeppchen/steuermaeppchen-2019-liegenschaftsunterhaltskosten.pdf.download.pdf/steuermaeppchen-2019-liegenschaftsunterhaltskosten.pdf); [law.ch](https://law.ch/lawnews/2025/09/abschaffung-des-eigenmietwertes-fuer-selbstbewohntes-grundeigentum/)

### Inferences
- Common practical tips that follow from the rules above, though no single official "mistakes" list was found:
  - Pay 3a contributions before 31 December (and before any retroactive purchase in the same year).
  - In a year of high actual costs (e.g. major renovation), compare the property flat rate with actual costs.
  - Bundle donations into one year to clear the CHF 100 minimum.
  - Check that canteen availability (Box G) is correct on the Lohnausweis.
- Federal and cantonal amounts differ, as the commuting cap shows. A line-by-line explainer should therefore always show two columns: "Bund" and "Kanton".

### Gaps
- No official ESTV or cantonal list of "häufige Fehler" was found in this pass. The items above come from the legal rules, not from an authority's published error list.
- Cantonal-specific missed deductions were not researched, e.g. ZH/BE insurance caps, cantonal 3a, rent deductions in ZG/VD, or cantonal donation caps.
